# Larkmere Series Bible

Series name: Larkmere (working name, confirmed in use for Book 1). Pen name: Elif Kessler. Publisher: Voxel Publications.
Plan: five standalone slow-burn romances in one lakeside town. Each book has its own couple, its own season, its own craft and its own complete happy ending. A reader can start with any book.

## Series promise (put on every cover and blurb)
Slow burn. Closed door, sensual not explicit. Small-town cozy fantasy with gentle magic. Happy ending guaranteed. Every book a standalone.

## The world in one paragraph
Larkmere is a lakeside town on Larkmere Water. In Larkmere, honest work carries honest feeling: whatever you make tells the truth. The harbor lanterns are lit at dusk. The ferry Heron crosses in the warm months and is laid up in the Net Sheds when the lake freezes. Each book picks one trade and one rule of magic. The magic is small, domestic and never a plot machine. Obstacles are always feeling, weather, magic or a person who loves the couple, never a meeting, vote, debt, count or any procedure.

## Series grid (only Books 1 and 2 are locked)
1. Proof of Home: baker and ferryman. Bread carries the baker's feeling. Spring to last of autumn. Published.
2. Warm Enough: knitter and schoolmaster. Wool holds the knitter's feeling. Winter. Grumpy/sunshine, snowed in. Canon in `novels/warm-enough/HANDOFF.md`.
3. Proposal only: second chance. Someone who left comes home in the thaw. Beekeeper, honey that remembers. Spring to early summer.
4. Proposal only: rivals to lovers or fake courtship. Glass and lamp-makers, summer.
5. Proposal only: finale in autumn at the lantern festival, older leads, ensemble payoff. Could promote a loved side character if Zia ever wants one carry-over.

## Cross-book rules
- Shared: the town, the lake, the Heron, the harbor lanterns, Lantern Street, the Lamplight Inn and the general rule that honest work carries feeling.
- Cameos: default is shared places only. No Book 1 character appears by name or by plot. If Zia wants cameos, allow at most four glimpses by trade only, never plot-relevant, none in the kiss chapter or the last chapter.
- Names: audit every new name against all Voxel books before locking. Already used elsewhere and rejected for this series: Pell, Bram, Hale, Oakes, Corrin, Wren, Voss.
- Format for every book: 2,300 to 2,700 words per chapter, alternating point of view, wanting rises to the last chapter. Chapter count and kiss chapter are set per book. Book 1 (Proof of Home): 32 chapters, first kiss at chapter 27. Book 2 (Warm Enough): 40 chapters, first kiss at chapter 33, release in chapter 40 (revision 2, 2026-10-22, see its HANDOFF). Time told by season, light and weather.
- Each book gets its own canon folder, handoff, tracker, voice guide. Nothing from another book carries in.
- KDP: series name "Larkmere", reading order note "Each book stands alone".
