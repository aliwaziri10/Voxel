## DECISIONS (continue the numbering of the log's DECISIONS section)
- D8 (2026-10-07). The ward's death is told several ways (ch24 platform off House Vane's ledge; ch26 Ember Festival on the Obsidian Terrace; ch27 as first drafted, Gilded Span collapse; ch28 "Gathering Square disaster", two hundred witnesses, burned to ash). Anchor: ch26, the one fully dramatised scene. 8 Frostveil, Ember Festival, Obsidian Terrace, about three thousand witnesses, a ward from the twelve-strong ward line collapses when an uncontrolled discharge shatters her contract marks; Kael is standing nearest. Ch27 and ch28 have been aligned to it. When the pass reaches ch29, 30, 32, 38 and 43, change any other location, crowd size, date or manner of death to match the anchor. The ward's first name stays Mara in the later chapters (ch43's "Mira" becomes Mara). Surname "Corrin" in ch27 stays.
- D9 (2026-10-07). Ch27's first kiss: ch26 is the first kiss of Sol's life, in the colonnade on 8 Frostveil. Any later chapter that places an earlier kiss elsewhere (ch27 had "the archive stacks") is changed to the colonnade.
- D10 (2026-10-07). Month names in Book 1 are Emberfall, Frostveil, Sunspire, Highsummer. "Harvestfall" appeared once in ch27 and was changed to Emberfall (the offer is dated 15 Emberfall, as in ch12).
- D11 (2026-10-07). Ch28 as written invented a "Ward Protections Act" (transferable consent), a character "Jace" (16 mentions, none in any other chapter), a "Gathering Square disaster", a Kael who was "a junior clerk, not the auditor yet", Kael aged twelve at the Accord signing, "twenty-seven days" to the Reckoning, and a Kael who "stood clean" (no marks) when the ward died. None of it connects to ch27 or ch29. Ch28 was rewritten (same two scenes, same beats, 2,649 words). The statute became the Emergency Safety Levy: Corrin's flat 20 percent ward repricing from ch22, ratified by the Council on 11 Frostveil, with a standing deposit and a retroactive assessment from the Festival; Valerius's amendment stretches the arrears clock from fourteen to thirty days (which ends nine days after the Reckoning, 8 Sunspire). Jace became Pell, the old Vane steward. Eighteen days to the Reckoning throughout. Kael is ten at Sol's mother's Reckoning and is the auditor. Sol asks Pell for a packet of the posting letters, loan ledgers and opening audit notes (nothing later depends on it yet; watch ch29 to ch31 for a payoff).
- D12 (2026-10-07). Spelling rule for fixes: American spelling (color, not colour) in Book 1, "grey" not "gray".
