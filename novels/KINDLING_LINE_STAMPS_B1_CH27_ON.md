## DECISIONS (continue the numbering of the log's DECISIONS section)
- D8 (2026-10-07). The ward's death is told several ways (ch24 platform off House Vane's ledge; ch26 Ember Festival on the Obsidian Terrace; ch27 as first drafted, Gilded Span collapse; ch28 "Gathering Square disaster", two hundred witnesses, burned to ash; ch29 a ward named Tessa on a platform). Anchor: ch26, the one fully dramatised scene. 8 Frostveil, Ember Festival, Obsidian Terrace, about three thousand witnesses, a ward from the twelve-strong ward line collapses when an uncontrolled discharge shatters her contract marks; Kael is standing nearest. Ch27, ch28 and ch29 have been aligned to it. When the pass reaches ch30, 32, 38 and 43, change any other location, crowd size, date, name or manner of death to match the anchor. The ward's first name is Mara (ch43's "Mira" becomes Mara). Surname "Corrin" in ch27 stays.
- D9 (2026-10-07). Ch26 is the first kiss of Sol's life, in the colonnade on 8 Frostveil. Any later chapter that places an earlier kiss elsewhere (ch27 had "the archive stacks", ch29 had "the kiss that had not happened") is changed to the colonnade.
- D10 (2026-10-07). Month names in Book 1 are Emberfall, Frostveil, Sunspire, Highsummer. "Harvestfall" appeared once in ch27 and was changed to Emberfall (the offer is dated 15 Emberfall, as in ch12).
- D11 (2026-10-07). Ch28 as written invented a "Ward Protections Act" (transferable consent), a character "Jace" (16 mentions, none in any other chapter), a "Gathering Square disaster", a Kael who was "a junior clerk, not the auditor yet", Kael aged twelve at the Accord signing, "twenty-seven days" to the Reckoning, and a Kael who "stood clean" (no marks) when the ward died. None of it connects to ch27 or ch29. Ch28 was rewritten (same two scenes, same beats, 2,649 words). The statute became the Emergency Safety Levy: Corrin's flat 20 percent ward repricing from ch22, ratified by the Council on 11 Frostveil, with a standing deposit and a retroactive assessment from the Festival; Valerius's amendment stretches the arrears clock from fourteen to thirty days (which ends nine days after the Reckoning, 8 Sunspire). Jace became Pell, the old Vane steward. Eighteen days to the Reckoning throughout. Kael is ten at Sol's mother's Reckoning and is the auditor. Sol asks Pell for a packet of the posting letters, loan ledgers and opening audit notes (nothing later depends on it yet; watch ch30 and ch31 for a payoff).
- D12 (2026-10-07). Spelling rule for fixes: American spelling (color, not colour) in Book 1, "grey" not "gray".
- D13 (2026-10-07). Cost numbers, set in ch29 and binding for the rest of Book 1 (they fit ch01's anchor of about three weeks per second, ch17's 0.58 years for a ten-second flare, ch21's 4.3 years for Kael, and ch27's five to six years from the Festival): on 13 Frostveil Sol has 17.2 years left at the start of the spire session and 13.4 after the first ten flares; a short training flare costs her about 0.4 years (best 0.38); Kael has absorbed 10.2 years in total (4.3 before the Festival, 5.9 on the terrace); his baseline was 42, so about 31 remain. Ch29 as written had Kael at 27.4 years lost with 18 left, Sol counting down from 23 to 8 in an afternoon, "312 flares", "131 years", and "six weeks, forty-two days" beside "sixteen days". When ch30 onward quote Kael's or Sol's remaining years, compare to these and fix the outlier (ch44 says Sol nineteen now, twenty-two before; see log flag 25).
