# Must contracts for Warm Enough. One rule per line: rule text | `regex`. Checked locally, no model.
- No em dash or en dash | `[–—]`
- No digits in prose | `\d`
- Banned words | `\b(some|something|someone|somewhere|somehow|somebody|particular|kind of)\b`
- No stock AI words | `\b(quietly|palpable|unspoken|tapestry|testament)\b`
- No month names | `\b(January|February|March|April|June|July|August|September|October|November|December)\b`
