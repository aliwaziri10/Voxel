## 6. The magic (state once, never vary)
1. Wool holds the honest feeling of the hands that knit it, row by row. Care knit in stays as warmth. Polite knitting holds only tidiness and is cold.
2. A knit warms its wearer only if the wearer lets it in. Someone who refuses care wears it like armor.
3. The warmth is set when knit and does not change after. A garment is warm where it was knit warm and cold where it was knit politely. Warm knits last.
4. A knit with no flaw sits like a shell. Gran's saying: "Leave one door open." One deliberate dropped stitch lets warmth in.
5. Newcomer's toll: anyone arriving in the last of autumn who has not been truly knit for feels the cold of the whole lake in the bones until thaw, indoors and out. Not illness, never danger, no villain, no doctor can help. Fire and coats do not fix it. It lifts at the Breaking regardless; a true knit worn and let in lifts it sooner.
6. Anyone's hands can knit warm, not only a Tansley's.
7. (Revealed chapter 32, never before.) The first Winter Knitter's page: the toll is paid back only when the knitter is knit for. Hester has been cold for nine winters because no one has ever knit for her.
8. Harbor lanterns are lit at dusk. The Heron runs again after the Breaking.
NOT: not a curse placed by a person, not a spell on the schoolmaster alone, not a countdown with numbers, not a prophecy, not a ghost, not a villain.
