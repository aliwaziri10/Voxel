Design follows accepted practice: a human reference of 22 public-domain novels from 1906 to 1927 (Standard Ebooks repositories on GitHub, 753 windows of 2,400 words); Cosine Delta on the 300 most frequent words (Burrows 2002; Evert et al. 2017) against the closest human profile, calibrated leave-one-novel-out at a 5 percent false-alarm rate (the RAID benchmark rule, Dugan et al., ACL 2024); plain measures MATTR-500, sentence-length mean and variation, share of sentences opening he/she/they, each flagged outside the human 2.5 to 97.5 percent range; Wilson 95 percent intervals; chapters 1 to 6 (hand-written) as the control. Runs in about 5 seconds once the novels are cloned.
