# Brief 11 | Ansel | early winter | touch level 2

PLOT: Market on the frozen shore. He carries every basket in town and is loved for it. Hester takes him by the sleeve into the Kettle Room, sits him down and makes him eat soup in front of everyone. It is the first thing he has accepted since the ferry.
PRESENT: Ansel (every coat; laughs before he answers). Hester (grey wool scarf, cold fingers). Mina Cray (flour-white apron, chipped blue teapot, blunt). The town (unnamed). Tilda Brook briefly. NOBODY ELSE.
SETTING: the market on the shore, the Kettle Room.
MAGIC: rule 2: he lets the soup in, because it is a plain gift and not a knit.
OBJECTS: the Grey. The chipped blue teapot.
LOCKED: none.
WITHHOLD: no sister, no family named.
INTERIOR: he realizes that he had not eaten a whole bowl of anything since the ferry.
STAKES: if he refuses she will let him go, and he does not want to be let go.
CALLBACK: ch10: her words on the roof; ch1: the pies he gave away.
ENDS ON: the empty bowl, Mina's raised eyebrow, Hester not looking at him.
