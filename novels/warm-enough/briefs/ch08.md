# Brief 08 | Hester | early winter | touch level 3

PLOT: The first try-on of the half-knit Blue in the workroom. Gran is asleep in the loft. Ansel stands in his shirtsleeves in the Blue. She fixes his collar with her fingers. For one breath the wool is warm as a stone with a morning of sun in it. She sends him out rudely.
PRESENT: Hester (grey wool scarf, cold fingers, tape, draws yarn across her thumb). Ansel (laughs before he answers; the Grey left folded on a chair, not worn). NOBODY ELSE.
SETTING: the workroom, the long table, the stove, the drying racks.
MAGIC: rules 1 and 3: the Blue is warm where she meant it, set when knit. Rule 2: he lets it in for one breath, and then braces.
OBJECTS: the Blue (cabled, half-finished, never completed). The Grey (left on the chair).
LOCKED: none.
WITHHOLD: no Piers name. She must not think that warm things go until chapter 16.
INTERIOR: she realizes her hands have stopped being polite without her; she throws him out because her own heart told on her.
STAKES: the Blue is a jumper she can never finish, and the Grey is cold, and the winter is not stopping.
CALLBACK: ch6: the warm row she ripped out; ch4: his river-stone wrist.
ENDS ON: the door shut behind him and the Blue still warm in her hands.
