# Brief 21 | Ansel | deep winter | touch level 4

PLOT: A cold snap; his stove fails in the night. Hester comes to the cottage with a pot and blankets, sits on the floor with her back to the door and knits until dawn, until he takes the blankets. In the dark they talk: his family (Hester learns he is the eldest of a river family who sends his wages home), her work, nothing about Piers. At dawn she covers his hands with hers. She came to him.
PRESENT: Ansel (every coat, the Grey, Tilda's scarf). Hester (grey wool scarf, cold fingers). NOBODY ELSE. Piers is asleep above the Kettle Room.
SETTING: the master's stone cottage, the dead stove, the door, dark and then dawn.
MAGIC: rule 5: the cold of the lake in his bones is worse in the cold snap; fire and coats do not fix it; her presence does not fix it but changes it.
OBJECTS: the pot, blankets, her knitting (the Green's sleeve). The letter from home.
LOCKED: none.
REVEAL (Hester): he is the eldest, sends wages home, never asks.
WITHHOLD: no tin contents; she does not touch the tin.
BODY: her back against the door, his against the wall, the distance between; at dawn her hands over his, cold and then not.
INTERIOR: he accepts the blankets at last and it costs him.
STAKES: a cold snap that could be dangerous if he were alone.
CALLBACK: ch10: the roof; ch11: the soup.
ENDS ON: her hands covering his and neither moving.
