# Brief 26 | Hester | deep winter | touch level 6

PLOT: He sleeps for the first time all winter, against her shoulder. She knits the Green's last sleeve by the stove's light and admits to herself she has meant every row since the roof. Their hands are joined under the blanket.
PRESENT: Hester (grey wool scarf, cold fingers). Ansel asleep. NOBODY ELSE.
SETTING: the shepherd's hut, the stove light, snow against the shutters.
MAGIC: rule 1: she knits meaning every row; the sleeve is warm. Rule 3: warmth is set.
OBJECTS: the Green (last sleeve). The blanket.
LOCKED: none.
BODY: his weight on her shoulder, his breath on her neck, her free hand knitting, her other hand in his.
INTERIOR: she realizes warm things go and chooses to mean the row anyway.
STAKES: if she finishes the sleeve she must give it.
CALLBACK: ch10: the roof; ch16: the first meant row.
ENDS ON: the needle clicking and his hand tightening in sleep.
