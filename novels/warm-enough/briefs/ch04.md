# Brief 04 | Hester | first frost | touch level 2

PLOT: The wrist reading in the Spindle House workroom, with Gran in the corner on her stool by the copper kettle. A newcomer's wrist tells the Winter Knitter what the wool must do. Hester sits him down and takes his wrist between two fingers through his three coat cuffs, then bare after he pushes up the cuffs for her. His wrist is a river stone: cold, smooth, hard. He says he is fine. She casts on the Grey in front of him: the first rows lie flat as a held breath. As he leaves he tries to carry Gran's coal scuttle and Hester takes it from him without a word.
PRESENT: Hester (grey wool scarf, cold fingers, tape, draws yarn across her thumb). Ansel (every coat, chalk on cuffs, laughs before he answers). Gran (cane, copper kettle, shawl with one small hole). NOBODY ELSE.
SETTING: the workroom, the long table, the stove, the drying racks.
MAGIC: rule 5: he is cold through every coat; she feels it through her fingertips. Rule 1: the first rows of the Grey are polite and hold only tidiness.
OBJECTS: the Grey (the first rows only). The tape.
LOCKED: none.
WITHHOLD: she does not think of Piers by name. No pressed page.
INTERIOR: she is startled that a man this loud has such a quiet wrist.
STAKES: the first frost is on the harbor; a newcomer without a Winter Knit by deep winter suffers all the lake's cold, and she has never been this slow.
CALLBACK: ch1 the warm length of yarn; ch2 her decision to knit by skill alone.
ENDS ON: the first rows of the Grey flat on the table and Gran's smile in the corner, too small to be seen unless you look.
