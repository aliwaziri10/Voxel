# Brief 06 | Hester | first frost | touch level 1

PLOT: At the Kettle Room Mina Cray teases her about the Onion and the lump of grey she has not stopped talking about. Hester snaps. Back at the Spindle House, Gran says, "Leave one door open." Hester says Gran's rule is for knitters who want to be wasted on someone. She starts the Blue, a cabled masterpiece. One row comes out warm. She rips the row out in horror.
PRESENT: Hester (grey wool scarf, cold fingers, tape). Mina Cray (flour-white apron, chipped blue teapot, blunt). Gran (cane, copper kettle, shawl with one small hole). NOBODY ELSE.
SETTING: the Kettle Room, then the workroom.
MAGIC: rule 4: a flawless knit sits like a shell; Gran's saying. Rule 1: one row of the Blue holds honest feeling.
OBJECTS: the Blue (cabled, half-finished, never completed; at this point only begun). The Grey (set aside). Gran's shawl with the one unmended hole.
LOCKED: "Leave one door open." Gran says it here, once.
SEED: the unmended hole in Gran's shawl (seed 6). A second hook by the door with nothing on it.
WITHHOLD: no Piers name, no pattern book, no mention of her husband.
INTERIOR: she fears the warm row and cannot say why.
STAKES: she has two jumpers on her needles for one man and is afraid of both.
CALLBACK: ch4: his wrist; ch2: the warm yarn.
ENDS ON: the ripped yarn curled on the table, still faintly warm.
