# Brief 02 | Hester | last of autumn | touch level 0

PLOT: Night in the Spindle House workroom: drying racks, the stove, Gran's copper kettle on the hob. Gran tells her "He's yours." Hester argues once and loses. Alone, she explains to herself why she makes correct wool and not warm wool: correct wool is tidy, even, true to gauge, and nobody can fault it. Do not give a deeper reason. She decides to knit the best jumper Larkmere has ever seen by skill alone, undyed grey. In her coat pocket is the length of yarn she cut from the ball where it lay against his boot. It is warm. She tests it across her thumb. She wants to burn it and does not.
PRESENT: Hester (grey wool scarf, cold fingers, tape measure round her neck under the scarf, draws yarn across her thumb). Gran (cane, copper kettle, shawl with one small hole). NOBODY ELSE.
SETTING: the workroom, then Hester alone at the long table. The loft above where Gran sleeps.
MAGIC: rules 1 and 3: the cut length stays warm, warmth is set when knit. Hester knows exactly what a warm knit feels like and refuses to name it as warm.
OBJECTS: the cut length of grey yarn. Hester's grey wool scarf. No Grey yet on the needles (she only decides on it).
LOCKED: none.
SEED: a second hook by the door with nothing on it. No name, no explanation. She hangs her coat on the first hook.
WITHHOLD: no Piers name, no pattern book, no first Winter Knitter, no nine winters.
INTERIOR: she is not angry that she was assigned him. She is angry that her hands were warm while she watched him and she did not give them permission.
STAKES: the Winter Knitter must knit every newcomer a Winter Knit; refusing is not an option in Larkmere and she has never refused.
CALLBACK: ch1: the ball of yarn at his boot, the lantern, her mouth thinning.
ENDS ON: the warm length of yarn in her closed fist and the stove open and waiting.
