# Brief 30 | Hester | late winter | touch level 3

PLOT: Tilda brings the tin to the Spindle House. Hester opens it: a scrap of undyed grey wool with one twisted first row. It is her own mistake from the winter she was twelve. She returns the tin to its owner herself and walks to the school and asks him. He tells her: he was the cold boy on the quay for one winter and a girl gave him a scrap. He has never given it away.
PRESENT: Hester (grey wool scarf, cold fingers). Tilda Brook. Ansel. Gran (cane) briefly. NOBODY ELSE.
SETTING: the Spindle House workroom, the walk to the school, the schoolroom.
MAGIC: rule 1 and 3: the scrap, knit by a child with feeling, still faintly warm after all these winters.
OBJECTS: the tin (exact: flat, dented). The scrap of undyed grey wool with a twisted first row.
LOCKED: none. ("I came for the winter I was warm" is NOT said here.)
REVEAL: twist one. Ansel's tin holds her own scrap.
BODY: the scrap warm across her palm; his hands loose and open at his sides.
INTERIOR: she remembers nothing of giving it and everything of how it felt to make it.
STAKES: the thing he never gave away is hers.
CALLBACK: ch5: the tin; ch9: his hand at his chest.
ENDS ON: "I have never given it away."
