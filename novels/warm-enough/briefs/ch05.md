# Brief 05 | Ansel | first frost | touch level 0

PLOT: Dawn. The Grey is folded on his cottage doorstep. It fits perfectly and it is cold as slate. He wears it over three layers and tells everyone it is wonderful. At school Tilda asks his favorite color; he says blue. She is hiding knitting under her desk. At night, alone with the stove in the master's cottage, he writes home that all is well. He puts a flat dented tin under his pillow.
