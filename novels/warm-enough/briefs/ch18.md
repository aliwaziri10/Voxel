# Brief 18 | Hester | midwinter | touch level 2

PLOT: The Kettle Room. Piers is easy and rueful: he is back to finish the map of the lake, and to see her. His old jumper is still warm at the cuff after nine winters; she touches the cuff with one thumb and knows her own work. Ansel puts his head in at the door with a message for Mina, meets Piers, and is kind to him. Hester lets go of the cuff first.
PRESENT: Hester (grey wool scarf, cold fingers). Piers (ink on his fingers, old grey-blue jumper). Mina Cray (flour-white apron, chipped blue teapot, blunt). Ansel briefly (Tilda's scarf, laughs before he answers). NOBODY ELSE.
SETTING: the Kettle Room, steam on the windows.
MAGIC: rule 3: warm knits last. Her warm sweater from nine winters ago has stayed warm, as set.
OBJECTS: Piers's old jumper. The chipped blue teapot.
LOCKED: none.
WITHHOLD: why he left (revealed 23). She does not explain the thaw he left.
BODY: her thumb on the warm cuff; chalk on Ansel's cuff as he leaves; the pull she feels toward the door.
INTERIOR: she compares two men's hands, ink and chalk, and hates the comparison.
STAKES: the warm thing she made once did not leave her; the man did.
CALLBACK: ch2: the second hook; ch16: warm things go.
ENDS ON: her thumb lifting off the cuff first.
