# Brief 07 | Ansel | early winter | touch level 1

PLOT: The lake freezes in a single night. School skating day on Larkmere Water; he insists on joining and falls again and again, to the children's delight. Hester stands at the shore, sent to fetch Tilda. She does not laugh and she does not help. He cannot stop thinking about the not-laughing.
PRESENT: Ansel (wears the Grey over three layers and says it is wonderful; laughs before he answers). Hester (grey wool scarf, closed face). Tilda Brook. The children (unnamed). NOBODY ELSE.
SETTING: the frozen lake, the shore by the school, the lantern poles of the Ice Road in the distance.
MAGIC: rule 5: the whole lake is ice and so is he, to the bone. The Grey holds only tidiness; the ice cannot be kept out.
OBJECTS: the Grey. The tin (his hand goes to it once).
LOCKED: none.
WITHHOLD: no boyhood, no tin contents.
INTERIOR: he learns the difference between being laughed at and being seen; the not-laughing is the second.
STAKES: a fall that hurts would keep him from teaching; he hides a bruised wrist.
CALLBACK: ch5: the Grey; ch4: her fingers on that wrist.
ENDS ON: Hester walking back up the shore with Tilda, not looking round, her scarf the only grey on the white.
