# Brief 16 | Hester | midwinter | touch level 2

PLOT: Midpoint. She starts the Green and lets herself mean one row. It stays warm. She does not rip it out. Gran gives her the old pattern book, with a ribbon-marked page that Hester does not turn to. Hester's first fear on the page: warm things go. A sledge bell rings on the Ice Road: the winter post.
PRESENT: Hester (grey wool scarf, cold fingers, tape, draws yarn across her thumb). Gran (cane, copper kettle, shawl with one small hole). NOBODY ELSE.
SETTING: the workroom, the long table, the pattern book, the stove.
MAGIC: rule 1: the row of the Green stays warm. Rule 3: it will not change.
OBJECTS: the Green (begun). The pattern book. The Blue (set aside).
LOCKED: none.
SEED: the ribbon-marked page in the pattern book (seed 22). Hester does not read it. A second hook by the door with nothing on it, which she looks at.
WITHHOLD: no Piers name until 17; she may think, once, of a man leaving at the thaw but not his name.
INTERIOR: she meant one row and it is warm; she is afraid to mean the next.
STAKES: warm things go; that is her whole rule.
CALLBACK: ch6: Gran's saying; ch8: the Blue warm for one breath.
ENDS ON: the sledge bell on the ice, far off, coming closer.
