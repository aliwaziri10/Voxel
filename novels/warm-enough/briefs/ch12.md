MAGIC: rules 2 and 5: the Grey on his body is cold; her grey scarf at his collarbone is not. She does not say why.
OBJECTS: the Grey. Her grey wool scarf.
LOCKED: none.
WITHHOLD: no Piers name; no kiss.
INTERIOR: she realizes her fingers are warm where she holds him.
STAKES: the squall is real weather and could knock either of them down; a bad fall would end the winter's work.
CALLBACK: ch10: his rooftop; ch8: the warm Blue.
