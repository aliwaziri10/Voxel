# Brief 22 | Hester | deep winter | touch level 4

PLOT: The cold snap bites the whole town; polite winter knits are not enough. The Great Knit-In fills the Kettle Room all night, everyone knitting. Ansel winds skeins for all and refuses a needle: "I am better at holding things." On a crowded bench her hands steady his over a skein. Gran's pattern book falls open to a page pressed under a ribbon; Gran snaps it shut.
PRESENT: Hester (grey wool scarf, cold fingers). Ansel (Tilda's scarf, laughs before he answers). Gran (cane, copper kettle). Mina Cray (flour-white apron, chipped blue teapot). Tilda Brook (lumpy blue scarf). Piers (ink on his fingers) as one of the room. The town (unnamed). NOBODY ELSE.
SETTING: the Kettle Room, crowded benches, steam, the all-night fire.
MAGIC: rules 1 and 6: anyone's hands can knit warm; the town's polite knits are cold and the warm ones carry the room. Rule 7 NOT revealed: she sees only a pressed page, not its words.
OBJECTS: the pattern book. Skeins of wool. The chipped blue teapot.
LOCKED: none.
SEED: the pressed page (seed 22).
BODY: thigh to thigh on the bench; her hands over his; his breath on her temple.
INTERIOR: she realizes her hands will not stay polite around him.
STAKES: the whole town is cold and needs warm knits to last the snap.
CALLBACK: ch9: skein winding; ch16: the pattern book.
ENDS ON: Gran's hand closing the book.
