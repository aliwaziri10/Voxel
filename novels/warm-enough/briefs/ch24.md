# Brief 24 | Hester | deep winter | touch level 3

PLOT: A climb to High Fold for raw fleece, roped together in the snow. Ansel comes to carry the load. Dunstan Rudd's hearth, the sheep, raw lanolin on her fingers: "warm." They start down as the sky closes.
PRESENT: Hester (grey wool scarf, cold fingers). Ansel (every coat, laughs before he answers). Dunstan Rudd (hill farmer, sixties, answers in one or two words). Sheep (unnamed). NOBODY ELSE.
SETTING: the slope to High Fold, Dunstan's hearth, the pasture.
MAGIC: rule 1: raw fleece holds nothing but its own warmth until knit. Rule 5: Ansel is cold in the snow as ever.
OBJECTS: the rope, raw fleece, the pack.
LOCKED: none.
BODY: the rope tugs her into him on the steep; lanolin slick on her fingers; his hand steadying her elbow.
INTERIOR: she thinks of the cream wool she wants and does not yet say why.
STAKES: the weather is turning and the way down is long.
CALLBACK: ch12: the squall; ch21: her hands over his.
ENDS ON: the sky closing like a lid over the pasture.
