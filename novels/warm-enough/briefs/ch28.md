# Brief 28 | Hester | late winter | touch level 5

PLOT: She finishes the Green. Mina says it: "You have been polite for nine winters." At dusk she gives the jumper to him. It warms from the shoulders to the waist. He takes her hand in both of his to thank her, too long. The hem is cool.

MAGIC: the Green (exact): moss green jumper in seed stitch; warm from the shoulders to the waist and at the left cuff, cool at the hem because the hem was knit first, politely. Rule 3: it will not change.
OBJECTS: the Green.
LOCKED: "You have been polite for nine winters." Mina, here only.
BODY: his hands round hers; the warmth climbing his arms; her own fingers warm for the first time.
INTERIOR: she watches him find the exact place where she began to mean it.
STAKES: giving a warm thing means it can go.
