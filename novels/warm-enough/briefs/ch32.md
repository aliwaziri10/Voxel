# Brief 32 | Hester | late winter | touch level 8

PLOT: The workroom, the cream wool. She shows him the dropped stitch on a swatch: "Leave one door open." Faces an inch apart. Gran's kettle screams overhead and Gran calls down. After he leaves, Gran shows her the pressed page: the toll is paid back only when the knitter is knit for. Nobody has ever knit for a Tansley. Hester has been cold for nine winters.
PRESENT: Hester (grey wool scarf, cold fingers, tape). Ansel (the Green). Gran (cane, copper kettle, shawl with one small hole). NOBODY ELSE.
SETTING: the workroom, the loft above, the stove.
MAGIC: rule 4 (the dropped stitch) and rule 7 (revealed here for the first time).
OBJECTS: the cream wool and swatch. The pattern book with the pressed page. The Open Door (not yet begun; the swatch is its sample).
LOCKED: "Leave one door open." echoed once by Hester here.
BODY: faces an inch apart, his breath on her lips, her thumb on the dropped stitch, the kettle's scream cutting through.
INTERIOR: she understands why she has been cold.
STAKES: she has never been knit for.
CALLBACK: ch6: Gran's first saying; ch22: the book snapped shut.
