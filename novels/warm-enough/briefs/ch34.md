# Brief 34 | Hester | late winter | touch level 10a, 10b

PLOT: He walks down Spindle Lane without a single coat and the Kettle Room goes silent. Tilda wins a bet with Mina. They are never alone: every doorway has a neighbor. One kiss stolen among the drying wool in the workroom is cut short by a knock.
PRESENT: Hester (grey wool scarf, cold fingers). Ansel (the Green only, no coats). Mina Cray (flour-white apron, chipped blue teapot, blunt). Tilda Brook (lumpy blue scarf). The neighbors (unnamed). NOBODY ELSE.
SETTING: Spindle Lane, the Kettle Room, the workroom's drying racks.
MAGIC: rule 5: the toll is lifting at the edges; he is warm in the Green without a coat.
OBJECTS: the Green. The drying racks of wool.
LOCKED: none.
BODY: his mouth on hers among hanging wool, his hands at her back, a neighbor's knock.
INTERIOR: she wants him in daylight and cannot have him.
STAKES: the town is delighted and nobody leaves them alone.
CALLBACK: ch33: the kiss; ch29: the Green lent.
ENDS ON: the knock and the wool swinging still.
