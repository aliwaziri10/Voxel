# Brief 01 | Ansel | last of autumn | touch level 0

PLOT: Dusk. The Heron makes her last crossing before the freeze and bumps the town quay with thin ice already whispering in the harbor. Ansel Brandt walks down in every coat he owns, shivering, joking about it. The quay folk press welcome on him: blankets, a shawl, hot meat pies. He thanks each one and gives each away before the quay ends: a blanket onto a porter, the shawl to an old woman, the pies to children. At the far end of the quay, by the Net Sheds, a woman with a closed face sits on a bench knitting grey wool. Her ball of yarn rolls across the planks and stops against his boot. He picks it up and holds the length of yarn out to her. The yarn is faintly warm in his glove. Gran Tansley, on her cane, tells the whole quay that Hester will see to him, the Winter Knitter will see to him. Hester's mouth thins.
PRESENT: Ansel (every coat, chalk on his cuffs, laughs before he answers). Hester (grey wool scarf, closed face, cold fingers; she says almost nothing). Gran (cane, bent hands, speaks to the quay not to him). The quay folk (unnamed, one short line each at most). NOBODY ELSE.
SETTING: the quay at dusk, harbor lanterns being lit, the Heron, the Net Sheds. No school, no cottage yet.
MAGIC: the newcomer's toll starts the moment he steps off: the cold of the whole lake in his bones, indoors and out. He has no name for it and never mentions it aloud; he turns it into jokes. The yarn is faintly warm. Do NOT explain on the page why it is warm.
OBJECTS: Hester's ball of grey yarn only. No tin, no Grey.
LOCKED: none.
WITHHOLD: no thought of a boyhood visit, no girl on a quay, no tin. He does not know her name until Gran says it.
SEED: a folded letter in his inner pocket, addressed in a child's round hand. He touches it once. Unopened, unexplained.
INTERIOR: he resolves to be no trouble to anyone this winter.
STAKES: he has a winter of work ahead and a cold he cannot name; if he cannot stand up in front of a class he loses the post.
CALLBACK: none (first chapter).
ENDS ON: Hester's mouth thinning, seen from across a short stretch of planks, while the first lantern lights behind her.
