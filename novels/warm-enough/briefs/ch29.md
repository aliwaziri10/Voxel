# Brief 29 | Ansel | late winter | touch level 4

PLOT: He lends the Green to Mr. Ostler, who was chilled. Hester is not angry; she is frightened. After hours in the schoolroom she stands too close and tells him to keep one thing. He says there is nothing he can keep. Tilda, sent to the cottage for blankets, finds the tin.
PRESENT: Ansel (every coat, Tilda's scarf). Hester (grey wool scarf, cold fingers). Mr. Ostler (old neighbor, wears a hat indoors, chills easily). Tilda Brook (lumpy blue scarf). NOBODY ELSE.
SETTING: the schoolroom after hours, Mr. Ostler's doorstep, the cottage.
MAGIC: rules 2 and 3: the Green warms Mr. Ostler because he lets it in and because it was knit warm; Ansel is cold again and gives that no weight.
OBJECTS: the Green. The tin (found by Tilda, off the page).
LOCKED: none.
BODY: her shoulder an inch from his chest; his hands on the slate; her breath on his collar.
INTERIOR: he knows he gives things away to avoid having them taken.
STAKES: a warm gift lent out, a keeper's heart.
CALLBACK: ch5: the tin under his pillow; ch28: the Green given.
ENDS ON: his sentence "There is nothing I can keep" and her face.
