# Brief 17 | Ansel | midwinter | touch level 2

PLOT: A sledge bell crosses the Ice Road: the winter post. The post driver (unnamed) hands down a letter in his sister's round hand and then a stranger in a worn grey-blue jumper steps off the sledge: Piers Ellory, ink on his fingers, easy smile. The post driver says his name aloud. Hester, on the quay with Gran and Tilda, goes white. Ansel sees it and says nothing. Piers greets her by name, warmly. Her hands close on her grey wool scarf.
PRESENT: Ansel (every coat, Tilda's scarf, laughs before he answers). Hester (grey wool scarf, cold fingers). Piers Ellory (ink on his fingers, old grey-blue jumper, kind, easy, rueful). Gran (cane). Tilda Brook (lumpy blue scarf). The post driver (unnamed, one line). NOBODY ELSE.
SETTING: the quay and the end of the Ice Road, midwinter light.
MAGIC: rule 3: warm knits last; Ansel cannot know that the jumper is warm and must not be told. Rule 5: he is warm at the neck only.
OBJECTS: Piers's old jumper (exact: worn, grey-blue, still faintly warm at the cuff). The letter from home.
LOCKED: none.
WITHHOLD: no reason Piers left; no history explained. Ansel guesses nothing aloud.
BODY: his jealousy lands low in his chest; his hands go still; he does not step toward her and does not step back.
INTERIOR: he decides to be glad for her, and cannot make the decision hold.
STAKES: the winter post is the only link to the sister, and a rival has just crossed the ice.
CALLBACK: ch15 the letter; ch16 the sledge bell.
ENDS ON: Piers's raised hand and Hester's fingers closing round her scarf.
