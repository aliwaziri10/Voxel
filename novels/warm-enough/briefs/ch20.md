LOCKED: none.
WITHHOLD: his reason for leaving before.
BODY: his hand offered, her hand staying in her pocket; her wrist remembering another wrist.
INTERIOR: she realizes her no cost her nothing, and that is what frightens her.
STAKES: a way out of Larkmere is on offer.
