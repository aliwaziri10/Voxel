# Brief 10 | Hester | early winter | touch level 2

PLOT: Before dawn she finds him on her roof with a lantern, clearing ice from the eaves, shaking, grinning. He has been mending the street at night since the first frost: loose tiles, gate latches, the lantern poles' bases. She stands in the snow below and says it.
PRESENT: Hester (grey wool scarf, cold fingers). Ansel (every coat, chalk on cuffs, laughs before he answers). NOBODY ELSE. The town asleep.
SETTING: the roof of the Spindle House, Spindle Lane, the lantern, the dawn light.
MAGIC: rule 5: he is shaking from the lake's cold and it has nothing to do with the roof.
OBJECTS: the Grey (he wears it, cold). The lantern.
LOCKED: "Stop being fine at me." Hester says it here, once.
WITHHOLD: no reason for the mending beyond that he cannot stop; no family.
INTERIOR: she realizes he does the mending in the dark so that nobody has to thank him.
STAKES: a fall from the roof; the ice.
CALLBACK: ch3: the latch; ch7: the not-laughing.
ENDS ON: he laughs it off and goes down the ladder, and the roof ice under her boot is not the cold she is thinking of.
