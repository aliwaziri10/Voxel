# Brief 19 | Ansel | midwinter | touch level 4

PLOT: The fitting of the Green before the shop window of the Spindle House. Her thumb runs down his forearm to the wrist to find the sleeve length; the left cuff is warm; he gasps; she drops the pins. Across the lane, in the Kettle Room window, Piers watches. Afterward Ansel offers Piers his spare cot in the master's cottage and means it, and hates himself for meaning it. Piers declines kindly: Mina has a room above the Kettle Room.
PRESENT: Ansel (every coat, laughs before he answers). Hester (grey wool scarf, cold fingers, tape). Piers (ink on his fingers). NOBODY ELSE.
SETTING: the Spindle House shop window, Spindle Lane, the Kettle Room window across the lane.
MAGIC: rule 3: the Green is warm at the left cuff because she knit it warm there. Rule 2: he lets it in.
OBJECTS: the Green (exact: moss green jumper in seed stitch; at this point unfinished, left cuff warm).
LOCKED: none.
BODY: her thumb on the inside of his wrist; his breath catches; the pins scatter on the floor.
INTERIOR: he sees that a good man has come to be loved by the woman he is falling for.
STAKES: a rival with an earlier claim and a kinder face.
CALLBACK: ch9: her fingers at the hand-off; ch12: her hands at his waist.
ENDS ON: Piers's smile as he declines, easy and a little sad.
