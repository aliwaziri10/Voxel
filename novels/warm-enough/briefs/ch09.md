# Brief 09 | Ansel | early winter | touch level 3

PLOT: Skein winding in the workroom for an hour while Gran sleeps in her chair in the corner. Ansel stands with his arms out, the wool looped round his wrists. Hester faces him, drawing the thread to her ball, the thread between them. The wool is warm where it crosses his wrists. Their fingers brush at the hand-off. Gran is awake in the dark and smiling.
PRESENT: Ansel (every coat, laughs before he answers). Hester (grey wool scarf, cold fingers, closed face). Gran (cane, copper kettle, shawl with one small hole). NOBODY ELSE.
SETTING: the workroom at night, the stove, the long table.
MAGIC: rule 1: the yarn Hester winds is honest, she cannot stop it. Rule 2: he lets it in without knowing.
OBJECTS: raw undyed wool skeins. The tin (a hand to his chest pocket, once).
LOCKED: none.
SEED: the tin (touched, not opened).
WITHHOLD: no tin contents, no boyhood.
INTERIOR: he finds he has stopped saying he is fine for an hour and does not notice.
STAKES: if he stands still he is cold, so he has been moving all winter.
CALLBACK: ch8: the warm Blue; ch4: the wrist reading.
ENDS ON: Gran's eyes open in the dark, bright, smiling.
