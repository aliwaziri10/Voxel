# Brief 15 | Ansel | midwinter | touch level 1

PLOT: He is warm at the neck only. Tilda explains the rules of Larkmere wool in kid language. At midnight he stands outside the Spindle House and watches Hester knit through the window with her eyes closed and a soft face. He cannot make himself knock. The reader learns that he is the eldest of a river-town family, sends his wages home, and has never asked anyone for anything.
PRESENT: Ansel (Tilda's scarf at the neck, every coat, the Grey; laughs before he answers). Tilda Brook (lumpy blue scarf). Hester seen through the window (grey wool scarf). NOBODY ELSE.
SETTING: the school, Spindle Lane at midnight, the window.
MAGIC: rule 5: he is warm at the neck only. Rule 1: Tilda explains honestly, in her own words.
OBJECTS: Tilda's lumpy blue scarf. The Grey. The letter from home.
LOCKED: none.
REVEAL (reader only): eldest of a river family, sends wages home, never asks.
WITHHOLD: no names for his family; no tin contents.
INTERIOR: he wants to knock and does not.
STAKES: he is sending nearly everything home and this winter he cannot afford to be ill.
CALLBACK: ch1: the folded letter.
ENDS ON: his hand raised to the door and not knocking.
