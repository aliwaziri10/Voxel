# Brief 14 | Hester | Longnight | touch level 4

PLOT: Dawn on the ice. She sits apart, ashamed that a child's bad knitting beat her best. Ansel brings two cups and sits beside her; the sleeping ring closes up round them and they are shoulder to shoulder. He says he is sorry the scarf was not hers. The sun comes up.
PRESENT: Hester (grey wool scarf, cold fingers). Ansel (wearing Tilda's scarf and the Grey; laughs before he answers). Tilda and Mina and Gran asleep in the ring. The town asleep. NOBODY ELSE.
SETTING: the ice at dawn, lanterns guttering, the Ice Road poles.
MAGIC: rule 1: the scarf is warm and she cannot argue with that. Rule 3: warmth does not change after knitting.
OBJECTS: Tilda's lumpy blue scarf. The Grey. Two cups.
LOCKED: none.
WITHHOLD: no Piers name; no pattern book.
INTERIOR: she realizes she would rather he had worn hers and does not want to want it.
STAKES: if she gives him a warm one she will have to mean it.
CALLBACK: ch13: Tilda's scarf round his neck; ch12: the drift.
ENDS ON: the sun clearing the far shore and his shoulder warm against hers.
