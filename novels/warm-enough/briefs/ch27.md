# Brief 27 | Ansel | deep winter | touch level 2

PLOT: The search party arrives at dawn with lanterns and finds them wrapped in one blanket. Gran and Tilda wait at the bottom of the hill. They step apart in front of the whole town. He tries to refuse breakfast and is told "Eat."
PRESENT: Ansel (every coat, Tilda's scarf). Hester (grey wool scarf). Gran (cane). Tilda Brook (lumpy blue scarf). The search party (unnamed). NOBODY ELSE.
SETTING: the hut at dawn, the path down, the foot of the hill.
MAGIC: rule 5: he is warm where she leaned and cold where she did not.
OBJECTS: the blanket. Breakfast.
LOCKED: "Eat." spoken here by Hester once.
BODY: the cold gap where her shoulder was; his hand empty.
INTERIOR: he is mortified and happy and cannot hide either.
STAKES: the whole town now knows and he cannot be fine.
CALLBACK: ch11: the soup; ch25: the blanket.
ENDS ON: the bowl in his hands and Hester's eyes on him until he lifts the spoon.
