# Brief 03 | Ansel | last of autumn | touch level 1

PLOT: His first morning at the school. The one-room schoolhouse stove draws clean; he was up before dawn fixing the stuck latch and the stove flap and tells the children it was always like that. The children name him the Onion because he will not take a single coat off, and he accepts it with delight. Tilda Brook, eleven, bold, speaks first: Hester does not like her, says she knits like she is angry at the wool. Mid-lesson Hester walks in with her tape and measures him on a stool in front of the whole class, over the coats, arms out. She never touches his skin. Her gaze meets his and holds one second too long. After she leaves he sees small mittens on the school hooks and touches one: a rumor of warmth, a child says Hester made those, before.
PRESENT: Ansel (every coat, chalk on cuffs, laughs before he answers). Hester (grey wool scarf, tape, closed face, few words). Tilda Brook (eleven, bold, knits with her tongue between her teeth; no scarf yet). The children (unnamed, one short line at most). NOBODY ELSE.
SETTING: the school, the stove, the hooks by the door, the window facing the Ice Road.
MAGIC: rule 5 toll: the room is warm and he is cold anyway. Rule 3: the mittens are old warm knits, faintly warm, because warm knits last.
OBJECTS: the mittens on the hooks (old work of Hester's). The tape. No Grey yet.
LOCKED: none.
SEED: the mending (seed 3): he fixed the latch and the stove before dawn; a child notices the latch does not stick and he deflects.
WITHHOLD: no tin, no boyhood, no reference to Hester as someone he has seen before.
INTERIOR: he notices Hester's hands are rough and cold and he does not like that she is cold; he tells himself it is professional concern.
STAKES: the class is watching to see if the new master is any good; a failed first day costs him the town's trust.
CALLBACK: ch1: her closed face and her mouth thinning; now it does not thin, it holds.
ENDS ON: his hand in the small warm mitten, the children going quiet.
