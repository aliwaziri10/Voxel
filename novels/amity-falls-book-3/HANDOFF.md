## Word count process rule, confirmed 2026-09-15

Writing to a single resolved beat and padding sentences in afterward produces decoration, not story, even once word count technically clears the floor. The fix is upstream of drafting: plan two or three real sub-beats per chapter before writing, not one beat stretched thin. **2,200-2,500 words is the acceptable landing range.** A chapter isn't done until its word count is actually checked against the real file size in the repo, not assumed from the draft in a session's own context.

## Tone clarification, confirmed 2026-09-14

"Racy" means tempo, tension, chapter-ending hooks, and earned emotional heat between existing characters, not open-door content, not a new romantic thread. Closed-door convention from Books 1-2 unchanged.

## What this project is

Direct sequel to "What the Valley Still Owes" (Book 2, complete at 41 chapters). Adelaide's letter named Ambrose Whitlock as the one who taught her the bargain, not its origin — Book 3's reveal is that the practice is also hereditary and skips generations. Dev, Priya's brother, is discovered to descend from the Whitlock line. The hunger waking in him is untrained, unwanted, and unstable. A hidden faction who track Whitlock bloodlines resurface, wanting the awakening to complete rather than be stopped. Six weeks to the autumn eclipse to save Dev.
