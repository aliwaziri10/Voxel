# WHAT THE BLOOD REMEMBERS — HANDOFF NOTES

**Series:** The Amity Falls Series, Book 3
**Working title:** "What the Blood Remembers" — TENTATIVE, not yet confirmed by Zia.
**Target length:** 100,000+ words, ~42-45 chapters, same structure as Books 1 and 2.
**Status:** Beat map drafted and pushed. Chapter drafting NOT yet started, pending Zia's confirmation of the direction below.

## What this project is

Direct sequel to "What the Valley Still Owes" (Book 2, `novels/amity-falls-book-2/`, complete at 41 chapters). Picks up from Book 2's final-page hook: Adelaide's letter named Ambrose Whitlock as the one who taught her the bargain, not its origin, meaning Denise's fifty-year cycle was only one branch of something older.

**Book 3's engine, as pitched to and provisionally approved by Zia on 2026-09-14 (final confirmation still pending before chapter drafting begins):** the bargain is hereditary as well as taught, and skips generations. Dev, Priya's brother, is discovered to descend from the Whitlock line through his and Priya's late mother's side, a fact none of them knew. The hunger is waking in him, untrained and unwanted. Unlike Denise's decades of controlled, surgical harvesting, an unwilling, untrained carrier is unstable: left alone, it will not take one memory every several years, it will take a great deal from a large part of the valley at once, in a single uncontrolled event, and it is actively endangering Dev's physical health as it grows, not just his memory. A hidden faction, referred to in the beat map as "the quiet family," who have tracked Whitlock bloodlines across generations, resurface, wanting the awakening to complete rather than be stopped, believing total completion would end the practice everywhere. The valley has six weeks, until the night of the autumn eclipse, to find a way to save Dev without repeating Denise's near-catastrophe or letting the quiet family get what they want.

## Why this direction over the alternatives considered

On 2026-09-14, Zia asked for a critical review of the initial pitch (a brand-new stranger character discovering a Whitlock inheritance) before committing. Six options were weighed:
1. Brand-new stranger as carrier (lowest risk, weakest emotional stakes).
2. Wren as carrier (highest emotional stakes, but risk of contradicting her stated parentage in Book 1/2 canon if it exists — NOT verified, so NOT used).
3. Dev as carrier (strong emotional stakes, low retcon risk since his lineage was never pinned down in Books 1-2, and direct payoff of the trust-repair arc built with Priya across Book 2 ch.8-9).
4. A hard countdown clock (the eclipse) instead of Book 2's slower, months-long pace.
5. A hidden antagonist faction (the quiet family) to actively oppose, not just a mystery to research.
6. Real physical danger to the carrier, not just memory loss, raising stakes past Book 2's version.

Chosen combination: 3 + 4 + 5 + 6. Rationale: this version gives readers someone they already love in danger (Dev), a visible ticking clock (the eclipse), active opposition to fight rather than only fog to clear (the quiet family), and real bodily stakes rather than only abstract memory loss. This was Claude's recommendation; Zia has not yet given final sign-off on the specific choice of Dev as carrier before drafting begins.

## Established canon this book must not contradict

- Everything in Book 2's HANDOFF.md "Established canon (final, for reference)" section carries forward unchanged: Denise alive, aged naturally, living alone with valley check-ins; Caleb's memory fully restored; Yusuf as permanent council chair who learned from his earlier hesitation; Wren a Finder apprentice who has learned real caution about full reads since her ch.12/ch.26/ch.36 costs in Book 2.
- Dev's arc across Book 2 (ch.8-9 introduction and trust-repair with Priya, ch.24 onward joining the investigation fully, ch.36-38 alongside Priya in the climax, a hint of something more between Dev and Priya left open but undeveloped) is the direct foundation for his role here. Book 3 must not contradict any of it.
- Priya's professional caution and instincts, established Book 1 and sharpened through Book 2's incident-log discipline, should carry forward in how she investigates the quiet family.
- Ambrose Whitlock himself remains unseen and largely unexplained by design, per Book 2's deliberate choice to leave him a hook rather than resolve him. Book 3 should deepen the mystery of what he actually was, not fully resolve it, per the planned ch.45 hook into a possible Book 4.
- NOT YET DECIDED, flag for Zia before drafting: exact nature of Dev and his mother's concealed Whitlock lineage (how it stayed hidden, whether Priya shares any trace of it herself and simply never triggered it, why now). This needs a firm answer before ch.4-5 can be drafted accurately.

## Standing rules for this book (carried from Book 2 unless noted)

- Target 2,000–2,500 words per chapter, 2,100 acceptable floor. Priority is pace and page-turn tension over hitting a word count exactly, per Zia's standing instruction from Book 2.
- **No em dashes anywhere in chapter prose.** Book 2 caught one late lapse in a high-tension action scene (ch.36) — treat action beats as a specific risk area for this, not just a general rule.
- Match established voice exactly: Mara dry/controlled/engineering-metaphor, Caleb steady/plainspoken/dry humor, Wren blunt/raw/fast-talking, Priya steady/procedural, Dev cautious/self-monitoring but growing more assertive across this book specifically as his agency becomes the point of his arc.
- **"Particular" remains the single most recurrent AI-tell word across both prior books.** Mandatory find-and-replace pass on every chapter before pushing, no exceptions, regardless of how clean a recent streak has been. Same for "the specific ___" and "the kind of ___" constructions.
- No other AI-authorship tells (uniform clause-stacked openings, rule-of-three lists, stock phrases).
- All filenames lowercase (`chapter_NN.md`).
- Zia is a non-technical, voice-dictation user — file paths and full GitHub URLs always given in copy blocks.
- Push each finished chapter directly via the API, verify by reading it back, before moving to the next.
- Repo owner is `aliwaziri10`, not `Wazzaboyzz`.
- Per Zia: once actively drafting, keep writing multiple chapters back to back (drafting, self-correcting, pushing, updating this table and beat_map.md) without pausing to check in after every single chapter. Only stop at a natural milestone or a genuine judgment call.
