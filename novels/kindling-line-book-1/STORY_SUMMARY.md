# KINDLING LINE BOOK 1 - THE STORY IN PLAIN WORDS (one page, for Zia)

Written 2026-10-04 for the full regeneration of all three books. Built ONLY from `architecture.md` (the locked plan) and the canon decisions in the Book 1 and Book 3 HANDOFF files. Nothing here comes from the dumped chapters. Zia: read it, then say what you want changed. Anything marked (my idea) is a proposal, not locked.

THE WORLD. Thornmere Reach is a cliff city of tall family houses. A few people carry the Kindling, a flight-gift. Every use burns years off the person's life and shows as ash-grey streaks in hair and veins. If a bearer uses it near another person, part of the cost jumps to whoever stands nearest. The bearer cannot stop it or aim it. Rich houses buy "wards": people who sign a contract to take that cost on purpose, for pay, and the system is controlled. What happens to Kael is none of those things (not chosen, not paid, not controlled), and that is why it is illegal and why it is the plot. House Corrin controls the ward trade. Each house must prove its claim in a public Reckoning. If it fails, a rival house can petition to take the claim away.

WHO. Sol (Isolde Vane) is the lead. Her house fell after her mother failed her Reckoning. Her mother had no ward, burned forty years in one flight, and died three winters later; Sol was twelve. Sol trains in secret and hides her grey streaks before anyone can see. Kael Ashworth is the auditor who rules on whether Vane keeps its claim. His house profits when other houses fail, and his father, Valerius, was the ruling auditor at her mother's Reckoning.

THE ROMANCE. Romance is the main story. Open-door heat: two or three on-page intimate scenes, one after the first kiss and one after the rupture is repaired. The first kiss comes in the fourth set piece, with the fallout right behind it. The rupture is real, not a misunderstanding: Kael knew and chose not to tell her.

CHAPTERS 1 TO 12. Sol and Kael are forced together. In the first set piece (about ch.5 to 8) Sol has to use the Kindling near him, and the cost jumps to Kael. He is bound by law to report it. In ch.10 he sees the cost is worse than House Vane disclosed, and he does not report it, on purpose.

CHAPTERS 13 TO 25. A night chase across the house ledges (about ch.13 to 15) shows both of them hurt and honest under real danger. Around ch.20 Kael finds proof that House Corrin engineered Sol's mother's failure, and that his own father ruled on it. The same week his house makes a dated offer to rule against Vane. He hides the proof, telling himself he is protecting her.

CHAPTERS 26 TO 32. A public event goes wrong and a ward is hurt or dies because Kael delayed. The first kiss lands here or is cut off by the fallout.

CHAPTERS 33 TO 39. A ledge or rope-bridge collapse, and Sol learns what Kael knew and when. They split. Each pays a real price. After the repair comes the second intimate scene.

CHAPTERS 40 TO 45. The Reckoning. Kael has to stand close enough to be hit, because his job requires it. Sol's control is earned but not complete. Corrin's scheme is exposed and the Reckoning is won. Valerius Ashworth is stripped of his seat (5 Sunspire, Year 3). Sol and Kael end together, Happy For Now. The last chapter shows who really controls the ward contracts: a loose ledger leaf from Corrin's seized books, where every row's total is a year or two above its lines, the difference in a second, darker hand under "carried".

HOW THIS FITS BOOKS 2 AND 3. Valerius is alive all through Book 1 and dies off the page in the year between the books, so Book 2 opens one year later with him eight months in the ground. His failing heart is planted in Book 1, chapter 3.
