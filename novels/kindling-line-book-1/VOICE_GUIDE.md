## POV Voice Distinction
- **Sol (primary POV):** guarded, clipped internal narration, short
  declarative sentences under stress; her interiority runs toward
  self-directed anger rather than self-pity.
- **Kael (secondary POV):** measured, procedural phrasing even in crisis —
  his narration should sound like someone auditing his own feelings the
  same way he audits a Reckoning, cracking only at real breaking points
  (see his Wrong Act beats in `architecture.md`).
Do not let either voice flatten toward the other, or toward Amity Falls'
established narrative voice — per `EDITORIAL_CHARTER.md`'s standing rule
against sanding distinct voices into one "clean" style.

## Standing Rules (inherited, not new)
No em dashes. Avoid "particular" and "some/something ___" hedges. No
recap paragraphs. No long description that stalls a scene already in
motion. No whiny lead behavior. No gratuitous intimate scenes beyond the
two to three placed above.
