# DIGEST - Kindling Line Book 1, ch.1-10

Fact lookup from the live chapter files (main), written 2026-10-03. Not new canon. If a chapter disagrees, the chapter wins; fix this file. Book 1 ch.1-10 contradict each other on many numbers (see SNAGS); this digest records what each chapter says, not which is right.

## KEY CANON IN THIS BATCH
- THE COST RULE as stated in ch.3, 8 and 10: "The nearest living body pays" (ch.3, Sol). Sol: "The Kindling does not recognize choice. It recognizes proximity." Ward-master Marek taught her "the nearest living body is not always the one standing closest" (ch.3, last exchange, unexplained). Ch.10 technical appendix: a contracted ward "absorbs ninety-seven percent of the cost"; "The remaining three percent bleeds to the nearest living body within a five-foot radius. The bleed is unavoidable." Ch.3: wielder pays primary cost, "a fraction always bleeds outward" (5 to 20 percent by intensity; exponential curve; 22 percent at 90 percent capacity). Ch.3 also: transfer radius 2 m at 5 percent, 30 feet at combat flare (both stated by Sol/Kael; ch.7 says documented range is five). Transfer is involuntary in every chapter.
- THE KINDLING: gold-white thread of light from wrist/fingertips (ch.3, 5); "wings" of force ribbons, invisible, felt as pressure, in ch.8; trigger word "Kindling" and a harness drill in a hidden rock cleft behind a tapestry (ch.1). Cost = years of life. Ch.1: threshold hold costs about three weeks per second (10 seconds = about 30 weeks). Ch.3: a flare costs "two years". Ch.7: baseline two years per use since she was sixteen; her 7-4-11 breathing regimen (inhale 7, hold 4, exhale 11, 20 cycles, three times daily) halves it to about one year. Sol's remaining span: "twenty-two" (ch.3, age 24, "if she never used it again").
- THE RECKONING: fixed for 29 Frostveil, Year 3 (ch.1, 3). Required of a house: a contracted, consenting ward ("Accord Article Seven, Section Three", ch.2); the claimant must fly/flare (ch.1 "three circuits of the Gallery Bridge, a climb to the High Aerie"; ch.3 "thirty seconds at combat threshold"; ch.4 "sustained flight over the Ascension Spire, minimum four hours"). Training outside a ward's radius is prohibited ("Article Four, Section One", ch.2). Unbuffered audit "eighteen years minimum for a standard pattern" (ch.1).
- SOL'S MOTHER (ch.1-3, 6-7): flew/flared her Reckoning without a working ward, burned forty years (ch.1, 2, 4) or twelve years (ch.3) in the flight, lived three more winters; the ward contract was "falsified"/"never bound as sworn" (ch.2, 6). Sol was twelve in the gallery (ch.3). Named MARA VANE (ch.7). The transfer landed on the nearest body, the auditor Valerius Ashworth (ch.2, 3). Ch.3: Kael says Valerius absorbed "thirty-two" years, "He did not retire. He was emptied."; Sol says three. Ch.7 gives Mara "three children and a husband who drank the ward fund dry".
- WHO'S WHO: Isolde (Sol) Vane, 24, sole claimant of House Vane, dark hair in a knot, storm-grey eyes ("her mother's"). Kael Ashworth, Auditor, charcoal coat with silver thread at the cuffs (ch.1, 7, 9), dark hair; eyes grey-green (ch.1), storm-water grey (ch.3, 6, 8), hazel with the left lighter (ch.9). Valerius Ashworth is ALIVE in Book 1: former Lord/High Auditor 22 years, now senior advisor to the Accord Council, seventy, "recused" (ch.1); sends Kael notes signed "V." (ch.6, 9). Lord Vane, Sol's father, has gout, storm-cloud hair, is poor (ch.2, 4). House steward of Vane: old "Uncle Pell" (ch.2). House Corrin: crimson serpent (ch.1) / twin-tower seal (ch.9); Lady Merren, Third Tier Contracts Division (ch.4); a Corrin steward with no throat ring (ch.4).
- THE BARGAIN SO FAR: Kael takes Sol's transfers and conceals them. Ch.5: first transfer, in the shear-zone chimney, to Kael's right forearm (luminous line fades into the bone). Ch.6: he redacts the recipient in an incident report ("[REDACTED]") and writes an addendum accepting responsibility; summoned by Valerius for the seventh hour. Ch.7: he says the fourteen-month south-face transfer is filed as "nerve inflammation". Ch.8: a second, larger transfer in a rockfall leaves an X-shaped mark shoulder to hip; he orders Sol to write the report with his name as the one who flared; Sol agrees ("Clear."). Ch.9: private four-column ledger (Date, Event, Proximity, Years Lost, Cumulative) starts at three years. Ch.10: he logs "First deliberate omission."
- WARD-CHAIN (ch.3): 20 silver links, "twenty years of stored capacity", Valerius's, borrowed from the evidence locker; Sol wears it. The Book 3 HANDOFF says the ward-chain stays dropped; do not carry it forward.
- DATES AND COUNTS (ch.1-10): ch.1 (1 Emberfall) 58 days to 29 Frostveil; ch.2 57; ch.3 (4 Emberfall) 55; ch.4 (5 Emberfall) 54; ch.6 (8 Emberfall) 51 ("seven weeks and two days"); ch.9 (13 Emberfall) "forty-six days" to "the winter solstice" and "sixty-seven" days. Sunspire has 30 days in Book 2; Book 1 gives Emberfall at least 29 (1 Emberfall to 29 Frostveil = 58 days).

## CH.1 | 1 Emberfall, Year 3 | POV Sol (as Isolde)
- WHO: Isolde Vane; Kael Ashworth (pale, angular, grey-green eyes); criers on the Gallery Bridge; Corrin agents in crimson sashes.
- WHAT: Reckoning declared ("Thirty-Seventh Reckoning"); ledger: liquid reserves 47,000 crowns, debts 52,000, a 5,000 gap; a ward costs 30,000 plus 15 percent Corrin brokerage (45,000). Secret threshold drill in a cleft. Kael delivers notice of Reckoning and a Corrin lien on the house; he will preside; his father has recused "on health grounds". Kael says her mother once flew the pattern for his father's Reckoning when he was a boy and said "Tell your father the numbers are honest."
- OBJECTS AND COUNTS: 58 days; 5,000 gap; forty years burned; "Vane's broken arrow" banner, "Ashworth's silver hawk", "Corrin's crimson serpent".
- OPEN: five thousand crowns, a path.

## CH.2 | 2 Emberfall | POV Kael
- WHAT: Vane spire archive; Uncle Pell compiles the claim ledger; ward-contract page blank for twelve years; heritage-risk surcharge applied only to Vane (and Darrow, Merek, which folded); Corrin blocks Vane's updraft corridors; Article Seven and Article Four cited. Kael: "I am not the court." Kael feels a hum in his veins near her Kindling.
- LINES: Sol: "Physics is neutral. The law is not. You are not." Kael's father: "An auditor who chases patterns becomes an advocate."

## CH.3 | 4 Emberfall | POV Sol
- WHAT: Courtyard at dawn; Sol shows a controlled flare (two years burned; no transfer because Kael stood at three meters). She explains the transfer rule; her mother's Reckoning (see KEY CANON); Valerius was the auditor who absorbed it. Kael says he was nineteen and in the gallery. He hands her the ward-chain. Last lines: "that the nearest living body is not always the one standing closest."
- OBJECTS AND COUNTS: 50 percent not stated; chain of 20 links; Marek's daughter, 17, a Corrin ward, whose ledger shows twelve years burned in three flares (should have been three).
- CONTINUITY SNAG (real): ch.3 says Sol's mother "burned twelve years" in 27 seconds; ch.1, 2, 4 say forty years; ch.3 says Valerius absorbed "three" years then "thirty-two".

## CH.4 | 5 Emberfall | POV Sol, then Kael
- WHAT: Lady Merren offers a Third Tier ward: fifty thousand crowns a year, three-year minimum, or a deferred plan at eight percent compounding with Vane's holdings as collateral in perpetuity. Vane's revenue "thirty-two thousand". Offer open three days. Sol: market rate is forty-five thousand. Kael watches from the embassy colonnade, is told the offer is designed to be refused, strikes out a recommendation to flag predatory contracting ("Outside auditor scope"). His own Kindling "has never manifested".
- LINES: Corrin steward to Kael: "You are a witness to a slaughter."

## CH.5 | 7 Emberfall | POV Kael
- WHAT: Shear-zone inspection on the north face, the chimney, rockfall; Sol's golden threads; Kael's right forearm marked and absorbed; "I didn't choose you." Sol has trained since fourteen. Kael decides the transfer goes in his private log, not the official report. Sol: "So you'll sit on it. Like you sat on the proof about my mother."
- OBJECTS AND COUNTS: shear block shifted three millimeters since autumn; Statute 47.1 (cost transfer to a non-ward is illegal).

## CH.6 | 8 Emberfall | POV Kael
- WHAT: Incident report for 23:47 on 7 Emberfall at the Lower Terrace boundary marker: four seconds of manifestation, recipient redacted, Sol caught him as the wind shifted; marks on his forearm; he is 28. His father's seal (a hawk). Valerius's note: present at the seventh hour with the original log.
- CONTINUITY SNAG: the incident in ch.5 is a daytime chimney rockfall on 7 Emberfall; ch.6 puts the first transfer at 23:47 the same date on a terrace.

## CH.7 | 10 Emberfall | POV Sol
- WHAT: Pre-dawn ledge behind the Vane western wing; Kael watches her 7-4-11 regimen (about one year per use). He reveals the south-face transfer cost him fourteen months, three days, six hours and he filed it as nerve inflammation. He will observe every session so the council has an Ashworth-signed record; Corrin requested an observer three times.

## CH.8 | 11 Emberfall | POV Sol
- WHAT: Patrol of three Vane guards and two Ashworth guards on the lower ledge; rockfall; Pell (a young Ashworth recruit) pinned and his leg mangled; Sol holds a falling slab with force-wings; she loses "three months, maybe four". Kael takes an X-shaped mark across the chest. He states the Kindling use will be recorded as his own. Sol feels his pain through the stone ("a bond", "resonance").
- CONTINUITY SNAG: "the old mark, the one from the warehouse district three weeks past"; the first transfer elsewhere is the chimney (ch.5) or the terrace (ch.6) the week before. Pell is also the name of the Vane steward (ch.2).

## CH.9 | 13 Emberfall | POV Kael
- WHAT: Valerius's dossier: Vane's debts 47,300 crowns; ward price tiers (12,000, 7,000, 3,000 per year); "Resolve this swiftly. V." Kael starts a private ledger: first row "5 Emberfall, Year 3. Forced proximity, training accident. Direct contact. Three years."; his hair greys, eyes hazel.
- CONTINUITY SNAG: first transfer dated 5 Emberfall (ch.9), 7 Emberfall (ch.5, 6) and "three years" ago vs "fourteen months"/"three months".

## CH.10 | 14 Emberfall | POV Kael
- WHAT: He reads the technical appendix (97 percent absorbed by a ward, 3 percent bleeds to the nearest body within five feet). Class Two breach rules (24-hour reporting; 2,000-crown fine; second breach revokes). He writes an addendum he does not send and a private cost-count entry: "No report filed. First deliberate omission."
- CONTINUITY SNAG: Ashworth is described as having "lost its lineage-house" with a dark scar of a holding three levels below (ch.10) but as the wealthy house above Vane everywhere else; Kael has kept a ledger "three years" (ch.10).

## SNAGS FOR BOOK 3 (do not fix without Zia; Book 1 is finished text)
1. Mother's burn: forty years (ch.1, 2, 4) vs twelve years (ch.3). Book 2 canon: bound ward-taker under a plate, Reckoning thirteen years before Book 2.
2. The auditor who absorbed her flare: Valerius Ashworth (ch.2, 3); Book 2 says Valerius is dead, "eight months in the ground" on 9 Sunspire.
3. Valerius alive and active in Book 1 ch.1, 6, 9; Book 2 has him dead eight months. The two books agree only if Book 1 happens eight or more months before Book 2 (Book 2 starts 9 Sunspire, Year 4).
4. Kael's ages: nineteen "three years" ago (ch.3) vs 28 (ch.6). Sol 24 (ch.1, 3).
5. Kael's eye colour: grey-green (ch.1), storm-water (ch.3, 6, 8), hazel (ch.9).
6. Transfer radius: five feet (ch.10), two meters at 5 percent and thirty feet at combat (ch.3), "documented at five" (ch.7).
7. Ward price: thirty thousand (+15%) in ch.1; fifty thousand in ch.4; twelve thousand a year per tier-one ward in ch.9.
8. Reckoning date: 29 Frostveil in ch.1, 3; "winter solstice" in ch.9; day counts disagree (46 vs 67 on 13 Emberfall).
9. Ashworth seal/colours: silver hawk (ch.1, 6), "crimson with a hammer crossing a quill" (ch.9), grey leather ledger with a hawk and scale (ch.7).
10. Sol's mother's name MARA VANE (ch.7); Book 2 also has an Ashworth warden Mara; the Book 3 HANDOFF already flags this.
11. "Sol" and "Isolde" both used; Kael calls her Isolde in ch.1 and Sol from ch.3.
12. Book 2 never mentions the Kindling; Book 1's seven/four/eleven breathing numbers (7-4-11) echo Book 2's eleven-breath cycle. Do not equate them without Zia.
