# DIGEST - Kindling Line Book 1, ch.31-40

Fact lookup from the live chapter files (main), written 2026-10-03. Not new canon. If a chapter disagrees, the chapter wins; fix this file. Ch.31-40 run 16 to 29 Frostveil and contradict ch.1-30 on the mother's name, the ward deaths, Kael's status and the ending of the Reckoning; this digest records what each chapter says, not which is right.

## KEY CANON IN THIS BATCH
- DAY COUNTS: right in ch.32 (12 days on 17 Frostveil), 34 (9 on 20 Frostveil), 35 (7 on 22 Frostveil), 38 (3 on 26 Frostveil), 39 (1 on 28 Frostveil). Wrong or loose: ch.32 Valerius "You have until the hearing. Six weeks" (twice); ch.33 "the Ember Festival was six weeks ago... forty-two days" (it was 8 Frostveil, eleven days before 19 Frostveil); ch.39 "six weeks" of drills and "six weeks since the rupture"; ch.37 "seven weeks since the first transfer"; ch.35 "under three months since Kael walked into the spire" and "forty-three days" with the ledger (which would put the find on 9 Emberfall; ch.20 has 29 Emberfall).
- THE ENDING OF THE BOOK WORKS LIKE THIS IN CH.40: on 29 Frostveil in the Great Hall of the Spire (black stone dais over the "Vein of the Reach") Sol manifests one gold column before twelve auditors, seven lineage heads and three Corrin representatives; a tendril of the Kindling strikes Kael for three to four years; Lord Corrin claims 12,460 crowns, thirty days to pay; Sol drops three vellum pages (proof from Kael) on the dais; Auditor Mara Vex suspends House Corrin from the ward trade, freezes its contracts, suspends the Vane debt, and relieves Kael of duty. The Reckoning plan of ch.38-39 (a projection array and a twelve-flare test) is never used on the page.
- THE MOTHER, AGAIN: ch.31 names her ELARA VANE (Reckoning Year 1; ward Marian Corrin; contracted absorption 12 years, actual 23; cause "Kindling exhaustion"); ch.36 Kael says "the Reckoning of Mara Vane" (18 Deepwinter, Year 2; ward who did not exist; forged signature; certified by Valerius; 12,000 crowns from Corrin to the ward registry three days before); ch.35 puts her Reckoning twelve years ago; ch.39 "forty years in forty minutes... forty-two... looked eighty"; ch.40 Lord Corrin says she flew "into a storm with a cracked harness strut" and Sol says "a ward who was never truly bound". Ch.31 gives Sol a sister, KIRA VANE (training accident, ward Torin Corrin, contracted 3 years, actual 8; grounded at seventeen, left sleeve pinned empty at the shoulder); ch.36 says "Sol's sister is ready... I have taught her everything". Ch.40 gives her a brother, JORY, plus father, three cousins and an empty chair.
- THE CORRIN LEDGER ROOM (ch.31): a concealed door in the Vane training-shaft stairwell leads to a slate-floored room of red-and-gold Corrin ledgers ("Ward Contracts: Northern Reach, Year 1-5"; later volumes through Year 3). Every contract in the Reach exceeds its term; margins read "Adjusted per Auditor Valerius Ashworth ruling" dozens of times; the Year 3 Vane entry reads "Contracted Ward: None. Status: Default to Corrin Pool. Designated Absorber: Corrin Selection Pending." A Dresen contract margin reads "Kael Ashworth. Auditor observation. Cost transfer anomaly noted. No ward contracted. Recommendation: monitor." Sol copies the pages and sends Kael a note ("east buttress, third bell"); the courier never arrives.
- THE VALERIUS LEDGER ENTRY (ch.35): in Valerius's hand, twelve years old: "House Vane claim denied. Corrin ward-contract ratified. Cost transfer validated per Section 7." Below it in Kael's own hand, dated three weeks after he found it: "Audit review confirmed. No irregularities found. K. Ashworth." Kael has carried the ledger forty-three days. The same proof had "sat in the archives for fifteen years".
- VALERIUS ASHWORTH, ch.31-40: ALIVE and present: Ashworth hall at midwatch (ch.32); high seat of the council (ch.36); his summons and messenger (ch.37); lineage head's box at the Reckoning (ch.40). Ch.40: Sol says he wrote a dissent against her mother's denial and Corrin paid 15,000 crowns to stamp his seal over it.
- KAEL'S STATUS SWINGS: ch.32 told by his father he will not stand as his son; ch.33 at his father's old desk, still auditor; ch.36 (23 Frostveil) he lays down his badge and name before seventeen councilors and three senior auditors, Valerius orders his name struck, he is dismissed as a "private citizen", and he drops the badge from a balcony; ch.37 (25 Frostveil) he is "Lord Auditor" with a seal, submits Corrin ledger extracts to the Central Archive and is addressed as sitting auditor; ch.38 (26 Frostveil) House Ashworth has frozen his accounts and cast him off, his sigil scratched from his satchel; ch.39 he wears the auditor's sigil and is "just Kael"; ch.40 he is "Auditor Ashworth", presents at the Reckoning ("I do not concur. I certify."), and is relieved of duty afterward.
- THE COST RULE, as stated: ch.33 the sixth transfer (three years); previous five: archive stacks, eastern ledge gust, market square enforcer, "festival disaster that killed the ward", a storm-wind message three days earlier; ch.37 a "hunger": the marks make a channel, "the more transfers, the wider the channel", Sol's fire seeps into his marks and dulls the pain, hands touching make a "resonance"; ch.40 Sol builds a "dam" inside her ribs that diverts most of the Kindling inward and a "tendril" leaks across an inch to Kael. Ch.34: Kael claims "I have wards. Contracted. Consenting. Paid." (no ward is shown).
- COSTS AND REMAINING YEARS: ch.36 Kael "perhaps forty left", counted as "thirty-nine, thirty-eight" during the scene; ch.39 chronometer (brass, Corrin-make) reads 0.15 years per flare at 3.2 seconds, recovery four beats, "less than a sixth of baseline", twelve flares for a Reckoning = 1.8 years, "average for a Vane flare is 1.8"; ch.40 Sol "nineteen years" feeding the Kindling minutes and days, seventeen days burned in practice to raise the dam, three to four years of Kael's burned. Ch.33 Sol burns "one year, two, three".
- THE PLAN OF CH.38 (not carried out): a Reckoning crystal on a rotating base geared to the water clock (one revolution a minute); a prism array from three flawed chandelier shards (Kael's mother's) mounted on the base to project the ledger to each house for three rotations (three minutes; charge four to five minutes, twelve percent scatter per pass); Sol alone to melt the rusted grating of the maintenance shaft from the Hall of Contracts undercroft to the plaza culvert; Kael on the bench.
- OFFER DATE (ch.36): the Ashworth offer to rule against Vane is dated 29 Emberfall, "thirty days before the Reckoning" (correct, and matches ch.20's date).
- THE HARVEST HOOK (ch.36): Kael says a deeper record names a controller of the ward registry "older than the Accord... the trade was not a trade at all. It was a harvest." Not followed up in ch.37-40.

## WHO'S WHO (new or changed)
- ELARA VANE / MARA VANE: see above. KIRA VANE: sister (ch.31, 36). JORY: brother, broken finger from the archive raid (ch.40). Sol's father: trembling hands (ch.40). Three cousins who never flew (ch.40).
- MARIAN CORRIN, TORIN CORRIN: the Corrin wards who absorbed the mother's and sister's costs (ch.31).
- AUDITOR MARA VEX (ch.40): presiding auditor of the Reckoning in the Great Hall. Councilor Veyne (female) again in ch.36.
- HOUSES: Dresen, Falke (ch.31 ledgers); Merrow again.
- WARD ON THE VANE LEDGE (ch.38, 43): MIRA, 23, sister in the lower terraces, mother who takes in mending, Corrin factor contract with three years left, case number V-447, died on the Vane western ledge ("transfer took forty years"); stain on the stone. In ch.32 the dead ward is MARA, 23, a cleaner with a sister in the lower reach, dead in a warehouse.
- LORD CORRIN (ch.40): large, crimson silk, smooth face (earlier green-and-gold, black silk and silver; a bone chair in ch.30).
- SIGILS: Vane thorned rose around a falling star (ch.35).
- SOL: wings (ch.33, 35), short hair at the neck (ch.39), grey-green eyes (ch.36), freckles (ch.36, 39). "Isolde" in Kael's mouth in ch.32.

## CH.31 | 16 Frostveil | POV Sol
- WHAT: The Corrin ledger room under the Vane spire (see KEY CANON); she copies the critical pages and sends a note by courier; she waits at the east buttress through the third to sixth bells and Kael does not come.
- OBJECTS AND COUNTS: she has not flown in three days; her father sits at the high table with the steward and the senior wing-captain.
- OPEN: where the note went; the copies under her floorboard.

## CH.32 | 17 Frostveil | POV Kael
- WHAT: Valerius summons Kael to Ashworth hall and demands the Corrin warehouse ledger; Kael refuses; Valerius threatens to deny it and to see him exiled; Kael: "Then I choose chaos."
- OBJECTS AND COUNTS: 12 days to the Reckoning; the dead ward Mara, 23, a cleaner; the warehouse raid "in early winter"; Kael climbed the Ashworth stair forty minutes.
- SNAG: Kael says he told his father two weeks ago the ward's death was an accident; Valerius says "Your mother was murdered by a system I oversaw" (a slip for Sol's mother).

## CH.33 | 19 Frostveil | POV Sol
- WHAT: Sol brings the copied ledger to Kael's office, accuses him; her rage flares and costs him three years (sixth transfer, chest); she says she is going to the council that day.
- OBJECTS AND COUNTS: she says he knew "twenty days"; Kael cites "irregularities in the 31st Reckoning record"; she lies to her cousins about where the money goes.

## CH.34 | 20 Frostveil | POV Kael
- WHAT: Kael's morning timeline; Sol and Kael argue on the ledge outside his chambers; she says she will file the evidence at the Tribune's office herself that day; she steps to the cliff edge; he stays at the table and starts writing.
- OBJECTS AND COUNTS: 9 days; seven working days to the evidence deadline; Kael burned the originals in his childhood-room fireplace.
- TEXT DEFECT: the narrator says "the public event in chapters twenty-six through twenty-eight".

## CH.35 | 22 Frostveil | POV Sol, then Kael
- WHAT: Sol leaves the Vane spire declaring "I will face the Reckoning alone... Mine" and flies north to the Reckoning grounds; Kael stays in the corridor with the ledger and his count (see KEY CANON).
- OBJECTS AND COUNTS: 7 days; the grounds are three days away by chain-lift and foot.
- TEXT DEFECT: each section is one unbroken paragraph.

## CH.36 | 23 Frostveil | POV Kael
- WHAT: In the council chamber Kael reads the proof (see KEY CANON), surrenders badge and name, is struck from the rolls by his father's command, is dismissed as a private citizen; Sol meets him at the door and they talk on the balcony; he drops the badge; she says "Then I will come with you"; they go to an abandoned north-face aerie.
- LINES: Kael: "I find out what happens at the bottom of things." Sol: "Lead the way, private citizen."

## CH.37 | 25 Frostveil | POV Kael
- WHAT: Five or more Corrin killers ("Inquisitor Vane sends his regards... Inquisitor Corrin" in the dialogue) trap Kael in the lower market; Sol drops into the refuse chute, they climb a vent shaft, cross the old smelter flue, she melts a hatch with a white lance of fire, they cross the great chain to the Ashworth winch house; Kael tells the sergeant he will report to his father after confirming the Archive copy.
- OBJECTS AND COUNTS: first transfer two years, night chase three more, then "five, seven" as the hunger count; seven weeks since the first transfer; the Archive opens at the sixth bell.

## CH.38 | 26 Frostveil | POV Sol
- WHAT: The projection-array plan (see KEY CANON); Sol on the stain where the ward Mira died.
- OBJECTS AND COUNTS: 3 days; the Reckoning begins at noon on the twenty-ninth; plan to meet at midnight behind the water-clock tower.

## CH.39 | 28 Frostveil | POV Sol
- WHAT: Kael times her flares with a stopwatch then a brass chronometer (0.15 years per flare); they kiss, retire to a heated cliff room, talk about the transfer; she believes the Reckoning needs twelve flares and that Kael will stand three paces back.
- LINES: Sol: "Prayers are plans you make on your knees."

## CH.40 | 29 Frostveil | POV Sol
- WHAT: The Reckoning in the Great Hall (see KEY CANON); final line: "What more is whether he will ever let me pay him back." The first snow of Frostveil falls on the windows.
- OBJECTS AND COUNTS: deficit 3,000 gold crowns; 700 training hours; Corrin's total 12,460 crowns plus a ward-fee for "the failed Reckoning of Year Zero"; thirty days to pay.

## SNAGS FOR BOOK 3 (do not fix without Zia; Book 1 is finished text)
1. Mother: Mara (ch.7, 36), Elara (ch.31), unnamed elsewhere; her ward: Marian Corrin (ch.31), nonexistent ward (ch.36), false binding (ch.40), recalled ward (ch.13), her sister Mara (ch.18). Burn 12 years (ch.3, 31 margin shows 12 vs 23) vs forty years.
2. Sol's siblings: none (ch.1-30), Kira the grounded one-armed sister (ch.31, 36), Jory the brother (ch.40; ch.17 has an unnamed brother).
3. Kael's status in 23-29 Frostveil (renounced ch.36; sitting auditor ch.37; frozen accounts ch.38; presiding auditor ch.40).
4. The Reckoning: twelve flares (ch.39) vs a single column and a legal trial (ch.40); a projection plan (ch.38) dropped.
5. Number of Kael's transfers: six (ch.33), seven (ch.44 below), tallies in ch.15, 25, 27, 29.
6. Where the Reckoning is held: grounds three days north with a stone circle (ch.35), the Great Hall of the Spire (ch.40), the plaza with an auditor's bench (ch.38).
7. Ward deaths: Mara the cleaner, 23, in a warehouse (ch.32); Mira, 23, on the Vane ledge (ch.38); see DIGEST_ch21-30 for six more versions.
8. Valerius and his offer: ch.36 gives the offer's date correctly; ch.40 has Kael "suppress this evidence for thirty days".
9. Kael's mother: sends terrible Ashworth tea and predicted the cost (ch.39); owner of the chandelier (ch.38). Not mentioned elsewhere.
10. Corrin ward contracts: "forty-seven" in ch.41 (see next digest) vs every contract in ch.31.
11. Sol's age 24 and "nineteen years feeding it" (ch.40); "four years since Kindling woke" (ch.35) vs since fourteen (ch.5), sixteen (ch.7), three/six/twelve years of training (ch.11-19).
12. The hunger mechanic (ch.37) and the dam (ch.40) are new and not used before.
