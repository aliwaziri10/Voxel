# DIGEST - Kindling Line Book 1, ch.11-20

Fact lookup from the live chapter files (main), written 2026-10-03. Not new canon. If a chapter disagrees, the chapter wins; fix this file. Ch.11-20 contradict each other and ch.1-10 on many points (see SNAGS); this digest records what each chapter says, not which is right.

## KEY CANON IN THIS BATCH
- DAY COUNTS ARE CONSISTENT HERE: every "days to the Reckoning" figure in ch.11-20 fits 29 Frostveil with Emberfall = 30 days (16 Emberfall = 43, 17 = 42, 19 = 40, 20 = 39, 22 = 37, 23 = 36, 25 = 34, 26 = 33, 28 = 31). The ch.1-10 counts (58, 57, 55, 54, 51, 46) fit the same rule; only ch.9's "sixty-seven" and "winter solstice" do not. Loose phrases ("six weeks", "five weeks and three days" for 31 days, "five and a half weeks" for 39) are sloppy but the day numbers hold.
- THE FRAUD THREAD: Corrin ward-fee inflation of 30 percent (ch.11, fragment on vellum, covers Accord years 5-14, houses Vane, Mire, Pell); master ledger kept in the Corrin strongroom, unseen by any auditor in sixty years (ch.11). Forged ward-consent contract 7-44 (ch.19). Year 1 ledger with Valerius's signature and three ward slots paid to Corrin (ch.20). Valerius's "dissent" (ch.17). Vellin contract file, coercion by family debt (ch.15).
- VALERIUS ASHWORTH, ch.11-20: alive and powerful. Titles vary: Ruling Auditor of the Reckoning Court and High Seat of House Ashworth (ch.12); Ruling Auditor (ch.16, 17, 18); Chief Auditor (ch.19); Lord Auditor of the Reckoning Accord, office in the Auditorial Spire (ch.20). He has gout-free, grey-eyed, ink-stained fingers (ch.18). Three incompatible versions of his role in Sol's mother's Reckoning: (a) he signed the verdict/denial (ch.12, 16, 17 opening); (b) he wrote a dissent refusing to sign, Corrin's majority stamped the denial over it, "K. Vane, Witness" also signed (ch.17); (c) he defends having signed the substitution of Mara Vane and calls it procedure, "we do not break the hinge. We oil it" (ch.18); ch.20 shows his signature on a Year 1 Vane waiver that was paid for with three Corrin ward slots.
- TWO COMPETING OFFERS TO KAEL: ch.12 (dated 15 Emberfall): rule against Vane and for the Corrin petition, become Chief Auditor, right of first refusal on ward contracts in the Reach, 12,000 crowns a year from the Ashworth treasury; valid to 13 Sunspire; failure means withdrawal and "a review" of his commission. Ch.20 (29 Emberfall): appointment as Ruling Auditor for the Vane Reckoning; on a verdict denying Vane and confirming the Corrin trust, House Ashworth gets a 12 percent tithe of Corrin ward-contract revenue for twenty years plus primary audit authority over Corrin ward assays; expires sundown 13 Frostveil (14 days); written acceptance required. He answers neither (ch.12 "in due course"; ch.20 locks it away).
- KAEL'S COMPROMISES (what he conceals): ch.11 he signed her vault chit, did not report the courtyard burn (to his shoulder), calls it "the lesser corruption" and "auditor's discretion"; ch.14 same reasoning, plus crossing marks on his forearm; ch.16 withholds the ledger from his father's study; ch.17 gives her the dissent; ch.19 files a Provision Twelve emergency request to his father and does not tell Sol; ch.20 files a false log ("No irregularities found in Year 1 Vane proceedings... proceed to Reckoning per schedule") and locks the ledger and offer in an iron chest under his window (key on a chain at his neck).
- THE COST RULE, as stated in this batch (differs from ch.3/10): ch.13 echo of sensation and memory, not only years; ch.14 Kael: "Most goes to the nearest body. A fraction stays with the wielder" (reverse of ch.3/10); ch.15 "the transfer doesn't care about consent. It doesn't care about distance. It only cares about proximity", Kael lost fourteen months "within fifty feet" of a dying ward; ch.17 "nearest soul", transfer "measuring the distance", dormant at twelve-thirteen feet, a ward contract shields only "if the binding itself was true"; ch.17 Sol destroys her flare-stone and takes the cost herself (thirteen beats, about a year plus) to avoid flaring at Kael.
- KINDLING COSTS PER USE (ch.11-20): ch.13 "six days per flight" (4 minutes; her mother never under twelve days; about 140 heartbeats per day); ch.14 "two, maybe three" years for sealing a ledge fissure; ch.17 0.54 to 0.85 years per flare, mean 0.58, a 1.25 anomaly; mother plateaued at 0.83; twelve heartbeats = a full year. Ch.17 numbers: ancestral route needs forty-seven flares (28 years at 0.6, 25 at 0.54); dissent says trigger clause 0.75 years per flare against a 0.80 route minimum. Sol's remaining span: two hundred days (ch.13), 12 to 14 years old count, 8.3 generous and 7.3 honest (ch.17).
- ABILITIES SHOWN: ch.13 a "membrane of force" that catches an updraft, "never wings"; ch.14 a thread that fuses stone ("weaving stone to stone", rock glows cherry-red, dust becomes glass) used to seal a failing ledge; ch.17 flare-stone (thumb-joint raw kindling-crystal) and force blasts that dent an oak-and-lead target board; breathing 4-6-8 in ch.17 (ch.7 is 7-4-11).
- KAEL'S TRANSFER TALLY (ch.15): fourteen months (Vellin death), twenty-three (chase two nights past), thirty-seven (Ledger Room), seventy-four (first, aerie garden) = 148 months, "twelve years and four months" (the sum is right in months; units are unclear in the chapter). Marks: four parallel grey lines on his forearm (ch.15); two crossing marks forming a V from elbow to wrist (ch.14); silver lines on neck and jaw (ch.16).
- THE MAP IMAGE (ch.14): Kael: the marks are "starting to look like a map. And the destination is the Reckoning chamber." Sigils given: Ashworth a crossed key, Vane a fractured spindle.

## WHO'S WHO (new or changed)
- UNCLE BRENN (ch.16): Sol's uncle, called "Lord Brenn" by Kael; grey-green eyes "same as her own"; spiral Kindling mark on his wrist; speaks of "your mother".
- THORNE (ch.16): Corrin envoy, silvered temples, dried-blood velvet coat, black lacquer scroll case, wind-skiff in Corrin blue and gold; delivered three ultimatums in a decade (two houses folded, one burned); four bone-masked wardens.
- MARA VELLIN (ch.15): Corrin-contracted ward, died at about twenty-two; wielder Toren, a Corrin scion; contract signed three years earlier, five years or until the wielder died, two years left; mother in the Lower Reach, sister at the Spire; letters in her pack ("Festival of Embers"). Not Mara Vane.
- MARA VANE (ch.18): per Valerius, "the claimant's sister" who stood in as ward at seventeen when the contracted ward withdrew six hours before a Reckoning; Kael says she "was my age when she died". In ch.7 (see DIGEST_ch01-10) Mara Vane is Sol's mother.
- SOL'S FATHER: drugged by a tincture in unmarked bottles (ch.17); "K. Vane" witness initial on the dissent (ch.17). SOL'S BROTHER: unnamed, gone six nights, runs messages for smugglers (ch.17).
- OTHERS: Toma Vell (ward, Contract 7-44, House Merren), Corrin Archivist Pell, Notary Heskin (ch.19); House Tove, House Merren (ch.12, 19); Houses Mire and Pell (ch.11). Corrin sigil: twin hawks on sails (ch.16), a coin crossed by a quill (ch.19), a hawk striking a serpent is called the Ashworth crest (ch.20).
- EYES: Kael flint (ch.11, 19), grey (ch.14, 17), winter-sky (ch.15); he has freckles across his nose (ch.13). Valerius grey (ch.18). Sol's mother's red coat with copper buttons, lavender collar (ch.16).

## CH.11 | 16 Emberfall | POV Sol, Kael, Sol
- WHO: Sol, Kael.
- WHAT: In the shared records vault under the Ashworth aerie Sol finds a torn vellum ledger fragment in the back of the ward mortality tables, vol. 3 (Accord years 8-12; the fragment covers years 5-14): fees inflated 30 percent for Vane, Mire, Pell, all Corrin-sealed. She came for her mother's flight-log (sealed, "forty years in a single morning"). Kael: a fragment is not a ledger; the master ledger is in the Corrin strongroom, no auditor has seen it in sixty years; he will petition the court for primary-source verification. Kael admits not reporting the courtyard burn to protect her claim ("the lesser corruption"). He presses her to take the fragment; she does, "I'll wait for your petition. But I won't wait forever."
- OBJECTS AND COUNTS: Reckoning in 43 days; vault closes at the seventh bell; Sol has trained in secret "twelve years"; her mother's Reckoning "twelve years ago"; Kael's father "ruling auditor when the Accord was signed".
- TEXT DEFECTS: opens "Three percent. She read the figure again. Thirty percent."; last line is a stray summary sentence.
- OPEN: the petition for the master ledger.

## SNAGS FOR BOOK 3 (do not fix without Zia; Book 1 is finished text)
1. Accord years: the story is in "Year 3 of the Reckoning Accord" (every header) but ch.11 and ch.20 put the first Reckoning and the Vane failure in Year 1 or 5-14, and say Corrin's monopoly is sixty years old. Mother's Reckoning "twelve years ago" cannot be inside a three-year-old Accord.
2. Valerius's role in the mother's Reckoning: signer (ch.12, 16), dissenter (ch.17), defender of procedure (ch.18), signer of a Year 1 waiver (ch.20). Book 2 has him dead.
3. Mother's ward: falsified contract (ch.2, 6, 17) vs real ward recalled three days before (ch.13) vs Mara Vane, the claimant's sister, standing in (ch.18). Mara Vane is mother in ch.7 and a seventeen-year-old sister in ch.18. Mara Vellin (ch.15) is a third woman.
4. Sol's flare costs: six days (ch.13), two to three years (ch.14), 0.54-0.85 years (ch.17); earlier two years (ch.3) and one year with the breathing (ch.7).
5. Cost rule direction: wielder pays most and a fraction bleeds (ch.3, 10) vs most to the nearest body and a fraction stays (ch.14); radius five feet (ch.10), fifty feet (ch.15), twelve-thirteen feet (ch.17).
6. How long Sol has trained: since fourteen (ch.5), "three years" (ch.14), "six years" (ch.15, 19), "twelve years" (ch.11).
7. The first transfer: chimney (ch.5), terrace (ch.6), aerie garden, 74 months (ch.15), courtyard (ch.11), inspection ledge (ch.14), 10 Emberfall west-face fall (ch.12).
8. Offers: ch.12 and ch.20 are different, both live, neither resolved. The Reckoning "moved early" cliffhanger (ch.13) is dropped.
9. Time Kael has known: three weeks (ch.15, 17, 18) vs six months (ch.16) vs "found you tonight for the first time" (ch.17) despite watching her in ch.13.
10. Sol's father: bedridden, drugged (ch.17) and a witness who let the sealing happen; Lord Vane has gout in ch.2, 4. Brother and Uncle Brenn appear for the first time (ch.16, 17).
11. Names recycled: Pell (Vane steward, Ashworth recruit, Corrin archivist), Mara (Vane, Vellin), Corvin vs Corrin.
12. Ashworth and Corrin sigils: see list above; also silver hawk (ch.1, 6), crossed key (ch.14), triple hammer (ch.12).
13. Sol's body count and killing: Kael and Sol kill two enforcers (ch.13) and no consequence is shown.
