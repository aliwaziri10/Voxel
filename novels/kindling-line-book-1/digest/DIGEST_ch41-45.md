# DIGEST - Kindling Line Book 1, ch.41-45

Fact lookup from the live chapter files (main), written 2026-10-03. Not new canon. If a chapter disagrees, the chapter wins; fix this file. Ch.41-43 give three different verdicts on the same claim; ch.44-45 are the epilogue. Ch.45 was rewritten (commit c56f3f4, hand "leans to the right" in 42bba69).

## KEY CANON IN THIS BATCH
- DATES: ch.41 = 1 Sunspire, ch.42 = 2 Sunspire, ch.43 = 4 Sunspire, ch.44 = 5 Sunspire, ch.45 = 7 Sunspire (all Year 3). Ch.41 says Kael presented the ledger "two days ago" (29 Frostveil, correct) but also "four days since" Sol climbed the Ashworth stair and found him with the ledger. Ch.44 says "six days ago" for the Reckoning (29 Frostveil to 5 Sunspire is six days, correct) and "the Reckoning Accord was six days old" (it is the Reckoning that is six days old).
- THE THREE VERDICTS (contradict each other):
  (a) Ch.41, 1 Sunspire, Auditor's Hall (crystal dome), the First Councilor presiding: the motion carries; all ward contracts Corrin administered over thirty years are nullified; the registry goes to a provisional administration of auditors from Houses Merren, Thorne and Vane; Corrin is enjoined from any ward registry or contract for five years; a three-auditor committee will investigate the cost-transfer mechanism; the mother's Reckoning finding is vacated, Vane's lineage rights, holdings and Council seat are restored; Corrin is fined thirty years of registry revenue for the families of dead wards and the houses that fell; Lord Corrin is barred from Council office for life; Kael is censured, his license suspended pending investigation and his Chief Auditor position vacated (House Ashworth to nominate a replacement within thirty days).
  (b) Ch.42, 2 Sunspire, the Spire courtroom, a woman presiding auditor: claim upheld; Vane keeps its lineage-house on the western face; five years of oversight with quarterly reviews by Kael, "reinstated to independent status effective today"; a ward-trade provisional oversight board with a charter drafted by representatives of Vane, Ashworth, Corrin and the Guild of Auditors; Corrin signs (ivy crest seal); the charter: contracts voluntary, witnessed, registered within 48 hours; no contract over three years without renewal; cost-transfer thresholds disclosed; thirty days' notice to terminate; transition support; forfeiture and criminal referral for coercion. Kael's new badge is the old one melted down and recast by the guild.
  (c) Ch.43, 4 Sunspire: Kael himself presides with an ivory striker (nine strikes: three opening, three evidence, three verdict): "House Vane's claim stands. The debt is void. The lands are restored. The lineage seat returns to its rightful holder." Corrin's seats are empty and its banner struck; Valerius Ashworth is named in the ledger as co-conspirator (entries spanning fifteen years); Corrin assets seized and the ward trade's profits fund released wards' pensions for three generations, Valerius's share too; a review board convenes on the sixth and Kael is to testify.
- CH.44 VERSION OF THE ENDING: Kael presided on the final day, Valerius was "stripped of his seat the same afternoon" and "stripped and exiled", the Ashworth banners furled; the ward-trade rules now require named consent, audited compensation, mandatory rest; existing wards may walk at the next solstice; Corrin's monopoly broken.
- WARD CONTRACT NUMBERS (ch.41): Corrin acquired forty-seven ward contracts in thirty years; thirty-nine from houses in distress; twenty-two offered ward service as the only alternative to foreclosure; fourteen told debts would be forgiven if a relative entered the registry; three told afterward that a relative had already signed; six died in service; average term eight years; average lifespan reduction twelve years for the wielders they served; Corrin acquired eleven lineage holdings in thirty years.
- THE MOTHER IN CH.41: "went under the plate believing the binding would hold, and it took nothing from the man beneath it, so the Reckoning found her empty instead"; burned forty years in one flight; took sight, memory, life in one afternoon; Valerius wrote a dissent and was overruled by Corrin's seal; the proof sat in a locked drawer twelve years; Sol was twelve and "left to maintain a crumbling spire alone".
- THE COST RULE AT THE END: ch.41 "the cost sought the nearest vessel"; wards are "the nearest vessels who had agreed to stand there"; ch.43-44 "the Kindling took from whoever stood closest"; ch.42 touch no longer makes heat or cost; ch.43 old marks and new marks "synchronized", a "closed circuit".
- COUNTS: Sol at the Reckoning loses three to four years (ch.44: "Three years. Maybe four"); her remaining: nine at the old rate, twenty-two before the Reckoning, nineteen now, eighteen if the winter is hard (ch.44; she says "eighteen" for the rest of the chapter); the Reckoning burn was "a sixth of what it was". Kael: seven transfers in nine weeks (three years at the Reckoning, two in the chase, one in the forge district from a ward-cuff malfunction, one in the council chamber when a Corrin agent tried to force the transfer onto a twelve-year-old boy, and others), about thirty to thirty-five years left. Ch.45: "She had burned three years in the Reckoning's final moment, and he had absorbed three."
- CH.45 HOOK: a carrier hawk brings a Records Office tube (grey wax, balanced scales, mended once) with a loose ledger leaf and a junior clerk's note, found between the third and fourth volumes of the Corrin registry. Columns: house, year, years burned, charge, total. Every total is a year or two above its lines, the difference in a second, darker hand leaning right, under the heading "Carried". Vane's row: forty years burned plus one carried. Newest row, 29 Frostveil, Vane and Ashworth, one year above what they paid, with "carried" beside it, although Corrin was frozen that noon. Kael: "Corrin was never the master. They were the broker." They decide to take it to the Council's clerk together and launch from the balcony.

## WHO'S WHO (new or changed)
- RUE VANE (ch.41): Sol's mother's sister, grey-streaked hair, in the gallery behind House Merren's banner. Called Aunt Rue in ch.22, 29.
- MASTER HENSHA (ch.41): Sol's trainer (Heston in ch.30).
- LORD CORRIN's HEIR (ch.41): leans forward, glances at the projected ledger; Lord Corrin warns the old contracts will be replaced by morning and walks out.
- FIRST COUNCILOR (ch.41): woman, presides in the Auditor's Hall.
- SOL'S UNCLE (ch.42): present beside her; unnamed (Brenn in ch.16).
- THE FORGE DISTRICT: where ward-cuffs are made (ch.44).
- THE SMITH AND THE GUILDMASTER (ch.42): recast Kael's badge; the guildmaster handed it to him herself.
- EMBER-ROOT SALVE (ch.44): draws heat and slows the spread of a Kindling wound; it does not heal.
- THE SILVER-LEAF CUTTINGS (ch.44): from the northern slopes, planted by Kael three weeks before the verdict in the northern bed of the Vane terrace; early-blooming variant, five years to first fruit.
- CORRIN AGRONOMIST'S REPORT (ch.44): valued the terrace at liquidation; Kael burned three reports; ash-fall from "the 317 eruption".

## SNAGS FOR BOOK 3 (do not fix without Zia; Book 1 is finished text)
1. Three incompatible verdicts and three different oversight structures (ch.41-43); Kael is suspended (41), reinstated as independent (42), presiding auditor (43).
2. Valerius: co-conspirator named (43), stripped and exiled (44); still unseen in ch.45.
3. Marks and transfers: "seven in nine weeks" (ch.44) vs the tallies in ch.15, 25, 27, 29, 33; the first marks "nine weeks past" (ch.43) vs a first transfer in ch.5 or 6.
4. "Plate" (ch.41) appears for the first time in Book 1; the binding that "took nothing from the man beneath it" is the ch.17 and Book 2 wording.
5. Sol's years: eighteen (ch.44) vs seventeen, twelve to fourteen, 8.3, 7.3, 23, 27 (earlier chapters); Book 3 uses 18.
6. Ch.45 carried leaf: "Carried" hand is unseen after 29 Frostveil; matches the Book 2 pulse from under the boiling house only by theme.
7. Rue Vane (ch.41) is the mother's sister but carries the Vane name.
