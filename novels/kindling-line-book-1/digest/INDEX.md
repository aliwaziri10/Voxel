## Facts checked against the chapter text (2026-10-04)
1. Sol's full name is ISOLDE VANE (Book 1 ch.16, 27, 42, 43).
2. The mother "died three winters after her Reckoning, twelve years past": Book 1 ch.1 ("She burned forty years. She died three winters later.") and ch.6. Ch.13, 39 and the ch.45 leaf say she was 42 and looked eighty. The Canon Lock line is right.
3. Sol's count: ch.44 "nineteen now, eighteen if the winter is hard"; the Reckoning burn "three years. Maybe four" (ch.44), three and three in ch.45. Twelve heartbeats make a year is ch.17.
4. Cost rule wording: ch.14 Kael, "Most goes to the nearest body. A fraction stays with the wielder."; ch.17 "nearest soul". Radius numbers vary (five feet, fifty feet, three hundred feet); Book 3 states none.
5. Names: Tessa is a nineteen-year-old ward in Book 1 ch.29 and a tech in Book 2 ch.4. Corwin, Farrow and Aldous also appear in the Amity Falls novels. The new Book 3 names were checked against every novel and story bible in the repo on 2026-10-04 and have no matches.
6. The leaf (ch.45): Vane row forty years burned plus one carried; newest row 29 Frostveil, Vane and Ashworth, plus one; heading "Carried"; the darker hand leans right.
7. Book 1 ch.41 says the mother "went under the plate believing the binding would hold, and it took nothing from the man beneath it". Keep the plate as written.
8. Kael's father: ch.44 stripped and exiled; Book 2 has him buried. Book 3 should say only that he is dead.
9. Ashworth sigil colour and Kael's title vary by chapter. Book 3 should not describe the sigil colour.
