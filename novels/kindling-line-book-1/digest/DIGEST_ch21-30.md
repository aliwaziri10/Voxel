# DIGEST - Kindling Line Book 1, ch.21-30

Fact lookup from the live chapter files (main), written 2026-10-03. Not new canon. If a chapter disagrees, the chapter wins; fix this file. Ch.21-30 contradict each other and ch.1-20 on almost every number and every ward death (see SNAGS); this digest records what each chapter says, not which is right.

## KEY CANON IN THIS BATCH
- DAY COUNTS: ch.21-30 headers run 1 to 14 Frostveil. Counts to 29 Frostveil are right in ch.22 (27), 23 (25), 24 (24), 25 (22), 27 (19), 28 Kael section (18), 29 (16, once), 30 (15). Wrong or stray: ch.28 Sol section "twenty-seven days" (should be 18); ch.29 "six weeks" and "forty-two days"; ch.29 Kael "council in six weeks". Ch.26's "nine days" of Kael sitting on the proof (8 Frostveil back to 29 Emberfall) is right; ch.28 "three weeks", ch.29/30 "three months" and "since Hearthfall" are not.
- THE WARD DEATH (told six different ways): (a) ch.24, 5 Frostveil: ward Mareth of House Corrin, same age as Sol, one-year contract, three paces, public demonstration on the Vane lowest ledge; Sol meant one year and three transferred; Mareth lives but ages three years, her band goes dead. (b) ch.26, 8 Frostveil: unnamed woman with grey in her hair, one of twelve wards at the Ember Festival on the Obsidian Terrace; "uncontrolled transfer", dies, "voided by magnitude". (c) ch.27, 10 Frostveil: Ward Mara Corrin, 23, dies at the "Gilded Span collapse" (Kindling-silk cable bridge; public Vane stabilising test); Kael's marks filed with the Accord physician on 3 Frostveil. (d) ch.28, 11 Frostveil: "the ward named Mara" burned to ash in Gathering Square three days earlier before two hundred witnesses, Kael six paces off. (e) ch.29: Tessa, 19, Corrin ward, collapsed at a public event while Kael stood above her. (f) ch.30: Mirelle, 14, Corrin ward, died in the testing spire; family paid 3,000 crowns. Whatever the truth, each version has Kael nearest or close and carrying the proof.
- VALERIUS ASHWORTH, ch.21-30: ALIVE and active in ch.21 (steward carries his offer), 22 (confronts Kael on the Ashworth western ledge, "former High Auditor and now Lord of House Ashworth"), 25 (sits in the council chamber), 28 (speaks and amends the Ward Protections Act), 30 (visits Kael's office; burned left shoulder, grey eyes, grandfather's carving quoted). DEAD or out of office in ch.23 (Lord Corrin: "He is dead. The dead cannot defend themselves.") and ch.27 (offer sealed by "his father's seal, though Valerius had not held the ruling seat in two years"; Mireau speaks of him in the past). Ch.24 calls him "the former High Auditor". His role: fabricated and approved the pricing cascade ("Adjust weights to accelerate Merrow/Tarlan/Vane default cascade. V.A.", ch.22); signed the bribed ruling and approval memo (ch.21, 23, 27); signed a ward-contract amendment six days before the mother's fall (ch.30); authorised the loan that seeded Corrin's monopoly (ch.28); "did not engineer the failure, did not take the bribe, ruled on the record as it existed" (Kael, ch.25).
- THE PROOF KAEL HOLDS (changes by chapter): ch.21 Valerius-signed ruling stripping Vane of its ward contract, a Corrin ledger entry for the bribe dated three days before, and a memo "The Vane claim cannose succeed. Ensure the auditor understands the required outcome."; ch.22 ledger fragment (pricing algorithm, debt-to-asset ratios) in his desk or coat; ch.23 one extract page ("Disbursement: Ward Contract 447-Vane. Ashworth", dated three days before the mother's hearing), which Kael BURNS on 4 Frostveil; ch.25 fragment, witness statement from a former ward keeper (dead three years), correspondence, in a false-bottom drawer; ch.27 ledger page of the bribe plus approval memorandum, in a strongbox, plus proof Corrin sabotaged the Span; ch.28 ledger page, correspondence, order authorising the loan; ch.29 "the only copy" in a locked office drawer, "a dropped letter" and an overheard clerk-to-factor conversation that Sol saw; ch.30 contract amendment, payment record, in oilcloth in a desk drawer. Kael has not given Sol the proof by the end of ch.30; she finds the Corrin file herself.
- OFFERS TO KAEL IN THIS BATCH: ch.21 (steward): Ashworth seat on the High Council at the next vacancy, father's debts to Corrin settled, sister's dowry; ch.22 (Valerius): rule on procedural grounds, Ashworth ward contracts renew at the old rate, sisters' dowries, brother's Skyward Guard commission; ch.23 (Lord Corrin, to stay silent): seat kept, brother's betrothal to the Corrin daughter proceeds, else Kael is framed for a planted forgery; ch.25: rule against Vane and Ashworth debts to Corrin are restructured, rule for and they are called in thirty days; ch.30: House Vellaryne (protection plus confirmed High Council seat for public exposure of Corrin, three days, expires at the first bell of the Reckoning) and the Ashworth autumn offer (High Auditor's seat). - THE COST RULE, as stated here: ch.21 Kael's table, auditor's share of each transfer rising from 12 to 31 percent (cost to subject 2.3 to 1.1 years, to auditor 0.4 to 1.3 years), "proximity demands payment. - PRICING: ch.22 a flat 20 percent rise on ward contracts, retroactive, hitting Houses Merrow, Tarlan and Vane; Vane ward retainer 4,200 to 5,040 marks a quarter, 800 marks in arrears, 14 days to default clause 4.3; a twenty-year ward costs 100,000 marks; the Merrow girl, sixteen, sold for forty thousand up front. Ch.22 uses marks; earlier chapters use crowns.

## SNAGS FOR BOOK 3 (do not fix without Zia; Book 1 is finished text)
1. Valerius alive (ch.21, 22, 25, 28, 30) vs dead or out of office (ch.23, 27). Book 2 has him dead.
2. Six different ward deaths (see KEY CANON) with different names, dates and places; Mara Corrin (ch.27) and "Mara" (ch.28) add a fourth Mara after Vane, Vellin, Vell.
3. Whose Reckoning: mother's (ch.1-20, 23, 27, 30), father's (ch.22, Aunt Rue). Father burned three years and took eight of Tomas's (ch.22) vs father drunk and absent (ch.17).
4. Who holds the Ashworth proof and what it says (inventory above); Kael burns the one page in ch.23 yet has "proof" in drawers in ch.25-30.
5. Kael's cost totals: 3 (ch.25), 5-6 (ch.27), 4.3 then 27.4 (ch.29); ch.28 says the cost "didn't touch him".
6. Transfer range fifty feet vs three hundred feet (ch.29).
7. Kisses: colonnade (ch.26), archive stacks (ch.27), archive corridor three nights before 14 Frostveil (ch.30), "the kiss that had not happened" (ch.29).
8. Month names: Hearthfall and Harvestfall appear for the first time (ch.27, 30) with events before Emberfall that contradict ch.1-20 timing.
9. Currency marks (ch.22) vs crowns (all else); "emails" and "newsflash" in ch.22, 28 (anachronisms).
10. Jace, Aunt Rue, Master Heston, Lord Corrin's legless chair, the Vellaryne offer: all new with no earlier setup; ch.30's wings vs ch.13's "never wings".
11. Reckoning terms: "The Reckoning is fixed" every chapter; ch.24 public demonstration gives a three-year burn and a "failed" ruling, and ch.27's inquest is held before a ward-cause inquiry, but neither moves the 29 Frostveil date.
