# Book 4 canon numbers — single source of truth
Read this before touching any date, age, or name in any chapter. Every
number below was checked against the live chapter text. Update this
file whenever a number gets newly confirmed or changed.

## Locked
- Present day: autumn 2024.
- Harriet Marsh: born 1949, age 75. Lost decade 1997-2007 (ages 48-58).
  Husband Thomas Marsh died 2003, inside the lost decade.
- Theo Marsh's age: 26, confirmed in ch.1 (credentials) and ch.15 ("I
  was sixteen when I started looking for you, Grandma. Ten years ago.
  I'm twenty-six now"). No birth year is stated anywhere in the
  manuscript — don't derive one.
- Drake's first (Millbrook) attempt: 2005, nineteen years before
  present. Confirmed in ch.5, ch.19, ch.21.
- Millbrook <-> valley drive: two hours. Confirmed in ch.5, 13, 15,
  28, 38, 44.
- Wren Castellano: 21 years old. Confirmed twice in ch.20.
- Warden succession: Josiah Whitlock (active 1847-1863, tried and
  failed to end the bargain) → his son Ambrose Whitlock (Warden
  1872-1901, tried again 1889, hid Adelaide's letter, taught Adelaide
  the renewal) → Sarah Voss/Mara's family (Menders, 1901-1947) →
  Weathers line → Caller line → vacant after prior Warden died 1987 →
  Yusuf (Warden through the ring-ritual era) → role passes to Wren by
  custody of the ledger, not blood (ch.9 — "whoever holds the ledger
  holds the duty"). Josiah and Ambrose are father and son, distinct
  people — do not collapse into one name.
- Martha Whitlock (ch.22): Ambrose Whitlock's daughter, valley clerk
  in 1978, the witness on the falsified renewal Drake altered. Born
  late in Ambrose's tenure (~1895-1900), age ~78-83 as clerk in 1978 —
  no supernatural longevity required. Confirmed book-wide, no
  conflicting source.
- Ambrose Kell (a different, later person — not to be confused with
  either Whitlock): the antagonist who forced Dev to redirect tolls
  and spread the "valley is cursed" lie a generation before Drake. Dev
  carries both the Whitlock and Kell bloodlines (ch.32/40) — two
  separate lineages meeting in one person, not a naming conflict.
- Ring ritual: 2023. Denise's split-the-weight ritual freeing her:
  March 2022, ending 50 years of secret renewals (1972-2022).
- Wren's mother's occupation: retired baker. Ch.7: "her hands strong
  from decades of bakery work before she'd retired." Confirmed
  consistently across ch.5 and ch.7.
- Clara Castellano (great-great-grandmother, signed folios 1870-1895,
  Finder until 1895): confirmed live in ch.3 ("Clara Castellano,
  Finder").
- Drake is arrested: ch.41's early-morning home arrest is canon,
  matched by every other chapter that references it.
- Countdown cluster (ch.16/18/19/20/21/28/29/31/37): one deadline
  (Friday-the-11th) through ch.21, a second later clock ch.31-37. Two
  crises, not a broken timeline.
- Kiss chronology: ch.17 (first) → ch.21 (interrupted) → ch.26
  (deepest).
- Denise's "granddaughter's husband" line (ch.16): reads
  "granddaughter's husband," referring to Caleb (Mara's husband, Mara
  being Denise's granddaughter). Correct as written.
- Elena Castellano is Wren's living mother (retired baker, ch.5/7/40).
  Wren's dead grandmother — the Finder from the 1985 Miller-girl
  search, ch.11 — is Vivian Castellano, a separate person one
  generation further back. Same collision pattern and fix as the
  Clara/Elena great-great-grandmother rename. Confirmed live in ch.11:
  "Vivian Castellano. She found the girl. The girl lived."

## Genuinely open, lower priority
- Archive building's earlier physical descriptions (converted barn,
  cold-storage shed, room above the general store) are the team's
  temporary workroom before the real building — the renovated old
  Main Street library, flooded in '98 and rebuilt on its original
  foundation — was ready. Reconciled by reading; flag only if a new
  chapter edit reintroduces an actual conflict.
- Calendar/season references: the "summer solstice" line is an early,
  later-abandoned publication target; "Opening Spring" is the real,
  final one. Reconciled by reading in story order.
- 1942 renewal gap (ch.10): mentioned in passing, not yet explored.
- Fading-memory thread (ch.11): Wren's own search debt is eroding
  rather than staying fixed, unlike every other documented debt.
  Ch.27-45 don't pay this off explicitly — worth tracking if a later
  revision touches these chapters.
- 1893 ledger vs. oral-tradition Mender-toll numbers (ch.12): the
  chapter explicitly resolves this as "both true in different
  registers" — intentional, not an error.
- Archive building flooded twice (1922, 1957); council sealed the
  1893 ledger in 1998 under then-chair Yusuf — no conflict with his
  later Warden appointment, ~26 years apart, different roles.
- 1984 renewal gap (ch.35, ch.43): a separate numeric gap from the
  1887-1891 folio gap and the 1942 gap above — three distinct "missing
  renewal" threads. Worth a check none are the same event given
  different years by mistake.
- `amity-falls-book-4_full_manuscript.md` (the concatenated file) is
  stale relative to individually-fixed chapters — still shows the
  pre-rename "Elena Castellano" for the ch.3 folio signer, and an
  outdated Theo-age line for ch.15. Do not trust it for naming- or
  number-sensitive checks; use the individual chapter files. Fine for
  fast greps to locate a chapter to check.
