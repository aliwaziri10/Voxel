Wren followed him up the stairs, her heart pounding. The archive basement fell away behind them: the careful shelves, the sleeved pages, the incomplete manuscript. Ahead: the county road, the forty-minute drive, the confrontation she had been dreading and preparing for in equal measure for six months.

*Find the truth,* her gift whispered. *Publish the truth. Let the world decide.*

She didn't know if she believed it would be enough. But she knew it was the only choice left.

The porch swing creaked as Mara stood to watch them go. Odette waved from the store. Caleb looked up from the orchard, pruning shears paused mid-cut.

The valley watched its chroniclers leave.

Wren gripped the strap of her bag: the manuscript, the fragment, the sticky note with Theo's handwriting: *VERIFY: Drake's copy may contain this page.*

She would verify it. Or she would die trying.

But she wouldn't die. She was a Finder. She found things.

And she had finally stopped letting people protect her from the truth.
