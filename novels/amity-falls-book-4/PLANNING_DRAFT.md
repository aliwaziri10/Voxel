## Zia's decision (2026-09-19)

Write Book 4 as the series finale, as a romance: small town, slow burn,
closed door, happy ending. End the series on a high. No more ritual or
paranormal-mystery plotting.

## Genre contract (Book 4 only)

- Genre: small-town romance with a light magical backdrop. Slow burn,
  closed door, happy ending.
- One central couple. Their arc is the book. Every chapter advances it.
- Magic is background only: a gift used for character, never a threat.
- Banned: rituals, hidden factions, armed villains, hereditary curses,
  a new "ancient teacher" thread, cliffhangers. The villain (Drake) is
  resolved by ordinary law, offstage.
- Series payoff: every main Book 1-3 character gets a warm landing.
- Length and pricing: not decided. Check top-20 norms in round 2.

## Story concept (proposed)

Working title: THE ONES WHO STAY (tentative; echoes Book 1's theme).
Season: late autumn first frost to the following spring blossom (adjust
after problem 5).

Lead couple:
- Priya Nair (POV 1). She is the person who holds everything and never asks
  anyone to stay. Wound: she decides for people before they can choose.
  Ethics angle: her gift could make honesty easy, and she refuses to use it
  on someone she loves.
- Isaac Brandt, 35 (POV 2, invented, needs a name-collision check). Records
  analyst at Millbrook County. His father came home from a work trip 19
  years ago missing a week and was never the same. Isaac became an archivist
  to make sure nothing is ever lost quietly again. Wound: he is the one who
  leaves when a place stops needing him. Ungifted outsider.

Engine (low stakes, warm): Isaac arrives with a request to trace the paper
trail on the old Millbrook case. The valley can share only ordinary records,
so Priya, sworn to secrecy, is assigned as his liaison. They fall for each
other through competence, honesty and small kindnesses while she cannot tell
him the whole truth.

Obstacles (internal, not villains): the oath versus honesty; a permanent job
offer that would take him away by New Year that he did not know how to
mention; her habit of not asking anyone to stay.

Resolution: Priya asks the council to change what outsiders may know and
speaks plainly at a town gathering. She then asks Isaac, in her own words
and not with her gift, to stay. He had already turned the offer down and was
waiting to be asked. He becomes the valley's first Archivist for Wren's
project. Epilogue: the Archive opens, both letters are filed, the whole
cast gathers, and Mara and Caleb's toast closes the series on the frost line
that stops at the orchard fence.

## Draft beat map (36 chapters, one line each; sub-beats come after approval)

ACT ONE, ARRIVAL AND WARINESS
1. Priya: an ordinary substation Tuesday; a request arrives from Millbrook.
2. Isaac: the offset map misleads him into the valley; first meeting,
   correct about a filing error, she is guarded.
3. Priya: council (Yusuf) permits ordinary records only; she becomes liaison.
4. Isaac: rents the orchard cottage; Caleb's dry welcome; Mara reads his
   water-stained folders like a structure.
5. Working session; Priya keeps her hands in her pockets (her gift).
6. Wren's Archive; Isaac offers real cataloguing; she tests him.
7. Isaac's father's story; why this case matters.
8. Night in the records room; she says one true thing with no gift.
9. First-frost cider night at the orchard; a dance; he gets the offer letter.

ACT TWO A, SLOW BURN
10. Mara to Priya: staying is a choice, not a proof.
11. Ice storm; stuck overnight in the substation; warm, no danger.
12. Walt Pruitt tells Isaac the local history; Dev needles them.
13. Odette's store: the whole town is watching; terrible sincere advice.
14. Breakthrough: his records match hers; Drake's paper trail closes.
15. Sunday dinner at Mara and Caleb's; she laughs; first touch on ice.
16. Midpoint: near-kiss; her gift flickers and she fears she manipulated him.
17. She retreats into work; Dev tells her the truth.
18. Isaac tells her what it cost his family; she listens without a gift.

ACT TWO B, TRUST
19. Isaac has guessed something is kept quiet: "I don't need to know, only
    not to be lied to."
20. Priya asks Yusuf about the oath; he shares his own regret.
21. Drake is arrested in another county on Isaac's evidence; he must testify;
    first separation.
22. Priya alone; the town closes around her.
23. Isaac in Millbrook; his father's case closes; a long phone call.
24. He returns; first kiss in the snow.
25. Closed-door night off the page; comic morning after; everyone knows.
26. Joy chapter: she lets herself want things.
27. Setup: the job deadline; he has not told her.

ACT THREE, CHOOSING
28. Priya finds the offer letter; the old wound: people leave.
29. Honest fight: he did not want to pressure her; she assumed the decision.
30. He goes to give his answer in person; she could compel him and does not.
31. Priya asks Mara how she stayed.
32. Grange hall gathering (mirrors Book 2 and 3): Priya speaks plainly; the
    council widens the oath; the Archive vote passes.
33. Priya drives out of the valley to the capital, and finds her way.
34. She asks him to stay in her own words; he had already declined.
35. Spring: the Archive opens; Adelaide's and Whitlock's letters filed; Denise,
    Ruth, Odette, Dev and Wren all land warmly.
36. Blossom-time epilogue at the orchard table: proposal, Mara and Caleb's
    toast, the frost line stopping at the fence.
