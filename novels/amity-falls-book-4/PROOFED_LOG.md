   1872-1901) — father and son, distinct people.

   a second, later clock. Two crises, not a broken timeline.
3. Ch.41 (home, 6:45am) is the one canonical arrest; ch.21/25/26/34/
   39/40 all match it.
4. Kiss order: ch.17 (first) → ch.21 (interrupted) → ch.26 (deepest).
5. Grandmother's death age = 14, standardized.
6. Harriet Marsh's home = "assisted living facility" / Willow Creek,
   standardized.

   differentiated on the page (ch.5/11 vs. ch.37/38).
8. Martha Whitlock = Ambrose's daughter, confirmed book-wide (ch.22).
9. Denise's "granddaughter's husband" line (ch.16) is correct as is —
   refers to Caleb.
10. Drake's em dash (ch.25, ×2): the one deliberate exception to the
    book-wide em-dash ban. Never add or "correct" any other em dash.
11. Ch.5's "-A.W." journal signature is a deliberate misdirection
    (Ambrose vs. Adelaide Whitlock, same initials).

    (ch.14 fixed to match).
