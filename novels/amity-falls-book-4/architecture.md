## Working Title
**The Secret She Kept Forever**
(Series: *The Amity Falls Series*, Book 4 — FINALE)

Title locked 2026-09-19, confirmed by Zia. Deliberately breaks from the
"What/Where the ___ ___s" pattern used by Books 1-3 (Where the Frost
Doesn't Reach / What the Valley Still Owes / What the Blood Remembers) —
that formula is oversaturated in this genre and risks blending into other
authors' titles on a crowded KDP search page for readers who don't already
know this series. Series recognition for existing readers is handled by
KDP's Series metadata field ("Book 4 of 4: Amity Falls Series") and
matching cover design, not by title-shape repetition. Cover design for
Book 4 should still echo Books 1-3's typography/color treatment for shelf
cohesion — flagged as a to-do for whoever builds the cover.
