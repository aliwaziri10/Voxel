4. Never state a radius in feet or metres. Use "arm's reach" and "the room".
5. Names: Isolde Vane (I.V.), Kael Ashworth. The works steward is never named. The custodian and the custodian's pick are never named.
6. Counts allowed on the page, exactly: Sol sixteen and a half; Kael about thirty-four; stored sum seventeen; twelve heartbeats make a year. Do NOT add Sol's count to the stored sum. In ch.31 (next batch) Kael pays a year; nothing in this batch pays one.

8. No em dashes. Spell grey. Avoid: particular, some, something, someone, somewhere, somebody, somehow, kind of, the specific. No chapter-number references in prose. No ward-binding, no chosen buffer, no oath that moves years.
9. Romance beat first in every chapter, before any plot.
10. Do not restage a first kiss, first "I love you" or first night. Do not repeat a beat from an earlier chapter; use the fresh beat given.
11. The echo (Sol asks Kael to stand back, he says yes before arguing) appears ONLY in ch.1, 8, 31, 43. It must not appear in this batch. Nobody here says "stand back".
12. Plain "the plate" means the plate in the bare iron door at the foot of the second stair (CANON_LOCK section 5).
13. The works steward's reveal is ch.27 ONLY. Before it, nobody says she is the carrying hand. After it, Sol and Kael know and keep it between themselves; nobody else on the page is told.

## CH.26 | KAEL | Day 24
SETTING. The east gauge hut and the yard of the boiling house, afternoon [V ch.17, ch.24 of this book]. Joren stands at the yard gate.
CAST (only these). Kael (square hand, stick on stairs only), Sol (undyed coat), Odo Marl (fifty, broad, says little, takes every reading twice), Tessa Rook (twenty-three, quick, chalk-line at her belt), Joren (older warden, scar across the back of one hand) at the gate. No one else.
ROMANCE FIRST. In the room over the tar-shop that morning Sol puts a wool sock on his numb left foot because the floor is cold, and he lets her. She says "I know you can" before he can say it. (Fresh beat, domestic.)
PLOT. Odo has laid out a trial of the rig in the yard: two cords from a stand-in plate (a board, a lamp, a sandbag) through a door frame to the far end of the lane. He and Tessa pull on Tessa's count in the trough, twice, and the stand-in lamp is dropped both times in the same breath. Kael says he should take Odo's place at the second cord. Sol says no. Odo says no, in fewer words: a puller does not move until the count is done, and Kael moves. Odo gives the two reasons plainly: the stair, because his left leg cannot brace; and the habit, because Kael went down the second stair alone on 11 Cinderveil [V B2 ch.33] and called Mara in on 4 Cinderveil [V B2 ch.26]. Sol adds that if he is at the rope and the rope fails he would run in, and he would be the nearest. Kael denies it and knows he is lying a little. This is the first open disagreement about who stands where. It ends unresolved: Kael accepts the door of the passage for now, and says aloud where he will be; Sol tells him to say it and stay there. He leaves the yard and says "Joren and Corren" aloud as he goes, because he is going to the lane.
MECHANICS. Closing needs both pegs pulled in one breath by two pullers beyond the room's reach. If a person stands nearer the plate than Sol when the line closes, that person takes the stored seventeen. Odo states this once, plainly, from the rule Kael gave in ch.8. Where the pegs sit is still not decided (ch.38).
LOCKED. Nobody burns. Nobody pulls. Kael does not become a puller. The echo is not used. Do not describe the rig hardware beyond "cords", "a door frame" and "the lane".
INTERIOR. Kael is angry that they are right, and angrier that being right does not stop him wanting to be the one who pulls.
STAKES. The plan lives or dies on the two pullers staying where they are.
SUB-BEATS. Relationship (the sock, then the quarrel); plot (the trial of the rig); interior (the anger at being right).
CALLBACKS. B2 ch.26 ("Mara!"); B2 ch.33 (the second stair alone); ch.24 of this book (the plan); ch.18 (the standing-near quarrel).
DATE. Day 24.
HOOK. At dusk Joren brings word to the room: the custodian's pick is to be named in two days.

## CH.27 | SOL | Day 25 | THE HAND
SETTING. The room over the tar-shop, night [V B2 ch.12, 44]. No other location.
CAST (only these). Sol, Kael, the works steward (small, upright, past sixty, grey hair pinned flat; no clasp at her throat, a pale mark where it sat). No one else. Ansa's pot may be at the door but Ansa does not appear.
ROMANCE FIRST. Waiting. Sol ties bowlines on a spare line, over and over, to keep her hands busy; Kael holds the other end. They say nothing. (Fresh beat; ties to Sol tying the first bowline in B2 ch.29.) Sol has told Kael first, as the rule requires, that the steward would come tonight.
PLOT (ONE SHORT SCENE). The steward knocks and comes in. She says, "You have my clasp, Lady Vane." Sol offers it; she says to keep it, Mara will want to see it. She speaks plainly, in order: (1) forty years ago the old head of the house, Malrik's father (unnamed, dead), ordered her to carry the years, and she wrote every second hand herself, alone, in the dark ink, under the word "carried"; (2) she carried Sol's mother's year among them, and she says she is sorry for the cost and not for the order; (3) she taught Aldous his letters so that someone would know the hand after her; (4) she wrote Aldous's name on the witness line herself, so that a clerk would compare hands and ask who had taught him, because she wanted to be found, but not by the Council and not by Thorne; (5) she locked Renn in the third gallery to keep him alive and away from the pegs, and she did not know the line would draw on him; (6) the name inked out on the last page of the primer is her own, with the day she began. Kael has the primer in his coat and holds it out; she does not take it. Malrik did not know. She asks to be given to no one until the line is closed. Sol says nothing. Kael says nothing. The steward leaves the way she came.
Last words at the door, to Sol: "Do not let anyone stand close when they come out."
MECHANICS. none new. The stored years are not traceable; she says she did not keep a book of whose they were, only of how many.
LOCKED. This is the only scene with the steward as the hand. No investigation, no tutor, no forgery-proof chain beyond the six points above. She is not named. The inked-out name stays inked out. After the scene Sol and Kael agree, in two sentences, to tell no one until the line is closed. The one-word fix to B2 ch.41 ("another man's handwriting" to "another hand's") is the editor's job after this chapter is generated and verified. The writer does not touch Book 2.
INTERIOR. Sol finds she is not angry and does not trust that, and says so aloud to Kael.
STAKES. If the Council hears of her before the close, the account of the forty years is lost and the custodian has a person to use.
SUB-BEATS. Relationship (waiting hands); plot (the hand); interior (not angry, not trusting it).
CALLBACKS. B1 ch.45 (the leaf, "leans to the right"); B2 ch.15 (the clasp); B2 ch.42 (the forged hand, upright, tail closed); ch.13 of this book (Corwin: "She said never let anyone stand close when they come out"); ch.20 and ch.25 (the primer, the clasp and note).
DATE. Day 25.
HOOK. The last words at the door; Sol understands they are about the close.

## CH.28 | KAEL | Day 26
SETTING. The room over the tar-shop, night then morning. No other location.
CAST (only these). Kael (square hand), Sol, Bram (young Council clerk, grey ribbon, very straight spine) at the door in the morning, Joren (scar across the back of one hand) at the stair. No one else.
ROMANCE FIRST. Late, with Sol asleep, Kael writes his fifth page by the lamp. He does not give it to her. In the morning she reads it without being handed it, sits on the bed, and says "Keep it where you can reach it." The page is one line he does not quote in full: when he wants to step in, he will say so aloud first and wait for her answer. (Fresh beat; the page carries into ch.31.)
PLOT. Bram brings the custodian's note at first light: the custodian names the pick by title only, as one ward-taker of the Council's roll, name sealed until the day. The pick will stand at the line when the order runs out. Bram, leaving, says quietly that the pick has asked whether Lady Vane is the nearest body at the plate. Kael thanks him and does not ask how he knows. Kael and Sol agree not to tell Bram about the steward. Kael says "Joren" aloud before he goes down to speak to him.
MECHANICS. none new. The pick intends to be nearer to the plate than Sol when the line closes, to take the stored seventeen for the Council. The text lets the reader work that out; the note does not say it.
LOCKED. Not a political scene: a note and one sentence from Bram. No custodian on the page. The pick is not named and not described.
INTERIOR. Kael is afraid of the page: he knows he wrote a promise he is not sure his body keeps.
STAKES. Someone will try to be nearer than Sol.
SUB-BEATS. Relationship (the page); plot (the pick named); interior (the promise his body might not keep).
CALLBACKS. B2 ch.44 (the fourth page); ch.1 of this book (the fourth page given); ch.27 (the secret kept); ch.18 and ch.23 (Bram).
DATE. Day 26.
HOOK. Bram's one quiet sentence about where the pick wants to stand.

## CH.29 | SOL | Day 27
SETTING. The cliff road at dusk, then the healer's back room [V B2 ch.2 cliff road; B2 ch.27 back room].
CAST (only these). Sol, Kael, Yelva (healer), Mara (Ashworth warden, twenty years' service), Joren (scar across the back of one hand), Tessa Rook. No one else.
ROMANCE FIRST. On the cliff road Sol steps to the drop side before Kael can, and he laughs, the first time he has in front of her since the public step. (Fresh beat; answers B2 ch.2 where he took the drop side.) Quiet; nothing is explained.
PLOT. In the healer's back room Yelva reports Mara's cold has reached the elbows. Tessa has come with the gauge: still seventeen, fixed. Sol shows Mara the copper clasp. Mara says "That one." and asks nothing. Sol says only that the woman will be heard and not yet. Mara says she can wait; she is good at waiting. Mara asks to be told the day the line closes. Back on the road Sol tells Kael aloud that the one at the plate must be her, with the rope rig and Odo and Tessa on the cords; Kael says he knows, and does not argue, and says he will be at the door.
MECHANICS. Mara's draw continues; it rises on nights after someone has gone below. The close ends it. No new rule.
LOCKED. No second telling of the steward's account. Mara is not told the steward is the hand beyond the clasp. The echo is not used.
INTERIOR. Sol finds that deciding it aloud made it lighter, not heavier, and wonders whether that is a warning.
STAKES. Mara's cold is at the elbows; the days are being counted.
SUB-BEATS. Relationship (the drop side); plot (the clasp, the cold); interior (lighter, and the doubt).
CALLBACKS. ch.16 (Mara: "I would know it again"); ch.25 (the clasp); ch.4 (the cold); B2 ch.2 (the cliff road).
DATE. Day 27.
HOOK. Sol, closing the door of the back room, hears Mara say to the ceiling, quietly, the one word "Soon."

## CH.30 | KAEL | Day 28 | POLITICAL SCENE 3 OF 3
SETTING. The mouth of the gallery under the burned ropewalk, morning; then the room over the tar-shop and the lane at night.
CAST (only these). Kael (square hand, stick on stairs only), Sol, Bram (reads the demand aloud), the Council custodian (an official in Council grey; a role only, no name, no description beyond grey and a scroll case), the custodian's pick (one ward-taker in Council grey, silent, no name, no face described), Joren and Corren, Ansa at the top of the steps, Odo Marl, Tessa Rook. The households of the Lower Reach stand back, unnamed.
ROMANCE FIRST. The night before, in the room: they say their counts aloud before sleeping, sixteen and a half and about thirty-four, the way another couple might say good night. (Fresh beat; the counting habit.) It is brief and warm.
PLOT (UNDER HALF THE CHAPTER, NO DEBATE). The custodian has Bram read the formal demand: both pegs surrendered to the Council; the pick to stand at the line on the day the order runs out; the reserves to pass at its end. Kael answers in exactly two sentences: "The warrants of this audit bear two names or they bear none. The line will be closed by the hands that found it, and the years will go where the rule sends them, not where the order does." The custodian says the order runs out at the end of the thirtieth day and the pick will attend. The custodian and the pick leave. No threat is spoken. Kael says "Joren and Corren" aloud before he goes to the yard.
AFTER. Odo and Tessa check each cord twice. Odo: "I take everything twice." Kael tells Sol and Joren aloud, before she asks, that he will be at the door and will say it first if he wants to step in. They go to the plate tomorrow, before the order runs out.
MECHANICS. none new.
LOCKED. This is the third and last political scene. No debate, no speeches beyond the two sentences. No custodian scene after this. The pick is not named.
INTERIOR. Kael hears his own answer and is proud of it and thinks of the fifth page and is not sure of himself.
STAKES. The order runs out at the end of the thirtieth day; the pick will be at the line.
SUB-BEATS. Plot (the demand); relationship (the counting); interior (pride and doubt).
CALLBACKS. ch.18 (the order); ch.28 (the pick named); B2 ch.45 ("two names or none").
DATE. Day 28.
HOOK. Kael's last line, aloud to the lane: "Tomorrow, then." Sol, quietly: "Say it first."
