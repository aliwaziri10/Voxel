# THREAD LEDGER - Kindling Line Book 3

Written 2026-10-04 from the live Book 2 digests (ch.1-45) and the Book 3 beat maps. Purpose: every Book 2 thread gets one decision so none is dropped by accident. Each row: THREAD | where it eases off | how it lands on the couple. Decisions are made under Zia's full delegation and can be reversed. No new characters are added; every name below is already in Book 2 or the Canon Lock cast.
Rule: a thread may stay alive only while it presses on Sol and Kael. When it presses only on the Council, it closes in one line, off the page.

## A. THREADS THAT CARRY THE BOOK (stay on the page, pressing on the couple)
1. THE HAND (the forger, the carried hand, the hooded watcher, the 8 Sunspire bearer, the pen ruled forty years ago, Aldous's tutor). One person: the works steward. Clues: ch.2 (she taught Aldous), ch.13 (copper payment), ch.20 (primer), ch.25 (clasp and note), ch.27 (she speaks). Lands: Sol wants her heard before anyone decides what she is; Kael does not manage it.
2. THE SECOND LINE and the extra breath. ch.1, 5, 6, 8, 9, 31, 43. It is the clock on the couple.
3. MARA. ch.4, 10, 16 wakes, 17, 29, 39, 43 the cold ebbs. Presses on Kael's guilt about 4 Cinderveil.
4. RENN. ch.3 his sheet, ch.14 found, ch.15 his account, ch.19 he and Ulla mean to leave, ch.38 his map. Ulla and Renn are the mirror of the couple (she waits, he comes back).
5. THE COUNCIL'S CLAIM ON THE STORED RESERVES (the custodian). ch.12 the claim, ch.18 the order, ch.28 the pick, ch.30 the demand, ch.31 the rush, ch.42 the pick held at the door. Lands: whoever stands nearest the line takes the stored years, so it presses on who stands near Sol.
6. THE STORED SEVENTEEN and Sol's mother's carried year. ch.7, 9, 21, 22, 43. Lands in Sol's decision to count her mother's year as hers.
7. KAEL'S HABIT of standing near and paying. ch.1, 8, 31, 43. The one act at the heart of the book.

## B. THREADS THAT CLOSE QUICKLY (one beat, then done)
8. THE CHANCELLOR'S QUESTION (what will you do if the hand belongs to a house you trusted). ch.2 coda: Kael answers by note in two sentences; Sol set the order of the questions. Closed.
9. FARROW'S POSTSCRIPT ("Ask the boy who taught him his letters"). ch.2: Kael asks Aldous with Malrik present; Aldous names the works steward. Closed.
10. THORNE'S CONCESSION and grievance. ch.12 only (Malrik at the tar-shop door). The grievance (three generations served, "It was borne") comes back once, in the steward's mouth in ch.27.
11. THE DEED. Void in Book 2. Not re-explained. One passing line in ch.2 at most.
12. THE AWNING, THE HOLD AND THE STANDARD (about 252 signers). NEW DECISION: with the Deed void, the standard lapses. In ch.3 Ansa says in one line that the awning came down the morning after the vote and the clerks paid wages to the day. Tam is not a plot point after that. Closed off the page.
13. TAM. Appears ch.3, 8, 23, 24 as Ansa's nephew. His withdrawal and the Hold are not mentioned again.
14. CORWIN (the smith). ch.13 only (the forge scene, "copper", "never let anyone stand close"). Closed there. The commission's gentle questioning is never shown.
15. THE COUNCIL SURVEYOR'S SEALED SHUTTING ORDER ("one person holds the page"). NEW DECISION: Renn is the Council surveyor and the page was lodged with him. He gives it to Sol unopened in ch.15, and it is the page whose back carries his map of the room, used in ch.38. So "who holds the page" is answered: Renn gives it to Sol, not to the Council and not to Thorne. Lands: Sol, not Kael, holds the plan.
16. THE DOTTED LINE EAST from the Lower Spine anchor. ch.21: it ends on the warm mark. Closed.
17. THE TAP AT ANCHOR SEVEN. Closed on eleven in Book 2. One line in ch.1 (Kael: it is shut, and the east pulse is still there). Nothing more.
18. THE BLUE WARD-LAMP in the ropewalk gallery (Ansa's dark lamp). NEW DECISION: the works steward lit it every evening so the meter-reader could see, and stopped after the hearing. She says so in one line in ch.27.
19. SOL'S MOTHER'S CONTRACT countersigned "Received for the works of the Lower Spine, House Thorne" in a steward's hand (B2 ch.17). NEW DECISION, important for readers: ch.27 says the steward countersigned it, in one sentence, and Sol does not answer. It is the strongest hit the steward has on Sol and it costs nothing to plant.
20. HOUSE ASHWORTH (Ferra, Auda, the elders). They do not appear in Book 3 until the end. ch.44 or 45: one short letter from Auda addressed to both names. This is the family's acceptance of Sol and the romance payoff. (Zia has still to decide how Auda was introduced; see READER_FIX_LIST.)

## C. BACKGROUND PEOPLE (no scenes; one decision each so they are not silently dropped)
21. VAREL (Tobin Varel, clerk, found alive in the gallery, B2 ch.13-15) and WENNA VAREL (his wife; Sol promised in B2 ch.9 to tell her what she learned the same day). Decision: one line in ch.3, Ansa mentions Wenna sent bread, Varel is home. Nothing else. Sol keeping her promise is shown by that line.
22. PELL (night attendant, archive tower), BRACK (Weir Stair keeper), IVES (gate clerk, Mire Gate). Witnesses from B2 ch.6-9. Decision: not mentioned in Book 3. Closed off the page.
23. CORMAC VRELL (keeper of the Thorne Precedent Archive, B2 ch.16-17). Decision: not mentioned. The archive stays shut. Sol keeps her mother's contract copy in her coat; it is the paper she lays on the table in ch.27.
24. DESSA and the surveyors' commission. Decision: not on the page. One line in ch.38: the commission entered Renn's map into the record.
25. BRAM (grey ribbon): carries the order in ch.18; writes two numbers in ch.23. Closed cast.
26. ALDOUS and MALRIK. Aldous: ch.2, 20, 25, 40, 44. Malrik: ch.12, then one look in ch.43. After ch.27 Malrik learns the steward carried the years for his father and his house; his scene is one line in ch.44.
