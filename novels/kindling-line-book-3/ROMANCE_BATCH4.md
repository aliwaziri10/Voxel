## CH.16 | KAEL | Day 14, the stair up to Yelva's back room
SCENE. Morning. One flight of narrow stairs, a rail on one side. Sol behind him. Mood: quiet, a little proud, a little afraid.
WHAT HAPPENS.
- Kael climbs the flight without the stick. His leg holds. Sol, behind him, says nothing and does not reach for him. He can hear her not reaching.
- Humour: at the top he says she can say it. She says she has nothing to say about a man climbing a flight of stairs. He says that is the loudest thing she has ever said. She says Yelva will complain about the mud regardless.
- Friction: he wants to go in alone and tell Mara what happened without Sol in the room. Sol says she will stand at the door, not in the words, and that is not up for discussion. He says he did not ask for discussion. She says he was about to.
- Touch: she grips the rail instead of him. Her knuckles go white. He sees it and understands the not-reaching cost her something.
- Want: he turns on the top step and kisses her once, quickly, before Yelva's door opens. She says: "That was not later." He says: "That was now." She says: "Acceptable."
WHAT CHANGES. He does a hard thing alone and she lets him, and both know what it cost her.
HANDOVER. Mara wakes properly, thirsty and cross; Kael tells her plainly what happened on 4 Cinderveil; the clasp; "I would know it again." Plot as in BEAT_MAP_BATCH4.md.

## CH.17 | SOL | Day 15, night, the room over the tar-shop
SCENE. Late. Boots by the door, a pot of cold soup, the lamp low. Mood: tender and tired, glad before worried.
WHAT HAPPENS.
- Sol lets herself be glad about Mara before she lets herself worry about anything. She says so aloud: "I am going to be glad for one whole hour." Kael says he will keep time. Humour: he does, and calls "half" at thirty minutes in his ledger voice.
- They talk about nothing: Yelva's temper, Ansa's soup, whether Mara will complain about the cot. Sol laughs and then stops, because she is crying slightly and did not expect it. Kael does not fix it. He sits beside her.
- Friction (small, honest): Kael says he is glad too and afraid to say so, in case saying it costs something. Sol says gladness is not a loan. He says that is the nicest thing anyone has ever said about his habits. She says it was an insult. He says it was both.
- Touch: she takes his face in both hands and looks at him as though checking for damage. He lets her.
- Want: a closed door, a lamp turned down. Cut away at the door, before anything is shown. Register: unhurried, warm, a little shaky, not new.
WHAT CHANGES. Sol learns she can be glad in the middle of the dark and not pay for it.
HANDOVER. Morning at the east gauge hut: Odo and Tessa chart the draw against the eleventh breath. Plot as in BEAT_MAP_BATCH4.md (scene 17B).

## CH.18 | KAEL | Day 16, the room over the tar-shop, morning
SCENE. Grey light at the window. Tea going cold. Mood: light, teasing, a little sharp.
WHAT HAPPENS.
- Kael stands beside Sol at the window and she moves him two steps off. Humour: he steps back, she says "More." He steps again. She says "There." He says he is now standing in the doorway to the stair. She says that is exactly the distance.
- Friction: "I am allowed to stand near you." "When I am burning, you are not." Neither wins. Kael says nobody is burning. Sol says that is today. He says that is a sneaky way to win an argument. She says she has been practising.
- Both laugh. It breaks the tension and then the tension comes back and sits between them, quietly.
- Touch: she crosses the two steps and puts her palm against his chest, just to feel it, then takes it back. He says: "That was nearer than two steps." She says: "I was not burning."
- Want: she asks him to say the rule aloud, just once, like a prayer. He says it. It sounds ridiculous and it sounds true.
WHAT CHANGES. The rule becomes a joke they both understand, which makes it harder to break and easier to keep.
HANDOVER. Bram knocks with the Council custodian's thirty-day order by note, dated Day 0, served Day 16; the pick will be named Day 26. Plot as in BEAT_MAP_BATCH4.md. (No scene with the custodian; the order is a note only.)

## CH.19 | SOL | Day 17, the head of the second stair
SCENE. Before the descent to the plate. Cold air, rope coiled at the top, Joren posted. Mood: tight, practical, tender underneath.
WHAT HAPPENS.
- They run the rule aloud like a ritual. Kael says "Joren" three times because Sol asks him to, to make sure nothing is left unsaid. Humour: Joren says he is not a bell. Sol says tonight he is.
- Friction: Kael wants a shorter rope so he can be nearer. Sol measures it with her arm and will not shorten it. He says she is being unreasonable. She says she is being exact.
- Touch: she ties the rope around his waist herself and hands him the far end. Her fingers are steady on the knot. His are not.
- Want: she presses her forehead briefly against his and says: "Count me." He says: "I always do." She says: "Out loud." He says: "In four. Hold six. Out eight." She nods.
- Quiet: he says he will keep the rope in his hand and feel her pulse through it. She says that is not what the rope is for. He says it is now.
WHAT CHANGES. He stands further from her than he wants to, and says so, and stays.
HANDOVER. The plate at the foot of the stair; Sol burns half a year; it answers with a twelfth breath; she feels his pulse through the rope; Ulla's hook at the top of the stair. Plot as in BEAT_MAP_BATCH4.md.

## CH.20 | KAEL | Day 18, the room over the tar-shop, before and during Aldous
SCENE. Afternoon. Ansa at the door. A loaf on the table. Mood: wary, then unexpectedly gentle.
WHAT HAPPENS.
- Before Aldous arrives, Kael and Sol argue about whether to let him in at all. Kael says he is a Thorne. Sol says he is also a man who spoke against his house in front of the whole Council. Kael says that is true. Sol says: "Then be fair." He says he is being careful. She says those are not the same.
- Humour: Ansa says anyone who wants to quarrel about bread should do it with her, not at her table. They both stop.
- Aldous arrives alone. Sol puts bread in front of him and says nothing. Kael watches her be kind to someone who owes her nothing and notices something warm and uncomplicated in his chest.
- Touch: under the table Sol finds his hand and gives it one firm squeeze, then lets go.
- Want: Kael realises he does not feel the urge to manage Aldous, and that the feeling is new, and that it came from watching her. He tells her so later, in four words.
WHAT CHANGES. Kael sees what it looks like to be generous without control, and learns it from watching her.
HANDOVER. Aldous brings the still-room primer; right-leaning model letters; the last page with a name inked out and a date. Plot as in BEAT_MAP_BATCH4.md.
