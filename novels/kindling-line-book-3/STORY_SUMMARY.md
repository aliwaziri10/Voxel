# KINDLING LINE BOOK 3 - THE STORY IN PLAIN WORDS (one page, for Zia)

Written 2026-10-04. This is the whole plot as the beat maps now stand. Nothing has been written as chapters yet. Zia: read it, then say what you want changed. Anything marked (my idea) is the author's proposal, not something Books 1 or 2 already said.

WHERE WE START. Book 2 ended with the Council's Deed thrown out and the tap shut. Book 3 begins the same night. Sol (Isolde Vane) has about eighteen years left to live and Kael about thirty-four. Sol wants to be his equal and never the one he pays for. Kael's habit is to step in front of her and pay the cost himself. The whole book is about him learning to let her ask, and her learning to ask.

CHAPTERS 1 TO 11. The clock is back on the page. Under the boiling house a second line is still adding a breath to every cycle, and we learn it holds years that were taken off other people over forty years. Sol and Kael say their numbers aloud again. Mara, hurt saving Kael in Book 2, is getting colder. Renn the surveyor is still missing. One short political scene (ch.2) closes Book 2's loose ends.

CHAPTERS 12 TO 23. They grow close. Renn is found alive. The line holds seventeen years, and one of them is the year taken from Sol's mother (my idea). A Council custodian claims the stored years and serves an order. In the middle of the book (ch.23) they say their counts aloud in front of the whole Lower Reach, and Ansa answers with one word: "Eat."

CHAPTERS 24 TO 30. Kael walks without his stick. The works steward comes at night (ch.27) and tells Sol and Kael everything: she carried the years for forty years on an old order, she taught Aldous his letters, she forged his name on the witness line so someone would find her, and she locked Renn in to keep him alive (my idea). They keep it between them. The Council names a person who means to stand nearer the plate than Sol and take the years.

CHAPTERS 31 TO 35, THE DARK MOMENT. First try at closing the line. Kael, out of habit, runs in without asking and pays a year, and the try fails. Sol goes alone to her mother's plate and says the hard things aloud. They talk plainly and he says only "I will try."

CHAPTERS 36 TO 40. Renn draws the room so only one person can sit nearest the plate. Malrik Thorne sends a note and his guards hold the door (my idea). Mara walks out on her own feet. Aldous brings the steward, who asks to stand outside the door.

CHAPTERS 41 TO 45. The Council's person is held at the line with words, not force (my idea). In ch.43 Sol sits at the plate and says aloud, "Kael, stand back." He says yes at once and goes. She burns half a year, the two pegs are pulled in one breath, the line closes and the seventeen years go to her. Mara's cold lifts. Ch.44 is the night after. Ch.45, a season later, they count aloud together, thirty-three each, a letter comes from Kael's grandmother Auda, and the iron is only iron.
