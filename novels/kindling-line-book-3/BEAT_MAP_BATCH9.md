## CH.41 | SOL | Day 38 | THE DESCENT
SETTING. The boiling-house cellar, down the second stair to the foot, before the bare iron door and the plate [V]. Chalk marks for the sockets, no pegs set.
CAST (only these). Sol, Kael (stick), Odo Marl, Tessa Rook, Joren and Corren at the cellar mouth. No one else.
ROMANCE FIRST. See ROMANCE_BATCH9.md.
PLOT. They go down in order, the way the map says: Odo and Tessa first, with the cords; Sol; Kael last, to the turn only. At the foot Odo marks the two sockets in chalk where the map puts them, lintel and sill, and checks them twice. Tessa reads the gauge and reads it again: seventeen, fixed. Sol asks Kael aloud to wait at the turn and he waits. She sits on the single step at the plate for the first time with no burn and lays her palm flat on the iron. It is warm like skin and it hums, and for a moment it is as if the plate knows her; she does not burn and she does not pull away. She counts the breaths of the line: eleven, and the twelfth after it. She says aloud, so that Kael and the techs can hear, that it is the same as before and that she is not afraid of it today, and then says she is, a little. The pegs are not set; Sol and Kael carry theirs wrapped in cloth, one each, and set them down on the stair. They go up in the same order.
MECHANICS. none new.
LOCKED. Nothing is burned. Nothing is set. The pegs are not described beyond "wrapped in cloth".
INTERIOR. Sol finds the plate is the first thing in the whole business that does not feel like someone else's decision.
STAKES. Tomorrow the pick arrives and the day after the line closes.
SUB-BEATS. Relationship (the descent; the wait at the turn); plot (the marks; the gauge; the warm iron); interior (the plate does not feel like someone else's decision).
CALLBACKS. ch.38 (the map); ch.31 (the failed attempt); ch.6 (the plate warm like skin); ch.5 (the warm iron); ch.37 (not wanting to be paid in years).
DATE. Day 38.
HOOK. At the cellar mouth Joren says the Council's grey has been seen on the Council road, three figures.

## CH.42 | KAEL | Day 39
SETTING. The lane behind the tar-shop; then the yard gate and the east approach, afternoon to dusk.
CAST (only these). Kael (stick), Sol, Ansa (small, brown, knotted), Joren, Corren, Odo Marl, Tessa Rook, Mara (on a stool at the cellar mouth, upright, a stick of her own), Aldous and the works steward (in the yard, waiting), the custodian's pick (silent, Council grey) and two men (silent, Council grey), the Thorne guard (a line, unnamed, silent). No one else.
ROMANCE FIRST. See ROMANCE_BATCH9.md (the hour in which nothing is waiting to happen).
PLOT. After the hour, Joren brings word that the pick and two men are on the east approach. Kael says "Joren and Corren" aloud and goes to the Thorne line with them. The pick stops at the line; the two men stop with the pick. Nobody speaks for a long while. Kael says, aloud and in plain words, the one thing he has to say: the room holds one seat at the plate and it is taken, and nobody can be nearer than the one who sits. The pick looks at the cellar mouth, at Mara on her stool, at the stair going down, and does not come further. The Thorne guard does not move. The steward and Aldous watch from the yard. At dusk the pick sits down on a stone and stays there, with the two men beside, and nobody makes them leave. Kael goes back to Sol and says aloud that he held the door and did not step past it. She asks if it cost him; he says it did and that it was not a year.
MECHANICS. none new; the nearest-body rule is not restated.
LOCKED. No custodian. The pick says nothing and does nothing. Nobody is touched. The steward does not speak in this chapter. No debate, no speeches.
INTERIOR. Kael finds that holding a line with words, aloud and plainly, and not with his body, is a different kind of strength, and that it frightens him less than he expected.
STAKES. The pick waits at the line overnight.
SUB-BEATS. Relationship (the hour); plot (the pick held at the line); interior (words instead of the body).
CALLBACKS. ch.28 (the pick named; asked whether Lady Vane is the nearest); ch.30 (the demand); ch.27 (Sol: one hour tomorrow in which nothing is waiting); ch.36 (the Thorne guard).
DATE. Day 39.
HOOK. Sol, at the end of the day, to Kael: "Tomorrow, then." Kael: "Ask me first." "I will."

## CH.43 | SOL | Day 40 | THE CLOSE
SETTING. The yard of the boiling house, the cellar mouth, the second stair down to the foot, the plate. Morning to evening.
CAST (only these). Sol, Kael (stick), Odo Marl, Tessa Rook, Joren and Corren, Mara (at the cellar mouth), Aldous and the works steward (outside the cellar door), the custodian's pick (at the line, silent), the Thorne guard. No one else.
ROMANCE FIRST. See ROMANCE_BATCH9.md.
PLOT. The company goes down in order: Odo and Tessa, Sol, Kael to the foot. Kael sets his peg in the sill socket when Sol asks him aloud, and waits for her answer after; Sol sets hers in the lintel socket. Odo and Tessa take the cords up to the turn of the stair and read the gauge twice (seventeen, fixed). Sol sits on the single step at the plate. Kael is at the foot, an arm's reach behind her. She says it aloud, because it is the rule and because she means it: "Kael, stand back." He says "Yes" and goes, up the stair, to the turn, to the door of the passage, and does not argue and does not look round. Tessa counts the drop in the trough. Sol burns half a year, six heartbeats, counting aloud; the cost comes out of her and nobody near, and she feels it as a cold pulled out of the bones. At the sixth heartbeat she says "Now." Odo and Tessa pull in one breath. Both pegs come out. The plate gives one long sound and goes silent. The line closes. The whole stored seventeen goes to Sol as the nearest living body: she feels it arrive, all of it, like a tide, and among the years there is one she knows, her mother's. She says aloud that she has them and will carry them. The iron is still warm and then it is cooling. From the cellar mouth Mara's voice comes down the stair: "Now." Sol cries. Kael stays at the door until she calls him by name; she says "Kael." He says "Yes," and comes down.
MECHANICS. The close: the whole stored sum goes to the nearest living body (CANON_LOCK section 5). The one burn is hers. Do not add her count and the seventeen on the page; the figure thirty-three is first said in the next chapter.
LOCKED. THIS IS THE ECHO, once. Nobody has said "stand back" in this batch before this moment, and nobody says it after. Kael says yes before arguing; he does not argue at all. The steward stands outside the cellar door and is silent. No pick on the page below ground. No new person goes down.
INTERIOR. Sol discovers that being given all of it is not the same as taking it, and that what she feels first is not triumph but weight and then, slowly, warmth.
STAKES. If a second person is nearer than she is, the seventeen goes to them; the map and Kael's yes make sure it does not.
SUB-BEATS. Relationship (asking; the yes; the call); plot (the pegs, the burn, the pull, the close); interior (the weight, then the warmth).
CALLBACKS. ch.1 (she asks him to stand back); ch.8 (he does it unasked); ch.31 (he steps in); ch.38 (the map); ch.29 (Mara: "Soon."); ch.21 (her mother's year); ch.37 (not wanting to be paid in years).
DATE. Day 40.
HOOK. Kael coming down the stair, slowly, stick on every step, and Sol saying his name a second time.

## CH.44 | KAEL | Day 40 night
SETTING. The room over the tar-shop, night. A short stretch at the door.
CAST (only these). Kael (stick), Sol (asleep for most of the chapter), Joren (at the door, brief). Aldous is at the door once, to give Malrik's line. No one else.
ROMANCE FIRST. See ROMANCE_BATCH9.md (the night after; cut away).
PLOT. After the closeness, Sol sleeps. Kael lies awake and counts her breaths, then says her count aloud once, softly: thirty-three, and his own, about thirty-three. Joren knocks and says it quietly. The pick stood at dusk and went back along the east approach without a word, and the two men with him. Mara walked home on her own feet and is asleep at Yelva's, warm. Aldous comes to the door with one sentence from his grandfather: Thorne owes the Reach a lamp and a door, and will keep both. Joren says the works steward asked to be given to no one until the line was closed and that the line is closed; Sol said, before she slept, that the steward should go to the Lower Reach and be heard by the households there, in their own time and in their own way. Joren says Ansa has taken her in for the night and has said "Eat." Renn's wife sent word that Renn and she go at first light and that the Council seal is with Bram. Kael does not reply to any of it except to thank Joren and to say he will tell Sol in the morning, aloud, first.
MECHANICS. none new. The carried years cannot be returned to their owners; Kael says once, to himself, that they will be carried.
LOCKED. No second telling of the steward's account. She does not appear. No investigation. Malrik is not on the page; Aldous speaks the one sentence and goes.
INTERIOR. Kael realises he is not afraid for the first night in a very long time, and does not trust it, and lets it be.
STAKES. None left; the chapter is the breath out.
SUB-BEATS. Relationship (the night; her breathing); plot (the loose ends told in a handful of lines); interior (not afraid; not trusting it; letting it be).
CALLBACKS. ch.43 (the close); ch.5 and ch.8 (counting); ch.12 (Malrik at the door); ch.27 (the steward's request); ch.19 (Ulla and Renn mean to leave).
DATE. Day 40 night.
HOOK. Kael sets the stick against the wall, lies down, and counts her breaths until he sleeps.

## CH.45 | SOL | A season later, no day number
SETTING. The room over the tar-shop, morning; the lane; the gallery mouth; the cellar of the boiling house and the foot of the second stair. No other location.
CAST (only these). Sol, Kael (stick), Ansa (small, brown, knotted), Joren (brings the letter). No one else.
ROMANCE FIRST. See ROMANCE_BATCH9.md.
PLOT. A season later. In the room they count aloud together, thirty-three each, the way they say good night. Joren brings a letter from the Ashworth gate, addressed to both names in a hand neither of them knows. Sol reads it aloud to Kael: it is from Auda Ashworth and it is short; it says the family has opened a place for both of them at its table and a line in its ledger, and that she would like to be told, aloud and first, the day they come. Kael says nothing for a long moment and then says he will say it first. They go out into the lane, where the blue lamp in the gallery mouth is lit, and the households of the Lower Reach are about their day. Ansa says "Eat." They go down to the cellar of the boiling house together, no rope, no cords, no one waiting. At the foot of the second stair Sol lays her palm on the plate in the bare iron door. It is cold, ordinary iron. She leaves her hand there a moment and it does nothing. Kael asks, aloud, whether she wants him to wait. She says no, come here. He comes. They go up.
MECHANICS. none new.
LOCKED. Auda appears only as a letter. No Malrik, no steward, no custodian, no pick. No plot is opened. No count is stated except "thirty-three each". Last image: the iron is only iron.
INTERIOR. Sol realises she is no longer counting the days she has; she is counting, with him, the ones they will spend.
STAKES. None; the book ends on rest.
SUB-BEATS. Relationship (the count together; the letter; the cellar); plot (the lamp, the plate cold); interior (counting the days they will spend).
CALLBACKS. ch.1 (the clock on the page); ch.43 (the plate warm); B2 ch.44-45 (Auda); ch.5 (the first count); ch.23 (Ansa: "Eat."); ch.30 (they say the counts aloud).
DATE. A season later.
HOOK. None. The last line is quiet: the iron is only iron.
