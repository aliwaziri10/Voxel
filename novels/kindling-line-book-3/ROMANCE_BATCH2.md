# ROMANCE BEATS - BATCH 2 (CH.6 TO 10) - Kindling Line Book 3

Written 2026-10-04. Replaces the one-line romance gestures for ch.6 to 10 in BEAT_MAP.md. Each romance beat is a FULL SCENE of at least 600 words, placed first, before any plot. The plot beats in BEAT_MAP.md stay as written and follow each scene. No new characters or places. No restaged first kiss, first "I love you" or first night. Counts off the page unless the BEAT_MAP entry allows them. No em dashes. The collar beat belongs to ch.12 and ch.23 only. "Later" and "Hold me to it" are their private words from ch.5.
Format: SCENE | WHAT HAPPENS | WHAT CHANGES | HANDOVER.

## CH.6 | KAEL | Day 4, the cellar mouth before the descent
SCENE. The top of the boiling-house cellar steps, early. Cold air coming up. Joren holding the rope, Corren and two scouts at the mouth. Mood: brisk, a little too brisk, covering nerves.
WHAT HAPPENS.
- Kael says "Joren and Corren" aloud as he announces they are going down, as the rule requires. Corren writes the hour. Sol watches him do it and says nothing, which he can feel as approval.
- Kael ties the line between them himself, on her wrist side. He gets the knot slightly wrong. Humour: she lets it hold for three breaths to see whether he will notice, then fixes it without a word. He says: "You let me." She says: "I wanted to see how long you would trust it."
- Friction: she wants to go first because the iron answers her and she is lighter on the stair. He wants to go first because of the stick and the dark. Neither says the real reason. She says it for both: "You want to be in front of me." He says yes. She says: "Then you will be behind me and you will hate it." He agrees.
- Touch: he checks her wrist cuff and she puts his hand flat over her pulse. "Count it." He counts. It is fast. She says: "I am allowed to be afraid. I am not allowed to stop."
- Want: a long look in the half dark. Neither says "later". Both think it.
WHAT CHANGES. He agrees to go second and tells her why he does not like it, instead of deciding it for her.
HANDOVER. The descent of the second stair as in BEAT_MAP.md (Renn's chalk, the bare iron door, the plate warm under both hands).

## CH.7 | SOL | Day 5, the room over the tar-shop
SCENE. Afternoon light on the table. The leaf lies beside Renn's sheet. Ansa is out. Mood: quiet, careful, a little sore.
WHAT HAPPENS.
- Sol cannot bring herself to read her mother's row. She says so. Kael asks, "May I?" He asks, which she notices.
- Humour: he reads in his ledger voice and it sounds like an inventory. She says: "Say it as though she were a person." He tries again and is worse. She laughs for the first time in two days, and he looks pleased and slightly hurt.
- Touch: when he reaches the carried line he stops. She puts her hand flat over the page, over his hand. He turns his palm up under hers.
- Friction: she says she wants her mother's year back, as a plain fact, and does not want anyone to promise it. He says he would bring it back if he could. She says: "That is not yours to pay, and I need you to stop saying that as if it were." He says: "I am saying it because it is true." She says: "Then say it and leave it. Do not reach for the bill."
- Want: she leans her forehead against his arm and breathes. He does not move the arm.
WHAT CHANGES. She tells him what she wants and what she does not want in the same breath. He takes both.
HANDOVER. Sol lays the leaf beside Renn's sheet, the small drawn line with eleven ticks, and says "moved to where." Plot as in BEAT_MAP.md.

## CH.8 | KAEL | Day 6, the lane above the tannery terraces
SCENE. Morning walk to the east-break stones. Wind and scree ahead. Mood: easy until it is not.
WHAT HAPPENS.
- Kael has been rehearsing something. Sol catches him at it: he steps back, stops, looks at his own feet and steps back again, silently. She asks what he is doing. He admits he is practising standing back. Humour: she laughs so hard she has to stop walking. He says it is harder than it looks. She says it looks ridiculous.
- Friction: she does not want him to practise. She wants him to do it when it counts, not as a performance. He says that is how he fails: by doing it only when it counts. She sees the point and says so.
- Touch: she grips his sleeve and turns him to face her. She says, serious: "If I ask, I need you to go before you argue." He says yes. She says: "You said yes at the tar-shop and then argued." He says: "I argued in my head." She says: "That counts as arguing."
- Want: she keeps her hand on his sleeve a breath longer than she needs. Wind in her hair. He thinks about how rarely she is the one who touches first, and notices it.
WHAT CHANGES. The stand-back promise becomes a thing they can joke about, which makes it harder to break.
HANDOVER. The stone, Odo, Tessa and Tam with the lamp. Sol closes the line. Kael steps back unasked and Sol notices. Plot as in BEAT_MAP.md. (This is the only chapter besides ch.1, 31 and 43 where the echo appears.)

## CH.9 | SOL | Day 7, the sill at night
SCENE. After supper, both on the sill, the lamp out. The Reach below is dark except for one blue point. Mood: quiet argument, low voices.
WHAT HAPPENS.
- Kael says he would give her his years if it worked that way. He says it plainly, not as a gift.
- Friction: Sol says it does not work that way: transfer is involuntary and nothing can be chosen. He says he knows. She says: "Then why say it?" He says: "Because I want you to know what I would do." She says: "I know what you would do. That is the problem."
- Humour: she asks whether he intends to put it in a contract with a signature line. He says he has already drafted the clause. She says the clause is rubbish.
- Touch: she takes his hand, turns it over, and traces the line of his palm with her thumb as if checking a rope for wear. She says: "I do not want your years. I want you to have them."
- Want: he asks her to say it again. She will not. He asks her to say it as a wish and not an offer. She says it as a wish, once. They end holding hands and not saying anything else.
WHAT CHANGES. She tells him she wants him to have his years, which is the first time she has said she wants him to live long.
HANDOVER. Odo converts the gauge and says seventeen. Plot as in BEAT_MAP.md.

## CH.10 | KAEL | Day 8, Yelva's back room, night watch
SCENE. Low lamp, Mara on the cot, a pot of Yelva's tea gone cold. Sol on the stool beside him, Joren on the bench in the passage. Mood: tender and tired.
WHAT HAPPENS.
- They talk in whispers about nothing useful: the tea, Ansa's soup, Yelva's temper. Humour: Sol says Yelva scolds the dead and the living in the same voice and prefers the dead. Kael says Mara has probably been listening and agrees.
- Sol insists she is awake. She falls asleep mid-sentence against his shoulder.
- Kael does not move. His leg goes numb to the hip and he does not move. He counts her breaths and finds he has stopped counting the line.
- Touch: her hand slips into his coat sleeve in her sleep.
- Want: he thinks about asking her something he cannot ask yet. He thinks about the next thirty years and then stops himself, because he does not know whether he has them, and then thinks of her saying "I want you to have them."
- Joren looks in, sees them, says nothing, and puts a blanket over their knees.
WHAT CHANGES. Kael lets himself picture the long road with her, once, and does not pretend he did not.
HANDOVER. Mara wakes for one breath and says "Renn, down." Plot as in BEAT_MAP.md.
