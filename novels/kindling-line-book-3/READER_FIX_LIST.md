# READER FIX LIST - Kindling Line (Books 1, 2, 3)

Written 2026-10-04 at Zia's request. Test used: would an ordinary reader FEEL this (confusion, a cheat, a broken promise), or only a checker find it? The snag lists in both digest INDEX files are NOT a to-do list. Only the items below are.

## A. MUST FIX (a reader would feel it). Needs Zia's decision before any edit.
1. Book 1, the ward's death is told about six different ways (digest ch.24-30, 32, 38), and the book ends with three different verdicts (ch.41-43). Pick one version of each and repair the others. Do this before Book 1 is published.
2. Book 1, Sol's mother and Kael's father keep changing: the mother's name and ward come in several versions (Vane, Vellin, Vex, Mira and others); Valerius Ashworth's part in her Reckoning differs by chapter; Kael's status swings through ch.36-40. Pick one version of each.
3. Book 2 ch.44-45: AUDA ASHWORTH, Kael's grandmother, appears from nowhere. No earlier chapter has a living grandmother. Either add one line about her earlier, or change the role to someone already on the page.
4. Book 1 ch.44 has Kael's father stripped and exiled; Book 2 has him buried nine months before ch.33. Fix by one line in Book 3 or Book 2 saying only that he is dead. Do not say how.

## B. CHEAP FIX (one word or one number; do together in one pass later)
1. Sol's peg is said to be found "in the outer chamber" in B2 ch.36 and ch.41; ch.29 has her take it from the ledge below the Tar Lane forge. Change two words.
2. B2 ch.45: "Auditor of the Council" should be "Auditor of the Accord".
3. B2 ch.39: "thirteen days" to the 28th should be eleven.
4. Two people named Tessa: Tessa Rook (B2 ch.4, a tech) and a nineteen-year-old ward Tessa in B1 ch.29. Rename the B1 one.
5. Ansa's room: over the tar-shop in B2; Book 3 batch 1 ch.3 puts her kitchen below it. Fix in the Book 3 beats.
6. Book 3 ch.21 beat says Sol's mother "died at forty-two, three winters after her Reckoning" and "thirteen years ago". The Book 1 digest cannot confirm "three winters after" and says twelve years. Drop "three winters after" and keep one year figure.
7. Names reused from Zia's other series (Amity Falls): Corwin, Farrow, Aldous. Zia decides whether to leave them. New Book 3 names Ulla and Yelva were not found anywhere else.

## C. IGNORE (only a checker would find these)
