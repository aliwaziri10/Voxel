## CH.31 | SOL | Day 29 | THE ATTEMPT
SETTING. The cellar of the boiling house, down the second stair to the foot, before the bare iron door and the plate [V ch.6 of this book]. Odo and Tessa on the stair above the turn, where the cords come up. Kael at the door of the passage at the head. No other location.
CAST (only these). Sol (undyed coat), Kael (square hand, stick on stairs only until he falls), Odo Marl (fifty, broad, says little, takes every reading twice), Tessa Rook (twenty-three, quick, chalk-line at her belt), Joren (older warden, scar across the back of one hand) and Corren (warden) at the passage, the custodian's pick (one ward-taker in Council grey, silent, no name, no face described), two men in Council grey (unnamed, silent). No one else.
ROMANCE FIRST. Before she goes down Sol puts the end of the rope in Kael's hand, as at the gallery water, and neither speaks of it. They say their counts aloud as they did the night before, sixteen and a half and about thirty-four. (Fresh beat: the rope end; the counting habit is already established.)
PLOT. Sol sits at the plate. Tessa counts the drop in the trough. Sol burns one year, twelve heartbeats, to bring the plate to its drop so the pull can be made; she counts the heartbeats aloud. At the third heartbeat there is shouting in the passage: the pick and two men have come early, a day before the order runs out, and are through Joren and Corren's line. Kael drops the rope and runs the stair. He does not say it first. He does not wait. He steps between Sol and the plate, nearer than she is, and the year comes out of him. He goes down on one knee, then on the floor, and his stick rolls away. The pick stops on the bottom step and looks at Kael on the floor and does not come further. Sol, at the plate, sees that if the pull is made now the stored seventeen goes to the nearest living body, and the nearest is Kael. She cries one word, "Hold." Tessa holds the count. Odo does not pull. The cords stay slack. The plate hums and goes quiet. The close does not happen. Joren and Corren bring the pick and the two men up and out. The pick says nothing. Odo and Tessa carry Kael up the stair. Sol carries his stick.
MECHANICS. The cost is the one in Book 1: the nearest living body pays. Do not explain timing. If a person is nearer the plate than Sol when the line closes, that person takes the stored seventeen. Sol states this in her own words once, in her head or aloud, as she cries "Hold."
LOCKED. This is the third appearance of the echo and it is his failure. He wrote on the fifth page that he would say it first and wait. He does not. Nobody says "stand back." No other burn; no years paid by anyone else; the pick takes nothing. The pick is not described and does not speak. Do not describe where the cords end.
INTERIOR. Sol sees him fall and the first thing she feels, for one heartbeat, is that the year did not come out of her. She does not look at it and does not name it. (It is named aloud in ch.33.)
STAKES. Mara is at the elbows and the day is nearly gone; the attempt failed; Kael is on the floor.
SUB-BEATS. Relationship (the rope end, the counts); plot (the burn, the rush, "Hold"); interior (the flicker she will not name).
CALLBACKS. B2 ch.14 (Kael anchors the rope); ch.1 (she asks him to stand back); ch.8 (he does it unasked); ch.28 (the fifth page); ch.30 ("Say it first"); ch.26 (Odo: a puller does not move until the count is done).
DATE. Day 29.
HOOK. On the stair going up, Sol says Kael's count aloud under her breath, about thirty-three, and knows exactly what it cost.

## CH.32 | KAEL | Day 29 night to Day 30
SETTING. The room over the tar-shop, night then morning [V B2 ch.12, 44]. No other location.
CAST (only these). Kael (square hand, stick at the bedside), Joren (scar across the back of one hand), Yelva (healer). No one else; Sol is not on the page.
ROMANCE FIRST. Kael wakes with a wool sock on his numb left foot. Sol put it there while he slept. He lies and looks at it and does not move. (Fresh beat: answers ch.26.) She is not in the room.
PLOT. Joren is on the stool by the door and says it exactly, because she asked him to: "She said to say it aloud when you woke, before anything else. 'I am at my mother's plate. Do not follow. I will be back. I am asking.'" Kael asks how long. Joren says since the carrying-up, a night. Yelva comes; his leg is worse, the stick is needed even on level ground for a few days, and what he paid cannot be given back. Kael says it aloud: he paid a year and he did not say it first. Joren does not argue. Kael finds the fifth page folded on the table, reads the one line, and puts it in his coat. He keeps his word and does not go after her, and says aloud that he is not going, because the rule is that he tells her before and asks, and she has asked. Yelva reports in the same breath that Mara's cold crossed the elbow in the night.
MECHANICS. none new. Kael is about thirty-three; he says it once, flatly, as a figure.
LOCKED. Nobody says "stand back". Kael does not go to the anchor. No steward on the page. The order's last day is the day ahead; Joren says it once and no one dwells on it.
INTERIOR. Shame, plainly: the body moved before the promise. He is not sure the promise is one his body can keep, and he does not hide that from himself.
STAKES. Mara; and Sol alone on the steps.
SUB-BEATS. Relationship (the sock, the message); plot (the cost, the leg, Mara); interior (shame).
CALLBACKS. ch.26 (the sock); ch.28 (the page); ch.21 (her mother's plate); ch.31 (the failure).
DATE. Day 29 night to Day 30.
HOOK. Yelva, at the door: the cold crossed the elbow in the night. Kael does not ask to go to Mara; he says he will be where Sol asked him to be, and sits down.

## CH.33 | SOL | Day 30
SETTING. The Lower Spine anchor chamber at the foot of the 211 steps, her mother's anchor plate [V B2 ch.4; ch.21 of this book]. No other location.
CAST (only these). Sol (undyed coat) alone on the page. Ansa may be heard setting a pot at the top of the steps; she does not speak and does not appear.
ROMANCE FIRST. Sol has carried Kael's stick down the 211 steps and leans it against the wall beside her. She says his name to the empty chamber and then his count, aloud, the way they say their counts at night. (Fresh beat: she keeps the counting habit alone.)
PLOT. She does not touch the anchor plate and does not burn. She sits on the last step facing it, the way she sat as a child in her mother's records room, and does the thing she asked Kael to do in B2 ch.12: says the hard thing aloud to someone who will not leave the room. She says that when he stepped in, for one heartbeat she was glad the year did not come out of her, and that she is ashamed of that and will not be less ashamed tomorrow. She says her mother's year is one of the seventeen and she decided to count it as hers, and that she will not let him pay for her again, and that she cannot pull two pegs alone and would not. She says she has been afraid since the public step that she would use him up. Then she says the thing that turns her back toward the stair: the plan failed because she relied on his habit being different, and the fifth page said what he would do, not what he would not; the only thing that works is to ask him aloud, in the room, and wait for his answer. She does not decide how. Ansa's pot is at the top of the steps when she climbs partway; she does not climb the rest. She eats on the stairs. The order runs out at the end of this day while she is below; she knows it and does not hurry.
MECHANICS. none new. The anchor plate is not burned and not touched.
LOCKED. At the anchor plate and only there. The echo is not used. No steward. Do not add Sol's count to the stored sum; she thinks of the seventeen as her mother's one and sixteen she does not know.
INTERIOR. The ashamed flicker said aloud is the chapter. It is not forgiven; it is only said.
STAKES. If she stays away, the close is lost and Mara goes on cooling; if she returns without having said it, she carries it in.
SUB-BEATS. Relationship (alone with his stick and his count); plot (nothing moves; the order lapses); interior (the confession aloud).
CALLBACKS. B2 ch.12 (Sol: "I only wanted to have said it aloud to a person who wouldn't leave the room"); ch.21 (the anchor plate, her mother's year); ch.23 (Ansa: "Eat."); ch.31 (the flicker).
DATE. Day 30.
HOOK. At the top of the steps the pot is still warm and there is no one there.

## CH.34 | KAEL | Day 31
SETTING. The room over the tar-shop; the yard gate of the boiling house; the door of the passage at the foot of the stair to the cellar. Morning to evening.
CAST (only these). Kael (square hand, stick), Joren (scar across the back of one hand), Corren (warden, leads two scouts), Ansa (small, brown, knotted; rooms over the tar-shop), Odo Marl, Tessa Rook, the custodian's pick (silent, in Council grey, at the yard gate) and two men (unnamed, silent). Mara is mentioned, not on the page. No one else.
ROMANCE FIRST. Alone in the room before light Kael says both counts aloud to the empty room, sixteen and a half and about thirty-three, the way they say them at night, and waits as if she might answer. (Fresh beat: the same habit, done alone and from his side.)
PLOT. The order ran out at the end of the day before. The pick is at the yard gate and has not come in; Joren and Corren hold the gate and say "No" in the fewest words they can. Kael says "Joren and Corren" aloud before going out to the gate and again before going to the cellar. At the cellar Kael tells Odo and Tessa aloud that no one is to go below until Sol is back, himself included; the room is empty. Then he does one thing he has not done: he stands at the door of the passage at the head of the stair, with the stick against the wall, while the plate hums below with the line still awake, and keeps his feet on his side of the threshold the whole time it hums. It is a small thing and costs him; he holds the frame. Ansa comes up the lane in the afternoon and says only that she carried a pot down and "She ate." Kael thanks her and does not ask more. At dusk Joren reports the cold has passed the elbow.
MECHANICS. none new. The plate hums because the attempt woke it; it does not burn and nothing is paid. The gauge: still seventeen, fixed.
LOCKED. Nobody says "stand back." Kael does not follow Sol and does not say yes to anything he is not asked. The pick does not speak. No custodian on the page.
INTERIOR. Kael learns what keeping his feet where he said feels like: a held breath that does not end. He does not call it growth.
STAKES. Mara has days; the pick is at the gate; Sol is below.
SUB-BEATS. Relationship (the counts aloud); plot (the gate, the empty room); interior (the frame).
CALLBACKS. ch.30 (the counting); ch.32 (he will be where she asked); ch.23 (Ansa: "Eat."); ch.26 (the door of the passage); ch.16 (Joren and Corren by name).
DATE. Day 31.
HOOK. Joren, quietly, at dusk: the cold has passed the elbow. Kael sets the stick back against the wall and sits at the table to wait for the morning.

## CH.35 | SOL | Day 32
SETTING. The top of the 211 steps at first light; then the room over the tar-shop. No other location.
CAST (only these). Sol (undyed coat, carrying Kael's stick), Kael (square hand, stick), Ansa (at the top of the steps with an empty pot, says one word). Renn is mentioned, not on the page. No one else.
ROMANCE FIRST. Sol comes up out of the dark with his stick and holds it out. He is at the top of the steps already; Joren told him she would be coming and he came to the top and no farther. He takes the stick. Neither speaks first for a long moment. (Fresh beat: the stick returned.)
PLOT. In the room over the tar-shop they speak plainly, aloud, in turn, as the rule asks. Sol says what she said on the steps: the flicker, the shame, that she will not let him pay for her again, that she will ask him aloud and wait. Kael says what he did and did not decide: he did not decide, he moved; he wrote that he would say it first and he did not; he does not know that he can promise it and will not say he can. Sol says she has read the fifth page and does not want a promise about his body; she wants him to answer when she asks. He says "I will try." She says that is the true answer and she will take it. It is not solved and both say so: he has failed once, and she will not pretend he has not. They agree the next attempt is not tomorrow: Sol will ask Renn, through Ulla, to draw the room so that it is settled where each person may stand and who can be nearest to the plate; the plan cannot go forward on habit. They lie down together, closed door, the brief warmth of two people who have said the hard things; no restaged first.
MECHANICS. none new. Do not say where the pegs sit; only that Renn will draw the room (ch.38).
LOCKED. Nobody says "stand back." Kael does not say yes before arguing. No steward. The close is not attempted. Do not add Sol's count to the stored sum or subtract one count from the other.
INTERIOR. Sol feels lighter and does not trust it, and says so aloud.
STAKES. Mara is past the elbow and the pick is at the gate.
SUB-BEATS. Relationship (the stick; the plain talk; the night); plot (the map to be asked for); interior (lighter, and the doubt).
CALLBACKS. ch.27 (lighter and not trusted); ch.29 (the doubt); ch.33 (the confession); ch.32 (he does not go); ch.28 (the fifth page); ch.23 ("Eat.").
DATE. Day 32.
HOOK. Ansa, at the top of the steps, taking the empty pot: "Good." Sol, to Kael, going up the lane: "Renn first."
