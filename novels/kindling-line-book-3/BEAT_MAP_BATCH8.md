## WRITER RULES FOR THIS BATCH
1. INVENT NOTHING beyond this file, CANON_LOCK.md and the previous chapter's real text. No new named character, place, object, custom, number, date or bell hour. Role words only for anyone else.
2. People who may appear are ONLY those listed under CAST. Crowds are unnamed and silent.
3. Dates: never write "Year 5", a month after Cinderveil, or a bell as an hour. Use "that night", "the next morning", or the day in the entry.
4. Never state a radius in feet or metres. Use "arm's reach", "the room" and steps ("three steps up"). The pegs' sockets are decided in ch.38 and only there; before ch.38 say "the cords" only.
5. Names: Isolde Vane (I.V.), Kael Ashworth. The works steward is never named; she is on the page ONLY in ch.40. The custodian and the custodian's pick are never named and never on the page in this batch.
6. Counts allowed on the page: Sol sixteen and a half (unchanged all batch); Kael about thirty-three; stored sum seventeen; twelve heartbeats make a year. The gap (sixteen and a half) is said aloud once, in ch.37 only. Do NOT add Sol's count to the stored sum.
7. Nobody burns years in this batch. The Kindling is not used.
8. No em dashes. Spell grey. Avoid: particular, some, something, someone, somewhere, somebody, somehow, kind of, the specific. No chapter-number references in prose. No ward-binding, no chosen buffer, no oath that moves years.
9. Romance first in every chapter, before any plot. Do not restage a first kiss, first "I love you" or first night.
10. The echo (Sol asks Kael to stand back, he says yes before arguing) appears ONLY in ch.1, 8, 31, 43. Nobody in this batch says "stand back". Kael's practice of waiting for her to finish asking is not the echo and uses different words.
11. Kael's stick: on level ground and stairs from ch.31. Left leg worse, slowly mending.
12. Plain "the plate" means the plate in the bare iron door at the foot of the second stair.
13. Nothing in this batch closes the line, pays a year or takes the stored seventeen.

## CH.36 | KAEL | Day 33
SETTING. The room over the tar-shop, morning; then the lane to the yard gate [V]. 
CAST (only these). Kael (square hand, stick), Sol (undyed coat), Renn (lean, forty or near, ink in the creases of every knuckle, cracked wrist in a binding), Ulla (Renn's wife), Joren (scar across the back of one hand), Corren (warden). No one else.
ROMANCE FIRST. See ROMANCE_BATCH8.md (they practise asking aloud and waiting, in small things).
PLOT. Ulla brings Renn up the tar-shop stairs; Sol asks him, aloud and plainly, to draw the room and the stair so that it is settled where each person may stand and where the pegs sit. Renn says he can, but he needs the sealed page he gave her (the back of it, which is clean), two days, and a promise that nobody makes him go down. Sol gives him the page and says nobody will. Ulla says she and Renn will leave after the close and that she is saying it aloud so that no one is surprised. Joren comes in with a folded note from Malrik Thorne, brought to the east approach by a Thorne house guard: from first light the Thorne house guard holds the east approach at Thorne's cost; the pick and anyone in Council grey are not to pass; Thorne keeps the door and nothing else. Sol reads it aloud. Kael says only that it is an offer of a door, not of a hand. They accept it as a door. Kael says "Joren and Corren" aloud before he goes to the yard gate with them to see the Thorne guard standing where the note says; the pick has withdrawn toward the Council road.
MECHANICS. none new.
LOCKED. Malrik is not on the page, only the note. The note does not mention the steward or the years. No custodian. The pick is not on the page; the Thorne guard stands, nothing is said, no one is touched.
INTERIOR. Kael sees that Sol does the asking now and he does the answering, and that he is more at ease in that order than he expected.
STAKES. Two days for Renn; Mara cooling; the pick close.
SUB-BEATS. Relationship (practising the rule); plot (Renn's two days; Malrik's door); interior (more at ease than he expected).
CALLBACKS. ch.35 ("Renn first"); ch.15 (the sealed page); ch.12 (Malrik at the tar-shop door); ch.30 (the pick).
DATE. Day 33.
HOOK. At the yard gate the Thorne guard stands in a line across the east approach and does not look at them.

## CH.37 | SOL | Day 34
SETTING. The yard of the boiling house in the afternoon; the room over the tar-shop at night.
CAST (only these). Sol, Kael (stick), Odo Marl (fifty, broad, takes every reading twice), Tessa Rook (twenty-three, quick, chalk-line at her belt), Ansa (small, brown, knotted). No one else.
ROMANCE FIRST. See ROMANCE_BATCH8.md.
PLOT. Afternoon: Odo and Tessa test the cords on the stand-in again in the yard, now with Renn's absence from the plan; Odo says nothing has changed in what he and Tessa are to do; Tessa says she would like the count to be spoken by Sol, since Sol will be nearest. Sol says the count will be hers and the pull will be theirs. At night in the room Sol says the gap aloud: sixteen and a half, between her count and his. She says she does not want the years when the line closes: they were taken off other people over forty years, and she does not want to be paid in them. Kael says he does not want it for her either, and that he cannot make the rule send them anywhere else. She says she knows; the rule is the rule and she is the one at the plate. They are both quiet. She says she will carry them and not call them hers, and Kael says he will count with her. The private scene follows, cut away at the door [PROPOSED; no first night].
MECHANICS. The close sends the whole stored sum to the nearest living body (CANON_LOCK section 5); Sol says it once, plainly, in her own words.
LOCKED. The gap is said aloud once, here. No other figure. Sol's count is not added to the seventeen on the page; Kael does not finish the sum.
INTERIOR. Sol sees that what she hates is not the years but being paid out of other people's lives; she says so and then lets it be.
STAKES. If she refuses the years, the line stays open and Mara goes on cooling; if she takes them she lives on what was taken.
SUB-BEATS. Relationship (the gap said aloud; the night); plot (the cords tested again); interior (not wanting the payment).
CALLBACKS. ch.22 (the gap; Kael will not finish the sum); ch.21 (her mother's year); ch.8 (the rule once); ch.27 (the hand carried them).
DATE. Day 34.
HOOK. Ansa at the door with the pot, not asking: "Eat." Sol eats, and says it is good.

## CH.38 | KAEL | Day 35 | RENN'S MAP
SETTING. The room over the tar-shop, morning; then the cellar of the boiling house down to the turn of the second stair; then the room. 
CAST (only these). Kael (stick), Sol, Renn, Ulla, Odo Marl, Tessa Rook, Joren and Corren. No one else.
ROMANCE FIRST. See ROMANCE_BATCH8.md.
PLOT. Renn lays the page on the table, its back covered in his small level hand: the second stair, the turn, the bare iron door, the plate, and two sockets, one set in the lintel above the plate and one in the sill below it, a man's height apart, so that a person sitting at the plate is between them and the cords run up through the lintel and the sill to the turn of the stair and on to the pullers. The foot of the stair has room for one person to sit at the plate and no more; the next nearest place is three steps up, and the pullers' place is above the turn, further than any person could reach the plate. Renn says the map is made so that nobody but the one who sits can be nearest. He adds the one thing he says plainly: the sockets take the pegs only if both are set in the same breath, and each peg is pulled by the cord and not by the hand. Odo reads the map twice. Tessa reads it once, then again. Kael is given the door of the passage at the head of the stair, further from the plate than Sol on any count, and says aloud that he will be there and will not move till she asks. They walk down to the turn of the stair to see it; Renn does not come. Odo and Tessa set the cord eyes in the wall on his instruction. The surveyors' commission enters the map in the record (one line, off the page, told by Ulla).
MECHANICS. Where the pegs sit is stated here and only here. A person other than Sol would have to sit on the plate's own step to be nearer; the room cannot hold two there.
LOCKED. This is the only chapter that states the sockets. Do not add measurements. The map's back is the page that was "lodged with the surveyor": answered in one line, the page went to Sol and not to the Council or Thorne.
INTERIOR. Kael finds it is easier to stand where a map says he stands than where his body says.
STAKES. If a second person can get nearer than Sol, the seventeen goes to them.
SUB-BEATS. Relationship (the map; the standing place); plot (the sockets; the nearest-body test); interior (easier to follow a map than a habit).
CALLBACKS. ch.31 (the cords); ch.26 (Odo: a puller does not move until the count is done); ch.15 (the sealed page); ch.36 (two days).
DATE. Day 35.
HOOK. Renn, rolling the page: "Now nobody can be nearer. Unless they are carried in."

## CH.39 | SOL | Day 36
SETTING. The yard of the boiling house, then the east approach at dusk; the Thorne guard in line at the gate.
CAST (only these). Sol, Kael (stick), Mara (Ashworth warden, twenty years' service, grey in the face, upright with a stick of her own), Joren, Corren, Yelva (healer), Tessa Rook. No one else.
ROMANCE FIRST. See ROMANCE_BATCH8.md.
PLOT. Mara walks to the east approach on her feet, against Yelva's orders, flanked by Joren and Corren, one on each side. Yelva follows and says her piece once and gives up. Mara asks Sol to tell her the day the line will close; Sol tells her: the day after tomorrow's night, in two days. Mara says she will be at the cellar mouth and not the door of the passage; that she does not want to be carried; and that she has wanted to say to Kael since the fourth that she would have run for his voice again. Kael says nothing and Mara tells him it is not a thing to forgive: "You called. I ran. That is what being called is." Sol watches Kael take it in without arguing. Mara asks to see the clasp again; Sol gives it; Mara holds it a long moment and gives it back.
MECHANICS. Mara's cold: the draw ends at the close. She says her hands are warmer when Sol is near [PROPOSED, not a rule; leave it as her one line].
LOCKED. Mara is not told the steward is the hand. Mara does not enter the cellar. No custodian, no pick.
INTERIOR. Sol sees that waiting is something Mara is good at and that she herself is not.
STAKES. Mara has two days of strength; the pick will come.
SUB-BEATS. Relationship (the evening; her hand in his); plot (Mara's walk, the day told); interior (Sol not good at waiting).
CALLBACKS. ch.29 (Mara: "Soon."); ch.16 (Mara: "I would know it again"); B2 ch.26 ("Mara!"); ch.25 (the clasp).
DATE. Day 36.
HOOK. Mara, turning at the gate on Joren's arm: "Tell me when." Sol: "Tomorrow night."

## CH.40 | KAEL | Day 37 | THE NIGHT MARCH
SETTING. The lane and the east approach by night; the Thorne guard's line; the yard gate of the boiling house; the cellar mouth.
CAST (only these). Kael (stick), Sol, Aldous Thorne (pale, his grandfather's height, about twenty-five, letters lean right), the works steward (small, upright, past sixty, grey hair pinned flat, no clasp at her throat, a pale mark where it sat), Joren, Corren, Odo Marl, Tessa Rook. No one else.
ROMANCE FIRST. See ROMANCE_BATCH8.md.
PLOT. At night the company walks from the tar-shop to the east approach in the dark: Kael, Sol, Joren and Corren in front, Odo and Tessa behind with the cords. At the Thorne line Aldous steps out and speaks plainly: his grandfather knows what the steward did for his father and his house, said nothing for a night, and then gave the door. Aldous has brought her. The steward asks, in order, to stand outside the cellar door while the line is closed and to be given to no one until it is done. Sol says yes and that nothing else is promised. The steward says one thing to Kael: that she is sorry for his leg. Kael says nothing and does not forgive. Aldous stays with her; Joren and Corren take the east approach with the Thorne guard behind them; Odo and Tessa go down to the turn of the stair to lay the cords out and read the gauge twice (seventeen, fixed) and come up. The company waits the night in the yard, in turns, with the guard in line.
MECHANICS. none new.
LOCKED. This is the steward's second and last appearance on the page. No investigation. She is not named. No second telling of her account. Malrik is not on the page; Aldous speaks for him in two sentences. The pick is not on the page.
INTERIOR. Kael finds it is possible to be civil to someone he cannot forgive and to leave it unsettled.
STAKES. She will stand outside the door when the line closes, so one more person is near.
SUB-BEATS. Relationship (the march, the night); plot (Aldous and the steward come); interior (civil and unforgiving).
CALLBACKS. ch.27 (her request); ch.25 (the clasp); ch.36 (Malrik's door); B2 ch.42-43 (Aldous speaks).
DATE. Day 37.
HOOK. In the yard, before sleep, the steward, to Sol, quietly: "Do not let anyone stand close when they come out." Sol: "I know."
