# Continuity Plan — Chapters 10-20 Coordinated Rewrite

Written 2026-09-28 after full-novel audit found four independent, contradictory
versions of the ch.13-15 infiltration/Deed-discovery scene, and the ch.36-39
"co-lead" beat pre-empted twice (ch.17, ch.28). This file is the single source
of truth for what actually happens in ch.10-20 before any prose is rewritten.
Read this BEFORE touching any chapter in this range. Update it if the plan
changes during execution - do not let the prose drift from this plan the way
the original generation drifted from brief.txt.

## Canon decisions (apply everywhere, no exceptions)

**The Thorne seal**, final and only correct version, to be used verbatim in
every chapter that describes it: a three-strand unbroken knot, a drop, a
flame, and a feather at each terminus, worked in violet-and-copper house
colors, pressed into wax that is dark and near-black. NOT red wax, NOT a
hound/hawk/viper/crown/vine/tower/thorn-bush device, NOT any animal or plant
motif. If a chapter needs a short version: "the Thorne knot" or "Thorne's
three-strand seal." Sixteen wrong variants were found across the existing
manuscript (ch.10 through ch.45) - every one of them needs correcting when
that chapter comes up for its structural check, not just ch.10-20.

**The site**: Anchor Seven, the Lower Reach, the disused ropewalk site
flooded and sealed since the ropewalk fire of Year One - the same site
planted in ch.8's rewrite via the Thorne-sealed entry pass and the Accord
register's "contested" (not "decommissioned") status flag. This is the ONE
site where the Ward Deed of Succession is found. No other site name is used
for this purpose anywhere in the book (Lower Ward Annex, Graywater Hollow,
Gray Hollow, "three miles north," the unnamed shaft site, and Ward Site
Seven-Three all need to be retired or repurposed as decoys/other locations,
not the Deed's hiding place).

**The escalation, chapter by chapter:**

- **Ch.9 (already clean, no change):** pattern formally named. Kael and Sol
  agree to investigate Thorne's reform petition together. Anchor Seven is
  named as one of several candidate disused sites worth checking, alongside
  the anchor status discrepancy from ch.8. Ends with the hooded figure in
  the archive stacks - keep this hook, it's good and unused elsewhere.

- **Ch.10 (rewrite needed):** Sol and Kael narrow the candidate list together,
  as partners - this is the first chapter where the pattern-named agreement
  from ch.9 is actually being lived, not just stated. They rule out several
  sites using Accord records (this can absorb the good "Emberwatch Ward
  ledger / absorption notations" research material from the current broken
  ch.10, which is genuinely strong material - just needs to NOT resolve into
  an entry-pass discovery or a full legal reading of clause 7/12/19; save
  that analysis for ch.17 and ch.20). Anchor Seven rises to the top candidate
  because of the "contested" vs "decommissioned" discrepancy. NO entry pass.
  NO scripted line. Ends on: they've settled on Anchor Seven, and Thorne's
  petition gains its next House signature, raising the time pressure.

- **Ch.11 (rewrite needed):** Preparation and a real complication - Thorne's
  surveillance (the hooded figure from ch.9) escalates, OR the political
  pressure escalates (a Council date gets set, or a rival house threatens to
  beat them to Anchor Seven). This is where tension builds without the
  discovery itself happening. Good material to reuse: the two-wardens/
  protective-detail negotiation from the current ch.11 works well as a
  partnership-in-practice beat and can stay, just not attached to a
  discovery scene at the end - it should end on them setting out for Anchor
  Seven, not arriving and finding a document.

- **Ch.12 (rewrite needed):** Optional - could be cut and merged into ch.13,
  or used for a short scene establishing exactly what's at stake if they
  don't move now (Thorne gains an eighth House signature, forcing a vote
  timeline). Do NOT use the scripted line here. Do NOT explain the Deed's
  legal content - nobody has found it yet in this version.

- **Ch.13-15 (rewrite needed, but the physical infiltration/danger material
  already drafted in the current ch.13/ch.14 is strong and reusable):** The
  Anchor Seven infiltration. Real physical danger (the descent/climb/guard
  material from the current ch.14 is good and can be kept, relocated to
  Anchor Seven and with the seal corrected). They find the Ward Deed of
  Succession, sealed, Thorne knot intact. They do NOT read it in full here -
  per rule 4, discovered but not understood. It's enough that they know what
  it claims to be and that it's dangerous. End ch.15 on the extraction/escape
  and the weight of not yet knowing what they've taken.

- **Ch.16-18 (mostly keep, fix the seal, remove the "co-lead auditor" formal
  declaration):** The entry-pass-in-the-coat discovery and the scripted
  confrontation line belong here and ONLY here - this is their one correct
  use in the whole manuscript. Keep the current ch.16's structure (it's
  well-written) with the seal corrected. Ch.17's archive-verification scene
  with Vrell is strong material and can stay - but change the ending: instead
  of "Co-leadership. Starting now," formally recorded, land on an informal,
  personal commitment only ("we go together from here" / "no more solo
  passes" as a private promise, not an Accord filing). The formal, costly,
  Accord-recorded cession of authority is ch.39's alone.

- **Ch.20 (mostly keep, fix the seal):** This is genuinely where the Deed's
  full legal meaning should land for the first time - clause seven and
  twelve, the "final authority rests with the administering house" language,
  Kael's refusal to certify. The current ch.20 draft executes this well and
  needs only the seal fix and a check that nothing here was already fully
  explained back in the rewritten ch.10/17 (it shouldn't be, once those are
  fixed per this plan).

## Ch.28 adjustment (not a full rewrite - a scope trim)

Ch.28 is the correct home for the first "I love you" (rule 7) and is well
written. It needs ONE change: remove "Equals, starting now. No unilateral
decisions. No solo audits." as a stated, settled resolution. Replace with
something that reads as a real, felt promise Kael is not yet capable of
fully keeping - an emotional commitment, not a structural one. This
preserves the ch.33-35 rupture's teeth (rule 8: NOT resolved by ch.35) and
ch.39's formal cession as the first time the promise becomes structural
rather than personal. Also remove the second verbatim instance of the
scripted ch.16-18 line here - by ch.28 the pattern doesn't need the exact
sentence repeated to land; a shorter callback ("the same thing I told you in
the coat" or similar) preserves the connective tissue without diluting the
original line further.

## What does NOT need to change
