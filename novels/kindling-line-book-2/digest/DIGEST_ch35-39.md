# DIGEST - Kindling Line Book 2, ch.35-39

Fact lookup from the live chapter files (main), written 2026-10-03. Not new canon. If a chapter disagrees, the chapter wins; fix this file.

## KEY CANON IN THIS BATCH
- THE RULE, UPGRADED (ch.35, 13 Cinderveil): before either goes past a door alone, they say it aloud to whoever is posted, by name. Named holders: Joren and Corren ("people, not a post"). Idea came from Ansa ("a rule that has to be spoken aloud, to another person, every single time"). Both pegs are carried "in one hand" to the commission.
- THE PEGS ARE TWINS (ch.36, 38): Dessa, chair of the commission of surveyors (old woman, ink in the creases of her knuckles, eleven surveyors, Renn's empty chair with his name-card), says same mold, so the second peg was placed, not found. By ch.38 she reads it as placed before the water began to recede, possibly before Renn went missing. This settles the old index note: the ch.36 twin pegs ARE the ledge peg (ch.29) and the table peg (ch.32).
- CASTERS (ch.38-39): four smiths on the Reach cast to that tolerance: one dead six years; one who left the Reach after her husband's Reckoning went badly; a boy of nineteen apprenticed to the fourth; the fourth cast his last commission for House Thorne eight years ago (a ceremonial die for a seal retired that year, found filed in the guild's own book). Dessa had the names a day early (17th, due the 18th). Nobody is named in the text.
- CELLAR CUSTODY: Council seal, Ashworth ward-band, and since the 15th a third lock insisted on by the Chancellor's office; nobody opens it without all three present. Commission ordered it reopened under joint Council and Ashworth watch.
- WATER: held three hands above Kael's scratch on the 13th; had dropped a hand's width by dawn on the 14th; Sol: walkable again "inside the week".
- CO-LEAD: on the 14th Kael tells Sol he intends to petition Ashworth's elders to recognise her as co-lead, "my name beside yours and not above it". On the 15th Sol says yes to the title, no to the timing: after the Council reading on the 28th, timed by her. Kael: "I'll draft nothing until you tell me to draft it." Kael keeps a ledger with a folded page of lines; the fourth line is unfinished; one line reads "Her name first, not appended". The house elders (ch.38) independently say do not petition early.
- THORNE AND ASHWORTH: Thorne's steward visited Ashworth twice that week: condolences, then a private offer declined unheard (ch.38). Tam's messenger source (ch.39): at the second visit a paper was shown, not taken, and looked at longer than a refusal needs.
- ASHWORTH HOUSE: Ferra, old woman, steward of the house, signed Kael's appointment papers three years ago; two elders; a cousin from a collateral branch who handles Council relations. Kael's father's rooms, pale ring on the writing table where the inkstand stood. Ferra: the house has "decided your father raised a son too tender for the seat".
- RENN MISSING: ten days on the 14th, twelve on the 16th, thirteen on the 17th. Ansa's lane boys have not seen a lantern and flat case.
- TAM WITHDREW on the 15th at first light (ch.37), after first telling Ansa; message to Kael: "the bread's managed...thank you for the no, and tell him this one's mine to give, not his to keep." He still works the tar, held shorter on coin. A second man withdrew on the 16th (ch.38).
- MARA: unchanged; healer says her colour is better (ch.39). Joren sits with her most mornings.
- ROMANCE STATE: ch.35 hold hands in rain; ch.36 Sol works chalk from his thumbnail, holds his hand down the lane; ch.37 he asks "May I take your hands"; forehead to his collarbone; ch.38 kiss on the spire stair; she covers his hand at Ansa's table. They sleep in one bed.

## CH.35 | 13 Cinderveil | POV Sol
- WHAT: Rain. Count 252, awning empty, no new signers since Tam. Ansa's advice (rule spoken aloud; "build it so it doesn't depend on either of you being your best self on your worst day. That's not romance. That's just carpentry."). At the cellar Joren: water stopped at change of watch, holding. Sol and Kael settle the louder rule in the rain; Corren is told by name. Kael: both will say any unknown aloud together; they hold hands.
- OBJECTS AND COUNTS: 252. Chalk under Kael's thumbnail (from marks on the third step).
- OPEN: What the second peg means.

## CH.36 | 14 Cinderveil | POV Kael
- WHAT: Commission sitting, Corren logs the hour at the door. Dessa on twins (see KEY CANON). Kael declines to guess about Renn; Dessa: first time in four sittings an Ashworth auditor has said what he does not know. Dessa wants both present when her people go down. Sol turns Renn's name-card to face the room. On the steps Sol works at the chalk on Kael's thumbnail. Cellar: water down a hand since dawn. Kael tells Sol the petition plan on the rope bridge; Sol: "That will cost you the house." Slate boy waves; Sol lifts two fingers in answer for the first time.
- CONTINUITY SNAG: Sol tells the commission her peg was found "in the outer chamber, the day the first ward-line was mapped". Ch.29 has her take it from the ledge below the Tar Lane forge.
- LINES: Dessa: "Who holds you to it?" Kael: "Joint, by name. Not the wardens on duty."

## CH.37 | 15 Cinderveil | POV Sol
- WHAT: Sol tests yes and no and finds the narrower answer (see KEY CANON); she admits she wants the title and needs it to be "a thing seen", not a gift. They agree to timing by Sol. Kael has not asked her once since the bridge ("One" morning counted). Slate shows two columns: the standard 252 and a second column "the hold" at 288, plus "Withdrawal paper signed: one." The awning clerk gives Tam's name and message. Ansa: "Tell the Auditor...". Slate boy: Tam put on his good coat. Bram brings Dessa's moved sitting: tomorrow, not the 18th; bring the second peg and the withdrawal stack.
- CONTINUITY SNAG (real): the "hold" column of 288 does not match earlier counts (five held; 252 signed; about 400 total). Kael answers "two hundred and eighty-seven held, one freed". The text never explains what the 288 counts.
- CONTINUITY SNAG: Tam is described as having "held since the fourth" yet he signed the standard on the 10th and is the one who withdrew.
- LINES: Sol: "I want it. I want it timed by me." Kael: "That isn't the same as doing it."

## CH.38 | 16 Cinderveil | POV Kael
- WHAT: Commission, benches added. Dessa's reading of the pegs, custody, the third lock (see KEY CANON). An older surveyor (broad, cane) recalls talk forty years ago of a second line under the boiling house that never reached the plate. Sitting adjourned to the 18th. Corren: elders want Kael tonight, father's rooms, no clerk of record. On the stair Sol straightens his collar and kisses him. Ferra and the cousin: Thorne's two visits; do not petition early or at all. Kael tells them the truth. At Ansa's: slate now "two hundred and eighty-six still holding", one more withdrew, "Tam's is holding". Sol on "tender": a soft lay takes strain along the whole length. Unanswered: what Thorne offered Ashworth.
- OPEN: The content of Thorne's offer to Ashworth.

## CH.39 | 17 Cinderveil | POV Sol
- WHAT: Caster's guild runner brings the smith list (see KEY CANON). Sol and Kael discuss it; Kael shows her the four-line ledger page (three lines finished). At Ansa's with Joren (coat on), Ansa (always lays a fourth cup now): 286 steady, no new withdrawal since the day before; a woman asked what happens to a man after he withdraws; Ansa: Tam is "held a little shorter on coin". Healer says Mara's colour better. Renn: thirteen days. Tam's news about Thorne at Ashworth's door.
- CONTINUITY SNAG: Sol and Kael both say "thirteen days" to the 28th; from the 17th it is eleven. Thirteen is the count of days Renn has been gone.
- CONTINUITY NOTE: ch.38 says names due by the 18th; ch.39 delivers them on the 17th, "a day early".
