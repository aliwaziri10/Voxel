Fact lookup from the live chapter files (main), written 2026-10-03. Not new canon. If a chapter disagrees, the chapter wins; fix this file. Chapter SHAs read: ch.40 c184f737, ch.41 5ebaf5ab, ch.42 8179b64f, ch.43 3dd464a0, ch.44 321584a9.

## KEY CANON IN THIS BATCH
- THE VOTE ON 28 CINDERVEIL, IN THE ORDER IT HAPPENS (ch.42-43). The clerk's count of houses set aside: ONE Ashworth (the eldest elder, voices it; Ferra reads the house's line: "The house stands behind the finding of its Auditor and answers for his error as its own, the error having been made in the house's name."). TWO the lord of the sixth chair, unnamed, who never signed and never explained why: "Aside". THREE Vane (Sol speaks from the officers' gallery as "Isolde Vane, last of her house": "House Vane sets the instrument aside. It would like the sum divided."). FOUR Lenmoor (withdraws its name and sets aside). Then, held: Estler (chair empty, "Not present", called three times), Farrow (junior cousin holds a sealed letter), Pryce (asks to lodge last of the seated houses), Corvane ("holds its voice"), Thorne (answers at the close). In ch.43: FIVE Pryce, SIX Corvane, Estler absent a third time, SEVEN Farrow (letter read aloud, withdraws its signature), EIGHT Dellyn, NINE Tavarel. Thorne then concedes. Dellyn and Tavarel are named for the first time in ch.43.
- RESULT (ch.43): the Chancellor: "The instrument is set aside by the voice of nine of the twelve houses. The Ward Deed of Succession is void as an instrument of transfer. Its authenticity as a document is not in question." Thorne: accepts the voice of the Twelve, withdraws the petition, and "The tap at Anchor Seven remains Thorne's charge to close".
- KAEL'S FINDING (ch.41), three parts: (1) the tap, tapped and metered three years, Thorne knot on the lock plate, "the light was a kindness and the lock was a crime"; survey iron stands in for a Thorne ingot, loses weight in wet air, Renn's sum brings the plate to surge mark before the month is out; the order to shut the tap is sealed for the Council surveyor alone and he has been missing since the first week of Cinderveil. (2) the instrument: clause seven passes every Schedule contract to the house administering reform; clause twelve makes that house's reading final and bars review; the Accord's transfer article allows only holder's consent or the sitting auditor's certification; Kael declines to certify and finds no named holder was asked; enters that the four non-signing houses were first counted in that room by Isolde Vane on 28 Sunspire. He enters one error of his own: the thirty days began with his care in opening the box before the Council. (3) the pegs: two brass pegs from one mold; the second "placed"; a second ward-line under the cellar on no plate; an older surveyor's forty-year-old talk of it; one smith left who can cast to that tolerance, given to the commission only. "The finding ... is entered without the leave of my house."
- ALDOUS SPEAKS (ch.41-42): stands as the steward's hand reaches his sleeve and sets it back in her lap. Says the witness's name on the Lower Spine leaf beside the Vane line is his; he never witnessed any Schedule contract; he did not write or sign it; he let it stand a month because he was told "an heir does not contradict his house's paper in public". Senior clerk compares hands: Aldous's letters lean right and the tail of his last letter stays open; the witness hand is upright and the tail closes; "a practised hand, and it has been careful to look younger than it is"; no date on the line; "Enter it as found."
- THE SMITH IS NAMED (ch.40): CORWIN. Guild master (heavy, burn scars up both forearms) clears the other three (dead eight years and more; woman three hundred miles east by the guild ledger; the boy never cast alone to that tolerance). Corwin cast the Thorne die eight years ago, nothing for Thorne since; still working, in plain sight, in his own shop. Dessa: "A shared mold and an old commission is a thread, Auditor. It isn't a rope." She will have him asked gently; asked, he answers everything and offers nothing.
- FOURTH LINE FINISHED (ch.40, evening 27th, at Ansa's table): "Mine to answer for, whatever the reading finds." Sol: "That's not about the petition." She keeps the page in her hand, does not refold it.
- FARROW'S LETTER (ch.43): Lord Farrow could not sit "and watch his own signature used against a girl who buried her mother under this house's silence"; says his name was added to a witness line he never wrote, on a leaf he never saw; does not believe Aldous wrote it; withdraws. POSTSCRIPT, smaller and shakier hand, left unfolded on the table, unread aloud: "Ask the boy who taught him his letters."
- TAP CLOSED ON ELEVEN (ch.45 preview, set up in ch.44): Thorne files notice to send its own crew to close Anchor Seven at first light on the 30th, no Accord escort (ch.44); the Council grants escort (Sol's order).
- COUNTS: hold column 286 through the 25th, 282 on the 26th (four holders within an hour); standard stands at 252. Ten days from the 18th to the 28th.
- ROMANCE STATE: ch.43 on the gallery stair Kael says he reached for the old shape (deciding alone) and put it down. Ch.44 night of the 29th: the sill scene, kept by Zia's decision; Sol: "I burned mine a long time ago" (the ledger). Kael writes a private fourth PAGE (not the fourth line): "the audit stands or falls on both our names now".

## CH.40 | 28 Cinderveil (spans 18th-28th) | POV Kael
- WHO: Dessa (coat buttoned, seat nearest the forge); the guild master; Sol; Kael; Ansa, Joren; the Chancellor (old woman, unadorned grey, wears no seal); Malrik Thorne (silver clasp, upright without support); the steward (small, grey); Auditor's gallery.
- WHAT: Guild room, the four names, Corwin. Ten days: finding written and cut; Renn not found; the 22nd search widens to the lower forges; the 24th a fisherman thinks he saw a man like Renn on a skiff heading south on the coast road "a full week and more past"; Mara's colour better, eyes seeing nothing. 27th, Kael finishes the fourth line. Morning of the 28th: grey, cloud on the spires; chamber full; eight chairs filled, four empty "by long custom"; Chancellor opens: thirty days lapse at midnight unless nine of twelve set aside; no house may voice until the Auditor has read his finding. Kael steps down alone; Sol stays in the gallery by agreement.
- OBJECTS AND COUNTS: two pegs in a cloth; Dessa's sheet with four names; four-line list in the ledger; 286 then 282.
- LINES: Kael: "Twelve houses in the chamber. The Deed stands unless nine set it aside. Eight signed Thorne's petition and four never did, so five of the eight would have to turn."
- CONTINUITY NOTE: Kael lists the four doubtful houses as Estler, Farrow, Vane and "the fourth" who never signed; the fourth is never identified.

## CH.41 | 28 Cinderveil | POV Sol
- WHAT: Sol counts chairs (eight filled, four not); Kael reads the three parts (see KEY CANON). Tam stands at the back wall between Ansa and Bram. Sol thinks of being "first" to sign the new standard; Tam's withdrawal took that certainty loose. Kael enters his own error. Farrow's cousin writes three lines on a scrap and sends a page out. Sol gives Kael a nod "exactly the size she would have given a colleague". The Chancellor: "The clerks will record the hour"; one afternoon and evening, five houses to turn. Malrik Thorne: "It is either very foolish or very deliberate, and I find I cannot tell which." Kael: "Then the record will hold both readings, my lord, until a reader can." Aldous rises: "I ask leave to speak to the finding."
- OBJECTS AND COUNTS: needles in Kael's dead leg when he stands still; Sol's own left leg goes to sleep.
- CONTINUITY SNAG (repeat of ch.36): Kael's finding says "Isolde Vane found the first [peg] in the outer chamber on the day the first ward-line was mapped". Ch.29 has Sol take it from the ledge below the Tar Lane forge; the twin is on the table past the iron door (ch.32).
- CONTINUITY SNAG (soft): ch.41 says the finding names a smith by guild's book; Kael says he gives the name to the commission only, yet ch.40 shows Dessa already holds it.

## CH.42 | 28 Cinderveil | POV Kael
- WHAT: Chamber turns toward the Thorne chair. Aldous speaks (see KEY CANON). Clerk's hand comparison. Kael feels the pull to stand at the boy's shoulder and keeps both feet where they are. Malrik: "The house will answer its grandson in its turn." Chancellor orders voices lodged in order of the seats from the dais's right hand; "The midnight bell will not wait for the slow." Votes ONE to FOUR, then holds (see KEY CANON). The Farrow cousin brings the sealed letter; Chancellor breaks the seal, reads the first leaf, and her face does "a thing" Kael has not seen in three years; Malrik turns and looks at her. She holds the letter until Farrow is called. Malrik defers: "Neither is a verdict."
- OBJECTS AND COUNTS: "Four voices lodged of the nine needed." Four empty seats that "held only stone". Estler's chair empty. Kael's sum: "not enough, not yet."
- CONTINUITY SNAG: the sixth-chair house is never named and the twelfth house never appears; with Dellyn and Tavarel arriving only in ch.43, the roll shows ten named houses. The order in the Book 2 HANDOFF omits the sixth-chair "Aside" between Ashworth and Vane.
- CONTINUITY NOTE (for Book 3 and Book 1): forged witness hand upright, tail closed; Aldous's leans right, tail open; Book 1 ch.45 carried hand "leans to the right" (per Book 3 HANDOFF, commit 42bba69). Check Book 1 against this.

## CH.43 | 28 Cinderveil | POV Sol, then Kael (switches inside the chapter)
- WHAT: Pryce, Corvane, Farrow (letter), Dellyn, Tavarel as in KEY CANON; "Nine." Chancellor's ruling; Malrik concedes; Aldous crosses the ring alone and his grandfather puts a hand on his shoulder. Ferra to Kael: the family account is due "by the first bell of the thirtieth. Not for the Council. For the family ledger." "Your grandfather's father lost a vote like this once. He wrote nothing down." Elder's message: proud, will not say so again. Stair talk: Sol makes Kael agree to tell Joren the tally first, then the steward that the petition may be drafted, then tell her aloud. Lenmoor first, then Pryce.
- LINES: Sol: "Call it the first day in a month nobody died of it." Kael: "Turns out simple and right stopped living in the same house around the time I met you."
- CONTINUITY SNAG (soft): Farrow's letter says his name was added to "a witness line"; ch.42 identifies only Aldous's name as the forged witness name.
- CONTINUITY SNAG (soft): ch.42 says the Chancellor read "the first leaf"; ch.43 calls it "the single leaf" and says the postscript was never read aloud, but she read it silently first, so she has seen it.
- CONTINUITY SNAG (soft): ch.43 opens Sol's POV and moves into Kael's thoughts ("Kael had spent the wait watching hands").

## CH.44 | 29 Cinderveil | POV Kael
- WHO: Kael; Sol; Ferra; Joren and Corren (told by name); Auda Ashworth (grandmother, in the Ashworth receiving room at the ninth hour); a clerk in the corner.
- WHAT: Kael woke before the bells; family account written, three pages. Ferra brings Thorne's notice. Sol and Kael agree the same view and the same rule (say it aloud to Joren first; Sol drafts the escort request, citing the finding). Joren: "About time a Council vote went the way the ropewalk floor did." Escort granted. Ansa's lamp in the ropewalk gallery has been dark since the hearing, "first time in years nobody's fed it". Auda's counter-offer (Ashworth seal alone on the final warrants) is refused with reasons; Auda withdraws it. Sol asks about the postscript; Kael has not told Auda. They resolve: the tap first, then Aldous's tutor, a private word with Farrow before the Council. Night: the private fourth page; sill scene.
- OBJECTS AND COUNTS: petition folded in quarters in Kael's coat, "waited two weeks"; "Forty households. Fifty. Ansa's roof is on that line"; Ansa's roof is "the fourth one from the corner"; Renn's old survey under four ink-pots.
- LINES: Sol: "I'm not asking you. I'm telling you what I think, and then I want to hear what you think, because that's the whole arrangement." Auda: "I'd rather be wrong quickly and say so than be right slowly and lose you over it." Kael: "I'm not keeping a ledger on it."
- CONTINUITY SNAG (real): Auda Ashworth and "grandmother" appear for the first time in ch.44 (then ch.45). Nothing in ch.1-43 names a living grandmother. In ch.41-43 the Ashworth voice is the eldest elder (male, cane) and Ferra is the house's steward; ch.44 opens "Your grandmother's steward sent word", which is Ferra by every other chapter.
- CONTINUITY SNAG (soft): Auda says "Six weeks" and Kael says it again in ch.45; 28 Sunspire to 30 Cinderveil is thirty-two days.
