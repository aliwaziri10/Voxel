# DIGEST - Kindling Line Book 2, ch.30-34

Fact lookup from the live chapter files (main), written 2026-10-03. Not new canon. If a chapter disagrees, the chapter wins; fix this file.

## KEY CANON IN THIS BATCH
- THE STANDARD WAS READ on 9 Cinderveil at the Thorne house, seventh bell of the evening (ch.31). Terms: wage from 1 Cinderveil, lamps kept at the house's cost, term "until dissolution of the trade or death of the bearer", a dependents line that names no one and leaves room for anyone, last line "Consent of the holder, given freely, upon the standard as set." The leaving paper is "a courtesy of the house", beside the standard and not within it, and was not read.
- THE HOLD was lifted on the five holders at that reading; wages resume that night. Tam signed the new standard on the morning of the 10th at the awning, alone (count now 252 of about 400, 148 still waiting).
- KAEL'S AND SOL'S ANSWERS (ch.31): Kael: "I raise nothing against its wording. I certify nothing as to its fairness, or as to the manner of its adoption." Sol: signs and certifies nothing. Kael raised once, into both books, that tonight's standard is not the one the 251 signed. The house records "he read it, he did not object" twice (table's book and Chancellor's clerk's book).
- THE RULE (Joren, ch.32): nobody goes past the table in the boiling-house chamber unless the Auditor and Lady Vane both say so aloud in the same breath. Joren later says "Telling isn't asking" and will ask where Sol is before letting Kael past.
- THE PEGS: the ledge peg (ch.29) is with Sol, in her glove. A twin brass tally peg was left squared on the table in the chamber behind the iron door (found 10 Cinderveil, ch.32); Kael carries it wrapped in his handkerchief. Neither is Renn's hand (he chalked words). The commission of surveyors sits 14 Cinderveil and will want both pegs.
- THE QUARREL (ch.33, 11 Cinderveil): Kael went down the second stair alone before first light; Sol: "I don't know if I can keep doing this...you and me, deciding...whether the other one gets to be afraid out loud." Unresolved at ch.34. They love each other, share the bed, do not touch; Sol's hand stops an inch short of his on the wall (ch.34).
- KAEL'S FATHER: buried nine months ago (ch.33); his saying: "a man tests his own floor before he lets his household stand on it." CHECK against Book 1 and Book 3 canon.
- THE BOILING-HOUSE STAIR: water rising about a hand's width a night on the third step (ch.33-34). Posted closed, Council seal on the hasp, Joren's watch (wardens Corren and one other). Renn's chalk at the second stair's mouth: "THIS ONE HAS NO BOTTOM I HAVE FOUND." Water reached the last word on the 12th.
- UNNAMED MAN in plain grey, dry boots (the Reach was wet to the ankle), sits silent at the Thorne table on the 9th. Afterwards a lamp goes out in the house and a shape stands in a window.
- MARA: no change throughout; cold at the wrists since the 4th. Kael talks to her daily.
- COUNTS: 251 held steady from the 3rd to the 9th; 252 on the 10th.

## CH.30 | 8 Cinderveil | POV Kael
- WHO: Kael, Sol, senior clerk (ink to wrist), Bram, healer, Mara, Joren, Ansa's boy, a boy in violet livery.
- WHAT: On the 7th at second bell Kael gives the senior clerk a statement (below the forge from the morning of the 6th to first grey on the 7th, by choice) and reports Renn missing: book six drawn at ninth bell on the evening of the 4th, chalk tally of thirty-nine rounds. Clerk enters "A place beneath the old forge on Tar Lane, reported by the Auditor, not surveyed." Commission of surveyors sits the 14th. Sol keeps the peg and will give it only to the commission. Kael tells Mara aloud. That night Kael and Sol set terms for the card: Joren at the door, a clerk of the Chancellor with the Chancellor's book, standard read aloud by the house's clerk, nothing signed, initialled or carried, either may say "not yet" and it stands (Kael adds this one; Sol writes it out). Sol stays on the cot while he sleeps; "Tonight is for sleeping." Slate: 251, five held (not three). First bell on the 8th the house accepts every term and adds that the five held holders attend "as parties to the standard"; the clerk of the table summoned them at fourth bell the night before, before the house had read the terms. The table seats twelve; the card of the 7th named two chairs, the reply asks for eight.
- LINES: Kael to the clerk: "I have a great many to make." Kael: "Either of us may say not yet."

## CH.31 | 9 Cinderveil | POV Sol
- WHO: Sol, Kael, Joren (door), Chancellor's clerk (thin book, seat by the door), steward, Aldous (pale, presses the table edge when the dependents line is read), an ink-stained clerk of the table, the unnamed grey man, Tam and four other holders at the table's foot.
- WHAT: On the stair: "Later." / "Hold me to it." The reading takes about an hour. Kael answers as in KEY CANON; the steward offers to lift the Hold in exchange for that reading standing as the Auditor's answer; Kael says it stands "as given", she lifts the Hold anyway. Sol asks whether the leaving paper is in the standard; steward says it is a courtesy beside it; recorded. Tam, afterwards, asks to sign tonight; his mother was "four years a bearer under the old trade", never allowed to read what she signed; he has read this twice at the awning and once tonight. Kael: not ours to decide, "We carry nothing from this table." Sol sees her own "I will not answer for him" can be a wall. On the stair she says "I want to go home" and asks him not to fill the quiet; he does not. Lamp goes out at the top of the house, a shape in the window.
- OPEN: Who the grey man is; who was in the window.

## CH.32 | 10 Cinderveil | POV Kael
- WHO: Kael, Sol, Mara, healer, Ansa (stool under her window, slate), the younger awning clerk, Joren, Corren and another warden.
- WHAT: Kael tells Mara Tam signed. On the rope bridge Sol steadies his elbow in a gust. Clerk offers tea; Kael declines. Count 252, one new name in seven days. Ansa knew at first light and kept her word to Tam not to tell Sol; Sol says "I would not have stopped him"; Ansa: "Would you not? I do not think you know." Sol: the same fault "in a smaller box". Kael and Sol go to the boiling-house cellar: bar lifted, door unlocked, same nine, eleven, single dark breath; the iron door, shut when the clerks left, now ajar; inside a single table, the stool, and the second brass peg. They send for Joren, who sets the rule. At home they eat standing; each peg on its own person or washstand or coat; Sol turns away in bed; Kael holds his hand an inch above her shoulder and does not touch.
- CONTINUITY SNAG (soft): the awning has stood "ten days"; from 29 Sunspire to 10 Cinderveil is eleven.

## CH.33 | 11 Cinderveil | POV Sol
- WHO: Sol, Kael, Joren, Corren.
- WHAT: Sol wakes to a cold bed. Joren: Kael went down alone, saying he would say the rule "for the both of you". Note under the salt dish: "Second stair. Back soon." (no hour, no K.A.) Corren: Kael marked the water on the third step; up a thumb's width already. Sol finds Kael kneeling at a narrow stair cut into the rock past the iron door, Renn's chalk at its mouth (see KEY CANON). Kael: he went because "if the steps gave way, it should go wrong for one of us and not both"; he calls the reasoning a hierarchy when Sol does; he cites his father's saying and says what he cannot forgive is "the renaming of it" as love. Sol: "Come up when you're done being brave alone." Kael holds out an empty palm; she walks past and does not take it.
- CONTINUITY SNAG (soft): ch.29's ledge below the Tar Lane forge has "a narrow dry stair down to the left of the ring"; ch.33's "second stair" is past the boiling-house iron door. The chapters do not say whether these are the same stair reached from two ends. Renn's chalk differs: "THREE" (ch.29) and "NO BOTTOM" (ch.33).
- LINES: Sol: "That's not a kindness. It's a hierarchy."

## CH.34 | 12 Cinderveil | POV Kael
- WHO: Kael, Sol, Joren, Mara, healer.
- WHAT: Kael chalks his own water mark below Corren's, climbs an hour later. Joren: "Telling isn't asking." Kael tells Mara what he did and that he said "I love you" five days ago. Sol comes home after the lamps; they lie a hand's width apart; she says "I am not going to turn over" and "It is not that I do not want to." At first light she is gone from the bed. The water has risen a hand's width in a night; Joren has posted the stair closed with the Council seal. In the afternoon Kael finds Sol on the low wall opposite the Tar Lane forge (Renn's thirty-nine rounds and the unfinished fortieth stroke, eight days on). They name the unsolved parts (Tam, deciding for each other) without resolving them. Both carry their pegs and keep touching them. Commission sits in two days. Sol's right hand crosses most of the span on the wall and stops an inch from his.
- LINES: Sol: "I did not say I wanted you to be so good at listening." Kael: "I am trying to believe more of them."
