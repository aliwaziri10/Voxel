# DIGEST - Kindling Line Book 2, ch.5-9

Fact lookup written 2026-10-03 from the live chapter files (main). Not a summary and not new canon. If this file and a chapter disagree, the chapter wins; fix this file. Format per chapter: DATE | POV | WHO | WHAT | OBJECTS AND COUNTS | LINES AND BEATS | OPEN.

## CH.5 | 13 Sunspire, Year 4 | POV Sol
- WHO: Sol (at the end of the public rail, where the tier narrows to a shelf); Kael (auditor's chair at the foot of the dais, alone, father's coat cut down to his shoulders); Malrik Thorne (white-haired, on a young man's arm; the young man is Aldous); the Chancellor (a woman, thin voice); the senior clerk (young voice); Ferra (Ashworth steward: tall woman, iron hair, ring of keys at her belt); six Ashworth elders in grey (second tier, right hand); an Estler (stout woman); a Farrow (tall man, brown coat).
- WHAT: Council sets a green-baize table. Malrik files a proposal "to administer the reform of the ward trade under the third part of the Schedule of Dissolution", four heads: continuity of protection, a standard of contract, a record of the houses' service, the authority for the offer. Authority = three succession instruments from the trade years, plus a fourth held under seal: "the Ward Deed of Succession", to be produced at the hearing. Chancellor: the Council has no record of it. Hearing set for the 21st. Petition posted at the Council door the morning of the 15th. Kael says the auditor "has found nothing yet" and will say so. Pen goes round: Thorne, Estler, Farrow sign (three). Ferra shakes her head once for Ashworth. The Vane bench is empty; clerk says "House Vane declines by absence". Ferra tells Sol privately: Ashworth will not sign on the 15th or at the hearing; elders' phrase: Kael "would decide what it meant". Sol had written Kael's words on the back of her hand in ink.
- OBJECTS AND COUNTS: Malrik's flat dark leather case. Malrik signs with a small mark of three strands crossing. Count: 3 signatures (Thorne, Estler, Farrow). Broom marks in the dust of the empty Vane bench.
- LINES: Kael: "I'd rather have been asked." Sol to Kael at the end: "Ask me again." He asks "Together?" and she answers yes.
- OPEN: Where the Deed is. A dotted line "running east off the edge of a sheet of ward-paper" (Sol's thought).

## CH.6 | 14 Sunspire | POV Kael
- WHO: Kael; Ives (gate clerk at Mire Gate, tired woman, ward office over the guard arch); Brack (keeper of the Weir Stair, about sixty, small and wide, palms the colour of old rope, fourth generation of Bracks on the stone; his father died at fifty-one in Thorne's service and Thorne paid the burying); Sol (at the table when Kael returns); Bram (the Council clerk who carries registers; grey Council ribbon).
- WHAT: Kael writes Sol a note (kept as: "I am walking the three lines the petition cites: Mire Gate first, then the east break, then the Weir Stair. Back by dusk."). Mire Gate: forty quarters of stipend receipts, ten years, all paid on the day, in a Thorne steward's even hand. East break: eleven ward-stones across scree above the tannery terraces, Thorne markers cut new last spring, all live. Kael slides on the scree, the gauge case falls forty feet and its glass cracks, the needle stays true. Weir Stair: a hundred wet steps; the steward's letter to Brack is dated the tenth of Sunspire. Sol reads the three instruments: they pass upkeep of a ward line to the house that bore its cost longest and give no authority to direct a reform. Report has two halves. Sol keeps Kael's note in her notebook behind two slips from the Lower Spine.
- OBJECTS AND COUNTS: Cracked gauge (glass cracked, needle true). Eleven east-break stones. Forty quarters. Instruments in three hands from three decades.
- LINES: Kael: "I am unaccustomed to being satisfied." Brack: write both halves down (Thorne kept the water clean; Thorne's wage took his father).
- OPEN: The fourth instrument must carry the authority the three do not.

## CH.7 | 15 Sunspire | POV Sol
- WHO: Sol; Kael; a Thorne house steward (narrow, Thorne grey with violet at the cuffs, forgettable face, bows a finger's width). This is the narrow man. Not the works steward.
- WHAT: Sol writes Kael a note. Her notebook line: "14 Sun. Told before. Not asked. Note kept." The petition hangs at the Council door in a black oak frame: twelve houses named down the left edge in old precedence; signed: Thorne, Estler, Farrow; Ashworth "not signed"; Vane "declines by absence". Needs eight. Count rises 3 to 5 (Lenmoor, Pryce) while she draws. Sol draws the Thorne seal five times. Kael's report goes in at second bell: bears out the three instruments and nothing beyond. Sol asks him to tell her when a route is a scree slope and let her say no; he says "Yes". The narrow steward invites her to hear Lord Thorne once, not in the chamber, without the Auditor, and mentions her mother could not be present.
- OBJECTS AND COUNTS: THE SEAL: near-black wax, disc the size of her palm, three strands plaited into one tight knot; strands end in a drop, a feather, a flame (this order). One strand rubbed with violet leaf, one with copper, one bare. The third strand (flame) pulls tighter, so the seal "leans to flame". A bearer with no name went down a barred stair on the eighth carrying the pass of the house (visitors' book at the Lower Spine stair).
- LINES: Sol: "I will repeat every word of it to the Auditor by supper."
- OPEN: What Thorne wants to say to Sol privately.

## CH.8 | 16 Sunspire | POV Kael
- WHO: Kael; a Thorne courier (young, nineteen at the outside, violet piping); Sol; Bram (Council record desk).
- WHAT: Courier brings a site pass for Anchor Seven, Lower Reach, beneath the old ropewalk, described as "Decommissioned". It admits "the bearer", once, with a warden of the house, on a day named to the house steward the evening before. Sealed the sixteenth. Same die, same lean (Sol checks against her charcoal drawing). Kael stops himself going to the record desk alone and carries the pass to Sol in his hand. Sol: "They have cut a door for each of us." Count: 6 (Corvane); two more carry the petition; hearing five days off. Renn's closures have a year, crew, crew master name and a lead disc the size of a palm on the hatch; Renn's district sheet numbers the ropewalk sites one to six and he redrew "site six". Record desk card: "Anchor Seven. Vault-linked. Ropewalk district, Lower Reach. Closure entry: none. Maintenance flag: open, entered in the last year of the trade, not lifted." Clerks' summary said closed. Pass entered unused in the Council receiving ledger. Pencil tick in the card margin; the desk rule is ink.
- OBJECTS AND COUNTS: The pass (4 lines). Sol's notebook: "16 Sun. Told before. Asked. Pass entered, unused." Senior clerk holds the decommissioning room key and sits at the Chancellor's table through the 17th.
- LINES: Kael: "I lean toward returning it unused. What would you do with it?" Sol: "Ask me again the day I decide to go." (about Thorne's invitation).
- OPEN: Who left the pencil tick; who changed "none" to "closed".

## CH.9 | 17 Sunspire | POV Sol
- WHO: Wenna Varel (about fifty, empty market basket); her husband Tobin Varel (clerk of ward-trade accounts, thirty-one years, missing six weeks; kept his own practice scripts since fourteen and took them with him wrapped in a bread cloth); Bram; Pell (night attendant, archive tower; lank, young, lamp oil on his cuffs); Sol; Kael.
- WHAT: Wenna: Tobin said "a line was drawing which should not be", and that he "put a message where a man who copies wards would look and a man who reads them would not", "for whoever is careful". Sol promises to tell her whatever she learns the same day. Kael tells Sol about the reader at the tower before going; they go together. Count 7. The reader (hooded, cloaked, hand holding the cloak shut) came four nights from the 13th: the three instruments; the deed boxes; the shelf lists; then asked for "a deed of succession" by name and said "it was not here". The reader watches them from three aisles back, then leaves by the servants' stair. Left: a pencil shaving and a hooked pencil tick on the label "Ward Trade, Succession, First Series".
- OBJECTS AND COUNTS: Pencil shaving kept in vellum beside Sol's seal drawing. Hearing four days off.
- LINES: Sol to Kael: "If the house does not have it, what is Malrik Thorne going to lay on the green table on the twenty-first?"
- OPEN: Tobin Varel's whereabouts and message; the hooded reader's identity; whether Thorne holds the Deed.
