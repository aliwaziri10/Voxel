## Corrections the digest has already forced on the Book 3 files (apply to CANON_LOCK.md and BEAT_MAP.md)
1. Sol's full name is ISOLDE VANE (B2 ch.15-17). Canon Lock section 6 still says UNSET.
2. The works steward is on the page in B2 ch.15, 16, 18, 20, 22, 29, 30, 31 (copper clasp, small, upright, past sixty, grey hair pinned flat). She leads Kael and Sol into the Thorne house. She is NOT a new face in Book 3 ch.2.
3. B2 ch.18: Malrik says the shutting order is "set down in one hand, and one person holds the page". B2 ch.20: the order in the master smith's hand was lodged with the Council surveyor (Renn) under seal on 28 Sunspire. Renn has been missing since 4 Cinderveil (ch.28). Book 3 can pay this off.
4. PEGS: Renn's ten brass pegs (ch.19) are a tally board. One of them is the ledge peg Sol takes (ch.29); its twin is left on the table in the chamber behind the boiling-house iron door (ch.32) and Kael carries it. Check at ch.36 whether the "twin brass pegs" there are these same two; this note previously treated them as different.
5. Cycle numbers in B2: eleven breaths natural (ch.14), ten with the survey chain on the plate (ch.15, 19, 21), nine by mid-Cinderveil and eight by end of Cinderveil if nothing is done (ch.19, 20). Book 2 ch.45 ends with "tap closed on eleven". Book 3 must keep these straight.
6. THE SECOND LINE appears in B2 ch.27, under the boiling house: two lines (nine and eleven breaths), halving to ten. The Book 3 plan's "second line under the boiling house" is therefore not new in Book 3. A third slow beat (31 to 34) is heard by Sol only, ch.29.
7. The Deed was opened 28 Sunspire. Clause 19 gives thirty days, ending 28 Cinderveil, and needs nine of twelve houses to set it aside. The Chancellor is a woman. Thorne's awning counts: 31, 69, 143, 214, 251 (held 3rd to 9th), 252 (10 Cinderveil), of about 400. The Hold and the WITHDRAWAL form are Thorne's tools (ch.24-25).
8. Kael's father was buried nine months before ch.33 (B2 ch.33). Check against Book 1 and Book 3 canon.
