## Corrections the digest has already forced on the Book 3 files (apply to CANON_LOCK.md and BEAT_MAP.md)
1. Sol's full name is ISOLDE VANE (B2 ch.15-17). Canon Lock section 6 still says UNSET.
2. The works steward is on the page in B2 ch.15, 16, 18 (copper clasp, small, upright, past sixty, grey hair pinned flat). She also leads Kael and Sol into the Thorne house. The Book 3 plan treats her as first connected to the carried hand in Book 3; that is consistent, but she is NOT a new face in Book 3 ch.2. She is also on the page in ch.20 (behind the Thorne bench, hand on Aldous's sleeve) and ch.22 (takes the joint note at the Thorne stair).
3. Book 2 ch.18: Malrik says the shutting order is "set down in one hand, and one person holds the page". Book 3 can pay this off (who holds the page). Book 2 ch.20 adds: the order in the master smith's hand was lodged on the morning of 28 Sunspire with the Council surveyor (Renn) under seal; no one has read it on the page by ch.24.
4. Renn's ten brass pegs (ch.19) are a tally board. The twin brass pegs of B2 ch.36 are different. Do not conflate them in Book 3.
5. Cycle numbers in B2: eleven breaths natural (ch.14), ten with the survey chain on the plate (ch.15, 19, 21), nine by mid-Cinderveil and eight by end of Cinderveil if nothing is done (ch.19, 20). Book 2 ch.45 ends with "tap closed on eleven". Book 3 must keep these straight.

## Continuity snags found so far (do not fix without Zia; they are Book 2 finished text)
1. ch.13: Kael says the tap was kept "for three years" but the other hand's entries date from Year One.
2. ch.14: "Five days ago the Deed had been a sentence in a docket" (the Deed was named on 13 Sunspire; this is the 22nd).
3. ch.12: Ansa lives in the room OVER the tar-shop. Book 3 batch 1 ch.3 puts Ansa's kitchen BELOW the tar-shop. Reconcile in Book 3.
4. ch.17: the contract lists Sol at age 8; Sol was 12 at her mother's Reckoning. Fine if the binding was four years earlier; Book 3 should not state the binding year.
5. ch.20 (soft): Renn says he "may not read" the brass case until the Council says; Thorne's filing, as read out, says the order is "to be read by him alone". The chapter never names the brass case as the shutting order.
6. ch.20 (soft): the Schedule lists eleven houses, but four non-signers are said to be on it, with twelve houses on the Council and eight signers. The numbers do not reconcile unless one signer is off the Schedule.
7. ch.20-24 (soft): bell numbering is not a single clock (ninth bell in the morning, fourth bell of the morning, second bell of the afternoon). Book 3 should not state a bell as an hour.
