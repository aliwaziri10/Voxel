# DIGEST - Kindling Line Book 2, ch.25-29

Fact lookup from the live chapter files (main), written 2026-10-03. Not new canon. If a chapter disagrees, the chapter wins; fix this file.

## KEY CANON IN THIS BATCH
- THE SECOND LINE IS ON THE PAGE in B2 ch.27, under the boiling house cellar: two lines, one cycle of nine and one of eleven, out of step, averaging ten (Kael: "Laid end to end they make twenty. Halved, ten."). The clerks cross where both go dark together, which is rare. Earlier digests said the second line had not appeared; it has.
- A THIRD THING, ch.29: at the ledge below Tar Lane forge, Sol hears a slow beat in the air at head height, right of the iron ring, no drop, only a long lean and return. Counts: 31, 34, 31. Kael cannot hear it. Renn's chalk says "THREE".
- RENN IS MISSING from the evening of 4 Cinderveil (drew survey book six at ninth bell of the evening, never returned it). His chalk and one brass tally peg are on the ledge. Sol takes the peg into her glove lining and says so aloud.
- MARA is hurt in ch.26 and still unresponsive on the 7th: eyes open, does not follow light, breathes alone, cold hands. Healer will not promise recovery.
- KAEL'S LEFT LEG: dead below the hip on the 4th; needles back to the knee by the 6th; he uses a stick through ch.29.
- ROMANCE: ch.28 (6 Cinderveil, in the dead forge on Tar Lane) Kael says "I love you" for the first time; Sol says it back; first kiss. ch.29: "Not here." / "Later."
- THE HOLD: Thorne's tool for anyone who asks to read the terms. Table's book logs each request with the hour. Three held on the 3rd, two more on the 5th.
- THE BOOK moves: the table's book and a flat leather case go from the cellar behind the iron door to the Thorne house on the noon bell of the 5th. The smiths' stair runs boiling house cellar to Tar Lane forge "and on below it" (Renn's chalk, book six leaf 14, struck through in the second ink).
- THE STANDARD will be shown: card from the steward, sealed near-black wax, to be laid before Kael and Sol together at the Thorne house, seventh bell of the evening, 9 Cinderveil, read at the table, not carried out. Inside line: "neither come alone".
- SIGNATURE COUNTS (Ansa's slate): 143 (1st) then 214 by the lamps of the 2nd, 71 that day; 251 by the 3rd, 37 that day; no new on the 4th or 5th. Nobody signs the withdrawal either.

## CH.25 | 3 Cinderveil | POV Sol
- WHO: Sol, Kael, Ansa (rooms over the tar-shop), Ansa's boy, Tam.
- WHAT: Tam's slate: "Tell the Auditor thank you for the no. I am keeping my name where it is. T." Sol writes her first non-name line since the linen: "Received, and kept where it is." (letters now the size of a bright child of eight). Kael plans the reading room (Bram at the wall, Mara at the door) and tells Sol where he will be. Sol at Ansa's window to watch the awning. Tam arrives with a Notice of Hold: "The engagement of the holder is held while the holder's consultation with an officer of the Accord upon the engagement is reviewed. No wage will be paid to the holder from the third of Cinderveil. The house will advise." Two others held with him, the two who asked at dawn on the 1st. Sol writes the receipt on the back ("Received of Tam, one Notice of Hold. 3 Cinderveil, second bell...I.V.") and asks what he wants: "Written down. So it isn't only mine." Tam did not tell anyone he was climbing the spire; the clerk knew his exact words anyway; Ansa told nobody. At noon bell the boy reports the three held are exactly the three written in the table's book on the morning of the 1st as asking to read the standard.
- COUNTS: 214, 71, 3 held. 400 total, 186 to go; Sol reckons the 5th or 6th at 70 a day.
- LINES: Ansa: "Don't carry his boots." Ansa: "You'll have rope back by the fifth."
- OPEN: How the house hears what is said on the spire stair. Sol's unspoken word "First" returns.

## CH.26 | 4 Cinderveil | POV Kael
- WHO: Kael, Sol, Tam, Ansa, Renn (by strip), senior clerk, Mara, Joren, a boy in the healer's grey.
- WHAT: Night of the 3rd Kael copies Tam's Notice into the Auditor's book: "Seen, and copied. K. Ashworth." First bell on the 4th: slate shows 251, 37 that day, clerks carried a book and a bundle down the boiling-house yard at lamp-lighting, a carter hired for noon. Renn's strip: book four of nine shows the boiling house with a cellar and a smiths' stair, struck through in a second ink, no level named; "Do not go looking. R." Senior clerk: the table's book is the petitioner's paper until ratification, filed notice to carry it up to the Thorne house at noon bell; Council office may object, motion to compel needs a sponsoring house and a second, heard at the last-week sitting. Sol is held by the healer till noon. Plan agreed: noon in the yard together, Mara and Joren at the gate. Kael goes alone anyway, leaves a note against the teapot ("Gone to look at the boiling-house cellar. Mara at the yard gate. Back before noon, and I will tell you all of it. K."), orders Mara to hold the gate. Cellar: new oak door, oiled hasp, three-ring lock plate with copper and violet knot; twelve dry steps; room the length of a chapel, floor under two fingers of black water, thin blue seam; ward-lamp over an iron door with an enamelled knot. Kael counts nine, eleven, nine, steps on what he takes for the drop. The swell: left leg gone below the hip. He calls "Mara!"; she runs in and is taken. Stylus scratches behind the iron door, then a knuckle raps twice from inside.
- LINES: Sol: "Fear is cheaper than a hold." Kael's own total: "I have decided what she can stand on."
- SNAG (soft): the awning clerk is "younger, ink on his cuff"; ch.27's clerk of the table is "small and dry". Not stated whether they are the same person.

## CH.27 | 5 Cinderveil | POV Sol
- WHO: Sol, Kael, Mara (cot, healer's back room), the healer, Bram (brings the notice), Ansa's boy, Joren (gate, white scar on back of hand), a warden, the clerk of the table (small, dry), a second clerk, both in grey edged violet.
- WHAT: Kael told Sol everything on the healer's step at half past noon on the 4th. Petitioner's notice defers the book to noon bell of the 5th "owing to an incident...an officer of the Accord and a warden of House Ashworth were injured". Sol: "It's the total I cannot sign." She asks him to stand on her left and say a thing "before I have said mine." Healer returns her knife in a linen bundle. Rope bridge: Kael asks for her arm. Yard: cellar door barred by Joren. Dray with tarpaulin at noon bell. Sol, since the office cannot stop it: "Lift the bar. Nobody goes past the arch without us watching." She counts nine, eleven, nine and finds the two lines; Kael says "Halved, ten" before she does. Clerks cross on the dark, take the oilcloth-wrapped book and a flat leather case. Clerk: "The house keeps its own count, my lady." No strip from Renn since the 4th.
- LINES: Sol on the new rule: "Then when you see a thing, say it before I have said mine."
- OPEN: Who taught Thorne's clerks the crossing; Renn.

## CH.28 | 6 Cinderveil | POV Kael
- WHO: Kael, Sol, Joren, Mara (seen in the healer's room), Renn (absent).
- WHAT: The 5th is spent finding Renn: register shows book six drawn at ninth bell of the evening on the 4th; hatch log shows no surveyor down since the chain went on the scale; commission rooms shut till second bell. Sol sets her slate face down, puts her gloved hand on Kael's knee (the needles ease). Slate: 251, two more asked to read on the 5th and were held by noon, and a man with a lantern and a flat case went down Tar Lane at the tenth bell of the night of the 4th into the old forge. Strip to Bram: "The Auditor and Isolde Vane to the old forge on Tar Lane. Joren with us. Back by the fifth bell of the afternoon. K.A." Forge at the blind end of Tar Lane, new oak door, oiled hasp, rotted bellows, anvil, stair down through a cleared gap, snuffed lantern half full of oil. Chalk: "BOOK SIX, LEAF 14. THE SMITHS' STAIR RUNS FROM THE BOILING HOUSE CELLAR TO THIS FORGE AND ON BELOW IT, STRUCK THROUGH IN THE SECOND INK. I HAVE GONE DOWN TO SEE HOW FAR BEFORE I SAY SO TO ANYONE. THREE, NOT TWO. DO NOT CROSS WITHOUT THE COUNT. R." Kael says "I love you" (said the first time as the only thing, no forgiveness bundled in); Sol: "I love you. I have for longer than I have let myself write down." Kiss. Sol ties the rope to the anvil horn. Kael kisses the heel of her right hand. Below: seam, nine, eleven, nine. Both say "Two."
- LINES: Sol: "It is mine again. I have not decided whether I am glad." (her hands)

## CH.29 | 7 Cinderveil | POV Sol
- WHO: Sol, Kael, Joren, Bram (by note), the works steward (copper clasp, grey hair pinned flat), two retainers in violet wool with copper-tipped staves.
- WHAT: They wait below the forge for the next run where both lines go dark for eight breaths. Missed the fifth bell of afternoon; second strip: "...still below the old forge on Tar Lane and will cross at the next dark. We are together. K.A." Bram's reply: "Entered at the fifth bell...Not back. The senior clerk reads the day's entries at the second bell of the morning. B." Sol ties Kael in with a bowline (two tries); eight-breath crossing, twelve paces; rope made fast to an iron ring. Ledge: dry shelf a body long and half as wide, thirty-nine chalk ticks (last group four), chalk "THREE. IT IS NOT IN THE WATER. I HEAR IT FROM THIS LEDGE AND HAVE NOT YET FOUND WHERE. R.", a brass tally peg on its end. Narrow dry stair to the left of the ring. Kael declines to go down ("Thank you for the notice."). Three-hour wait, Kael warms her hands in his coat; "Not here." "Later." Dawn: Joren and two retainers at the lane mouth; steward says the rope is untouched and "asks nothing about what was found" (she glances at Sol's glove, where the peg is). Steward gives the card (see KEY CANON). Joren sits down on the forge step: "Twenty years."
- LINES: Steward: "The house has learned what you two ask." Sol: if the house has drawn the standard only now, it lied at the awning; if not, it has decided to break its own rule for them.
- SNAG (soft): ch.19 digest calls Renn's ten pegs a tally board; this ledge peg is one of those (ring turned to the wall). Do not confuse with the twin brass pegs of ch.36.
