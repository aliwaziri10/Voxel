# DIGEST - Kindling Line Book 2, ch.15-19

Fact lookup written 2026-10-03 from the live chapter files (main). Not a summary and not new canon. If this file and a chapter disagree, the chapter wins; fix this file.

## KEY CANON FOUND IN THIS BATCH (use these)
- SOL'S FULL NAME IS ISOLDE VANE (ch.15, 16, 17; initials I.V.). Not UNSET any more.
- THE WORKS STEWARD IS ON THE PAGE FROM CH.15: "a small, upright woman past sixty, her grey hair pinned flat, a polished copper clasp at her throat: three strands, knotted". She says "I am the steward of House Thorne's works in the Lower Reach." She gives no name ("You do not need my name"). She is on the Thorne bench in ch.16 and leads Kael and Sol into the house in ch.18. She is NOT the narrow house steward of ch.7.
- The hooded watcher on the rope bridge (ch.9, 11, 16) wears the same copper clasp. Book 2 never says outright that the watcher is her.
- The second line has not appeared yet. The "tap" is on the Vault's trunk line under the ropewalk gallery in the Lower Reach (Anchor Seven). Do not confuse it with the Book 3 "second line under the boiling house".

## CH.15 | 23 Sunspire | POV Sol
- WHO: Sol; Kael; Renn; Varel; three retainers in violet wool with copper-tipped staves; the works steward.
- WHAT: In the dark of the gallery, the steward says the box "is not a prize. It is a weight." The pedestal is a scale: the line reads weight; the room has been "balanced for three years". If the box is carried up, the tap surges into the Lower Reach. She argues Thorne served as wards for three generations and "paid in years and children". The swell comes faster (eleven breaths becomes nine, then eight). Varel recalls Thorne set an iron ingot on the plate twice: twelve pounds. Renn's survey chain (a hundred links, ten pins, a plumb) is about twelve pounds. Sol and Kael haul it across on the drop. On the fifth pull the swell comes early; the charge goes through Sol's wet rope into her palms (welts from heel to fingers); Kael takes the rope, Sol calls the count. The chain on the plate settles the cycle at ten, not eleven. Kael and Sol call "Joren!" and "Mara!" together (the wardens come); the steward lets them pass. Renn seals the box lid with a Council wax strip: "Twenty-three Sunspire. First bell. Unbroken."
- OBJECTS AND COUNTS: the Deed box. Chain: a hundred links, ten pins, a plumb. Cycle now ten.
- LINES: Steward: "A house that has already paid the cost has earned the right to control who pays it next." Sol, of her mother: a ward-line "hasn't decided who owes it yet". Kael: "I hate that it was you." Sol: "I know. Thank you for saying it."
- OPEN: What the 3-year charge is for.

## CH.16 | 24 Sunspire | POV Kael
- WHO: Chancellor (thin old voice); Malrik Thorne (deep grey, silver clasp, white hair combed flat) on his grandson's arm; the works steward behind the Thorne bench; Sol (hands in fresh linen, grey healer's salve); Cormac Vrell (named at the gate, ch.17).
- WHAT: Kael tells the Council: no report, a finding: the Vault's trunk line tapped three years and metered; ledger in two hands (Varel's and an older one); every page copied and stamped by Renn. Malrik: Thorne kept the Lower Reach lit three years, "does not apologize for lamps", asks the Chancellor to open the box. Sol, as "Isolde Vane, accredited ward-taker, joint petitioner", adds that a Council chain is balancing the line, not Thorne's iron. Chancellor: the vote is held; the box is to be opened in the chamber before the full Council at ninth bell on the 28th; sublevel stays under Council guard; Ashworth wardens keep the floor. At home Kael had visited the Thorne Precedent Archive on the 23rd without telling Sol (pass: lower gallery, western face, bearer Kael Ashworth). Sol: "I'm asking you to stop deciding what I don't need to know." Kael saw a Vane spine on the fourth tier and did not open it. New rule: Kael shows Sol any pass before using it. A hooded figure with a copper clasp watches their window from the rope bridge.
- OPEN: Why Thorne opened its archive.

## CH.17 | 25 Sunspire | POV Sol
- WHO: Cormac Vrell (keeper of the Precedent Archive: narrow, pale, dark hair drawn back, grey scholar's robes with violet piping, small square hand); six retainers in violet on the stair of the western face; Sol; Kael.
- WHAT: The archive is one long vaulted chamber cut into the rock with tiered shelves and a window over the Reach floor. Thorne's ward-service ledgers, three generations, by name and year (a red-brown dot beside each death). Malrik asks through Vrell that she read it all before the box is opened. Sol: she will read every page and oppose him anyway. Her mother's contract (cracked green leather, "Ward-taker, House Vane"): term "until dissolution of the trade or death of the bearer"; schedule "Dependents: Isolde, daughter, age 8" in her mother's forward-leaning hand; counter-signature in "a steward's hand, flat and firm: Received for the works of the Lower Spine, House Thorne", with the knot. Sol asks for a copy under the archive's seal (two pages, delivered by noon the next day). Sol can curl her fingers to the second knuckle by ch.18. Supper invitation: seventh bell, tomorrow, "He asks that neither come alone."
- LINES: Vrell: "The house is watching everyone, Lady Vane. It learned the habit from being watched." Kael and Sol: "From here. Together. Before."
- CONTINUITY NOTE: the contract lists Sol at age 8; Sol was 12 at her mother's Reckoning (see Book 2 HANDOFF canon), so the binding was about four years before the Reckoning.

## CH.18 | 26 Sunspire | POV Kael
- WHO: Malrik Thorne (smaller than Kael remembered); Aldous (about twenty-five, his grandfather's height, none of his ease); the works steward (leads them in, stands behind Malrik's chair); Sol; Kael.
- WHAT: Thorne house at the top of the Reach: wide shallow stair, a lamp under violet glass at every fourth step, dark panelling, copper lamp brackets, no portraits, round table for four, one wall all window over the Reach. Sol's place has no cutlery: a two-handled broth cup, torn bread, cheese cut in squares, a halved cored pear. Malrik makes his own oak-gall-and-rust ink and touches each name in the archive each season, taking four days. Offer: Kael is to say publicly that Thorne "kept the Lower Reach lit for three years, at its own cost, in good faith and in the public interest, and stands ready to make the trunk line safe". There is an order for shutting the tap safely; Thorne smiths took three years to learn it "and set it down in one hand, and one person holds the page". Answer due by sixth bell on the 27th. Malrik admits "I would lock it again" (of Varel). Sol: Malrik says things twice in different words "until they sound like law".
- OPEN: Who holds the page with the shutting order.

## CH.19 | 27 Sunspire | POV Sol
- WHO: Sol; Kael; Renn (at a trestle in the hatch office with a ledger and a board of ten brass pegs); Joren; Mara.
- WHAT: They argue all night. Renn's counts: cycle ten, steady since the chain; the chain loses an ounce or two a week; the cycle falls to nine by the middle of Cinderveil and to eight by the end; at eight the line surges. Sol grips a peg on Renn's board for a count of four (first use of her hand). The reply to Malrik, written on the back leaf of Renn's ledger, says the auditor will REPORT what the house offered, in the house's words, and will not certify good faith or stewardship; asks that the shutting order be lodged under seal with the Council surveyor before the box is opened. Mara witnesses Sol's assent: "Mara, warden, twenty years' service, witnessing." Reply goes out an hour before the sixth bell. At the change of guard Renn counts 101 links: a bright copper link with a three-strand knot lies between the ninth and tenth pins. Hatch seals are whole. So there is another way into the gallery, off Renn's survey, known to Thorne.
- OBJECTS AND COUNTS: Renn's ten brass pegs (a tally board, NOT the twin ward-line pegs of ch.36). Chain 100 links plus the copper 101st.
- OPEN: The other way into the gallery.
