# DIGEST - Kindling Line Book 2, ch.1-4 and ch.45

Fact lookup from the live chapter files (main), written 2026-10-03. Not new canon. If a chapter disagrees, the chapter wins; fix this file. Chapter SHAs read: ch.1 c4a9a928, ch.2 974dfd6a, ch.3 a71ff359, ch.4 046676da, ch.45 111f35be.

## KEY CANON IN THIS BATCH
- THE OPENING SITUATION (ch.1, 9 Sunspire): the Instrument of Dissolution (signed "six days ago") is read into the public record before the full Council. The ward trade is dissolved; infrastructure, reserves and binding records pass to the Accord Council; an Auditor is named "to oversee the dismantlement, and shall hold no obligation to any house"; every ward contract is reviewed; reserves are redistributed "as the Council determines the need".
- SOL'S HOUSE (ch.1): House Vane has had no seat for thirteen years, "since the last ward-taker of the Lower Spine line had gone under a plate and not come up whole"; the chair passed to a cousin. Sol stands at the rail of the second tier; the Vane chair was the fourth from the dais on the north side, marked by a square of paler stone. Sol and Kael have worked side by side "a year" in the yard (ch.1, ch.3).
- THORNE'S GRIEVANCE (ch.1): Malrik (deep grey, plain silver clasp, white hair, straight spine, on his grandson's arm) says he, his father and his grandfather all served as ward: "It was a debt that was never repaid... We were told the cost was shared. It was not shared. It was borne." He claims "a house that has already paid the cost has earned the right to control who pays it next" and asks his house be given a seat at the mechanism-building table. The Chancellor (old, small, thin voice, a woman) answers: the Accord has no path for one house to administer another's contracts; the Council will hear a formal petition when Thorne files one. Thorne's grandson (tall, not much past twenty) is silent and looks once at Sol.
- KAEL ON HIS FATHER (ch.1-2): "Valerius Ashworth is eight months in the ground, my lord." Valerius's desk: dark oak, a gouge in the left drawer where he drove a letter opener through a lying report; the office smells of pipe tobacco and ward oil; Kael has moved nothing.
- THE RULE IS BORN (ch.1, 3): Sol: "tell me now, and tell me first, every time." Ch.3: "Before is better than after... It isn't the same as asking." Kael: "Would you take the Lower Spine anchor with me?" Sol takes the register desk.
- THE LOWER SPINE ANCHOR (ch.4): 211 steps (counted by Odo going down, by Kael coming up); round chamber "the size of a small church"; a dark stone slab "as long as a barge" in a pit, with veins of unmined old silver ("a season, a life spent"); the plate at the north end in a shallow socket: black iron, a hand's breadth thick, wide as a door, scorch fanning out "like a scorched hand". Odo: "Ward-taker sits at the plate. Cost goes down through her hands." Thirteen years ago Sol's mother sat at the plate and it "had taken more than it was owed"; she walked out on her own feet and never fully walked back into her body. Kael did not touch the plate.
- THE HIDDEN DIAGRAM (ch.4): behind a facing stone at the base of the east wall, in oilcloth, a ward-paper survey diagram of the Lower Spine with the Council's old stamp; a thin dotted line from the anchor's south edge runs east toward the cliff face and Lower Reach and off the paper; no label.
- THE 8 SUNSPIRE BEARER (ch.3-4): the Thorne holdings register and the stair-head visitors' book both end "8 Sunspire. One bearer. Name not given. Authority, pass of the house." Three names in three years before it: a works crew, a Council inspector (now dead), a steward of the house. The Thorne steward at the stair says a bearer of the blood may enter without a name, "the family's to say". The pen that ruled the column and wrote the last entry "was the same pen" and no Works Office clerk held it (ch.3, last line).
- SITE SIX (ch.2-3): survey sheet shows six sites boxed in red, all marked closed; Renn redraws site six an arm's length to the left because the floor stops agreeing with the wall at a course of flags laid across the old line in "a cream mortar I don't know" (not the Accord's grey lime); "It was worked on."
- COUNTS: eleven disused ward sites on Kael's list; six in the Lower Reach district; seven of the eleven are Thorne-held (Sol's count in the nine-volume register); Lower Spine anchor is fourth from the top (a Thorne holding, upper stair shut "since the accident").
- TAP CLOSED ON ELEVEN (ch.45, 30 Cinderveil): see ch.45 below. Thorne's tally book: "Ten breaths. Close in the trough." Sol: eleven. The tap closes after a crest on the tenth that would have flooded the Lower Reach. Then a pulse from the east under the boiling house on the next eleventh breath, felt only by Sol.

## CH.2 | 10 Sunspire | POV Kael
- WHO: Kael; Sol (hands in her coat pockets); Renn (lean, forty or near, ink in the creases of every knuckle; "Council survey commission, Lower Reach roster"; talks when nervous; his wife says the Council should pay him by the silence; has "never been under this floor"); a splicer's widow across the lane.
- WHAT: Gallery under the burned ropewalk (burned in the Accord's first year): four hundred paces, iron-banded hatches stamped with Accord lead discs. Sites one and two thirty paces apart: lead-stamped, drained, dry. Sol lies beside a hatch: "It breathes. Slow. Like a sleeper." Kael logs her words in the gauge book with her initials, the first entry he had not shaped. Renn: "The trade never wanted anyone under the floors. It wanted the floors to work." Noon: Kael on the Accord ending "a wage". Fourth hatch: Renn stares at the floor, will redraw site six and not say why until he has "a second line to set beside the first". Cliff road: Kael steps to the drop side. Night: Kael's father's desk; his list of eleven sites; the Lower Spine anchor fourth from the top; he builds the assignment sentence ("Structural survey. Two techs. One morning.") before he has asked Sol anything, and writes it in the blank margin. Renn's note: "Redrawing site six. My first line was made from the clerks' description and the ground does not agree with it. Don't know what I'm looking at. Will bring the second sheet at first bell. Not urgent. R."
- OBJECTS AND COUNTS: survey fair copy, six boxed sites stamped that dawn; Kael's list of eleven; flow gauges; Sol's initials in a hand that "leaned the other way from his own".
- CONTINUITY NOTE (for Book 3): Renn's note is in "small and level" letters with "every descender... closed, a neater hand than his talk". Ch.42's forged witness hand is upright with a tail that closes. State only; no inference made here.

## CH.3 | 11 Sunspire | POV Sol
- WHAT: Kael's list now reads "Lower Spine anchor... K.A. alone on the stair" and "I.V. Register of Thorne holdings, research wing. Full week." He tells her he built the sentence before asking; she says "Ask me"; he asks; she chooses the desk ("I'll tell you what it says before I decide what it means. That's mine to keep"); he adds I.V. to the leave slip anyway. Research wing: tables in the pattern of a ward-line, "seven aisles and a spine"; Renn leaves his second sheet with the porter and meets Sol: "Bring every sheet to both of us." She finds the Lower Spine entry and the 8 Sunspire line, copies it, and pulls the ninth volume; Bram (young clerk, name on a slip at his cuff): "Nobody's had the ninth out in a year, my lady."
- LINES: Sol: "I'm counting." Kael: "I'd rather you were angry at what I did than at what I hid."
- OPEN: who the 8 Sunspire bearer was; whose pen.

## CH.4 | 12 Sunspire | POV Kael
- WHO: Kael; the house steward of the Lower Spine stair (narrow man in Thorne grey, violet cuffs, "a face designed for not being remembered"); techs Odo Marl (fifty, broad, western-office surveys since before the Accord, takes readings twice) and Tessa Rook (twenty-three, quick, chalk-line at her belt); Sol.
- WHAT: Stair head, leave read twice, the missing second name noticed; the visitors' book (grey cloth, Date/Name/Authority); descent, chamber, plate, diagram (see KEY CANON). Kael tells the techs he will report the whole of the room to Sol and that nothing is said outside the three of them "Not because it's secret. Because I don't yet know what it means." Evening: Sol hands him her slip of the same entry; "You beat me to the fact. I beat you to the pen."
- LINES: Kael: "I did not touch it. I wanted to." Sol: "Who draws a line and doesn't say what it's for?"
- CONTINUITY NOTE: the Lower Spine house steward (a narrow man) is a different person from the works steward (small, grey-haired woman) who stands at Malrik's shoulder from ch.15 on. Book 3 must not merge them.

## CH.45 | 30 Cinderveil | POV Sol, then Kael (switches at the scene break)
- WHO: Sol; Kael (stick, dead left leg, needles worse on stairs); Corren; Joren; Thorne's crew-master (broad, rope scars up both forearms) and seven Thorne men in oiled leather; Malrik Thorne ("ten years older than he had in the chamber two days ago"); four grey escort wardens; Chancellor (plain grey); senior clerk; Auda Ashworth; Ferra.
- WHAT: Descent: second landing, the lamp bracket glass clean, reservoir dry, a pale ring on the flagstone where oil was poured out, not burned down; Corren: "Two days. Three at the outside." Corren's two men went at first light after the fisherman's skiff; the sighting was a week older than the 24th. Malrik: Aldous is at the house; "Thorne has not fed a lamp on this stair since my father's day." Crew-master gives Sol the blackened tally book; "Ten breaths. Close in the trough." Sol counts eleven; they count together; Malrik says "Hold" at the crest; "Eleven" together; "Now"; the wheel turns; needles settle to zero. Malrik: "I would have closed this tap on the tenth breath myself, Lady Vane, with my own hands, if you had not said wait." Sol: "You held. I only spoke." Malrik: "Yes. I noticed that I did." Sol alone feels a faint movement from the east on the next eleventh. Scene break: Ansa's bread to the gallery mouth for everyone who stood at the wheel; trunk gauges steady. Council at the ninth hour: the petition read by the senior clerk; "sole lead" struck in red ink with the date; signed Kael then Sol beneath; "The warrants of this audit bear two names or they bear none." A single knock of a cane foot from the Ashworth bench. Kael tells the Chancellor two things aloud (the eleven count and the pulse from the east under the boiling house, which has "been adding a breath to every cycle we have counted"; Aldous will be asked tomorrow who taught him his letters). Chancellor: no survey of such a line exists; "A ledger is only as honest as the last hand that held the pen. Who held it forty years ago is a question I have been too old, and sat too high, to ask." Last line: she asks what he will do if the hand belongs to a house they have both trusted.
- OBJECTS AND COUNTS: tap closed on eleven; Thorne's tally says ten "under his lordship's grandfather"; "Forty households. Fifty." (the Lower Reach); the fourth roof from the corner is Ansa's; Ferra took the three-page family account at first bell; the fourth page stays in Kael's coat, to be given to Sol that night.
- LINES: Kael: "the largest things in a life arrive looking exactly like paperwork." Sol: "It didn't soften you. It just stopped you deciding things alone. I happen to like what's left."
- CONTINUITY SNAG (soft): the petition is read as "Auditor of the Council"; every other chapter says "Auditor of the Accord".
- CONTINUITY NOTE: the Chancellor's closing question is unanswered; Book 2 ends on it.
