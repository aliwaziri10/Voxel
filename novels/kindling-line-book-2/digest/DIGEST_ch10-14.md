Fact lookup written 2026-10-03 from the live chapter files (main). Not a summary and not new canon. If this file and a chapter disagree, the chapter wins; fix this file. Format per chapter: DATE | POV | WHO | WHAT | OBJECTS AND COUNTS | LINES AND BEATS | OPEN.

## CH.10 | 18 Sunspire | POV Sol
- WHO: Sol; Kael; a Thorne runner (violet card).
- WHAT: In the decommissioning register room (low ceiling, no windows, one ward-lamp that must be fed oil every two hours) they read the Accord's register. Thorne invites Kael alone to a private audience; he declines and tells Sol first. Lower Reach: six sites under the old ropewalk district, five with closure entries, Anchor Seven's status is "Contested" (maintenance flag never lifted). Varel stopped initialling his entries three weeks before he vanished. At dusk an unsigned strip is found in the register room's returns slot (needs a key neither held): "Anchor Seven. Ropewalk sublevel. Ask what a decommissioned site needs a live ward-charge for." Kael drafts a petition for access in both names, shows Sol each paragraph before writing the next, and leaves out the strip on purpose (no seal, no provenance).
- OBJECTS AND COUNTS: the anonymous strip (folded in Sol's notebook). Six ropewalk sites, one contested. Sol's mother's ward-contract carried "contested" status for two years after the accident that should have ended it.
- LINES: Kael: "Faster isn't the measure anymore." Kael: "Together," and he holds the stylus out for her name beside his.
- OPEN: Who left the strip.
- NOTE: the chapter's last line says a charge is drawn "in a sublevel no one had officially opened in three years".

## CH.11 | 19 Sunspire | POV Kael (final scene Sol)
- WHO: Kael; the under-clerk at the registrar's office (opens seventh bell); Sol; Renn (lean surveyor, ink in the creases of his fingers; holds a Council survey commission); Joren (older Ashworth warden, thick through the shoulder, scar across the back of one hand); Mara (Ashworth warden, twenty years' service, checks her short blade with her thumb).
- WHAT: Council grants the access petition in full: both names, Ashworth and Vane, entry to the sublevel of Anchor Seven from first bell on the 21st through the 23rd. Witness may be proposed but not compelled. A second sheet in Thorne violet (dark wax, copper thread in the tie) defers Thorne's hearing from the 21st to the 24th "to allow the petition's signatories time to weigh the auditor's forthcoming report". Count: 7. Kael had written no report. Renn's survey margin: "Floor level here re-laid in the last few years. Mortar too clean for a dead site." They nominate Renn as the commissioned witness (Sol chooses him: "He talks too much when he's frightened"). Escort: two Ashworth wardens on the ropewalk floor, perimeter only, answer jointly to Kael and Sol, nothing goes upstairs without both names. Kael gives the order with Sol at his shoulder. On the rope bridge between the Ashworth spire and the research wing a hooded figure at the far anchor post lets the hood fall a finger's width: copper cloak clasp, three strands, drop, feather, flame. At the post, a fresh knife scratch: a line and "Seven".
- LINES: Mara: "Your father would have sent us in blind, my lord." Kael: "Here are the reasons."
- OPEN: Whose clasp (a Thorne watcher); what Thorne needs three more days for.

## CH.12 | 20 Sunspire | POV Sol
- WHO: Sol; Kael; Renn; Ansa (small, brown, knotted, hands like ropes; spliced on this floor forty years till the fire; lives in the room over the tar-shop; knew Valerius; calls Sol "the ward-girl"); the Council runner.
- WHAT: The Lower Reach smells of tar and wet hemp; the ropewalk burned in Year One. Around the sealed hatch at the gallery's far end the flagstones are re-laid in cream mortar (Accord works crews use grey lime); the iron ring is bright on the inner curve, so lifted. Ansa: a blue ward-lamp glows up the shaft at dusk, lit every evening for years, nobody sees who; a thin clerk with ink on his cuffs (Varel) sat at a bench three days, five or six weeks back; "the ones with the copper on their collars" came asking about her lodgers. Runner: an eighth house has signed; the vote is now held in the same session as the hearing, on the 24th. Sol says aloud she is afraid of the hatch; Kael says he is too. They sleep in the room above the tar-shop (Ansa's spare cot). At dusk the lamp lights though nobody went near the hatch: whoever lit it is already inside.
- LINES: Renn and Kael both say "we'll always know when to worry". Kael: "I'd rather be frightened beside you."
- OPEN: Who lit the lamp.

## CH.13 | 21 Sunspire | POV Sol
- WHO: Sol; Kael; Renn; Joren; Mara.
- WHAT: They wait through the night (Sol's choice; Joren watches the hatch). At first bell Renn reads the grant to the wardens, Joren lifts the ring (hinges oiled, not Accord grease). Spiral stair, dry to the second landing (pumped for years). Chamber at the foot: a deep-blue ward-lamp on an iron bracket, a chair, a low table, a cup, a blanket, a stylus of worn bone, a small ledger. Last margin line in Varel's hand: "They come at the tenth bell. Do not answer the door." Last date nine days earlier. The ledger is a meter: a draw on the Vault's main line logged in units the Accord has not used since the trade ended; six weeks of Varel's entries after years of another hand, "older and blunter", no gaps, never balanced against the register. Renn stamps every copied page with his commission, hour and date. Left passage: a lattice of bound-force threads; Sol cuts three with her damping-sigil ward-knife; the fourth is a piggyback trigger: ward-sleep venom darts, which she pushes Kael clear of. Door: seven rotating rings in a bronze face, copper and violet enamel rim in the Thorne knot. Sol opens it by ear (her mother taught her the Vault's old tongues before reading). The door then swings inward from the other side: a lamplit face.
- OBJECTS AND COUNTS: Varel's ledger. Seven rings. Tap logged "for three years" in Kael's words though entries date from Year One (CONTINUITY SNAG, minor).
- LINES: Kael: "Next time I'll tell you before I step." Sol: "We didn't finish opening that."

## CH.14 | 22 Sunspire | POV Kael
- WHO: Varel (thin, about fifty, ink in the creases of his knuckles); Sol; Kael; Renn.
- WHAT: Varel: six weeks ago he followed a charge line from his ledgers to a site the register called contested; three retainers in violet kept him there to watch the meter and gave him a blanket; if the meter stops balancing the Vault trunk surges back up the tap and finds the weakest cellar in the Lower Reach (Ansa's tar-shop, the splicing halls, the rope-makers' tenements: forty or fifty households). Thorne comes at the tenth bell every night, except last night. A flooded gallery beyond the door, forty paces to an arch and a pedestal. The ward-line runs buried under the water; the charge cycle is about eleven breaths (swell and drop). Kael asks Sol to cross first and anchor the rope himself ("and I hate it"). Sol counts; Kael counts aloud. Sol: her mother said a ward-line "is a debt that hasn't decided who owes it yet". Alcove: a lead-lined oak box bound in iron on the pedestal, near-black wax strip unbroken on the lid, a rusted band showing a vellum wrapper marked "Ward Deed of Succession". They decide not to open it; Renn will stamp the unbroken wax at the stair top. A bell rings overhead; at the sixth stroke the ward-line flares white and every lamp goes out; iron scrapes at the top of the stair; boots; a voice: "Auditor Ashworth. I would leave that where you found it."
- OBJECTS AND COUNTS: the Deed box ("the size of a small loaf"). Eleven-breath charge cycle: FIRST appearance of this number on the page.
- OPEN: Whose voice; whether the Deed is genuine.
