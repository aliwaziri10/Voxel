# Proof of Home: chapter tracker

A chapter is DONE only when it has been rewritten, proofread on all 10 points, pushed, and confirmed live on GitHub. Nothing marked DONE is ever proofread again.

## The 10 points

| Point | Check |
|---|---|
| P1 | Word count 2,300 to 2,700, reached through real sub-beats, never padding |
| P2 | Zero em dashes |
| P3 | No banned words or hedges: some/something/someone/somewhere/somehow, particular, the specific, the kind of, one beat too long, tell words |
| P4 | No planning-note leaks: no chapter numbers, "the last chapter", touch levels, the word reader, labels from the canon |
| P5 | Point of view and tense: odd chapters Sarah, even chapters Jack, close third, past tense |
| P6 | Metaphor discipline: baking comparisons only in Sarah's chapters, water comparisons only in Jack's |
| P7 | Canon: fixed names, no dates, weekdays, years or ages in digits, no kiss before chapter 27, Nell never in an intimate beat, no invented backstory |
| P8 | Touch level matches the plan exactly and the ache rises one notch from the chapter before |
| P9 | Grammar, commas, full stops, dialogue punctuation |
| P10 | Rhythm and tells: sentence lengths vary, bread magic shown small and specific, ends on an image, not a summary |
