Proof of Home is a slow-burn cozy fantasy romance set in the lakeside town of Larkmere. Sarah Quill is a baker whose bread carries whatever she feels while shaping it. She inherits her great-aunt Fenna's bakery on Lantern Street and promises herself one season before she decides whether to stay. Jack Mercer pilots the ferry Heron across Larkmere Water. He believes wanting someone to stay is the same as trapping them, so he will never ask. The love story is the whole book. Every chapter raises the ache by one notch. Their closeness is restrained, sensual and never explicit. The first kiss comes in chapter 27. After it the longing keeps growing and is released only in the final chapter. Odd chapters are told from Sarah's point of view and even chapters from Jack's, in close third person, past tense. Time is told by season, light, weather and the bread: no dates, no weekdays, no ages in digits. No em dashes. Follow the saved beat map for every chapter.
