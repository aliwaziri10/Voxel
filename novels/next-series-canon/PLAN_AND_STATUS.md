## What the pipeline actually sends to the model (read from live files)

1. `story_bible.continuity_prompt_block(series)` puts only three things in front of EVERY chapter prompt: `characters` (name to description), `plot_facts` (list of strings), and `books` (title and summary). Everything the model must know has to be in those fields or in the chapter's own beat.
2. `voxel_cli.py novel` REUSES a stored beat map if it exists and its length equals `--chapters`. So a human-approved beat map saved in `story_bibles/<series>.json` under `book_beat_maps["<exact --book title>"]` replaces the model-invented one. This is the main protection against invented plot.
3. Each beat map entry is `{"chapter": n, "chapter_date": "...", "sub_beats": [{"type": ..., "detail": ...}]}` with 2 to 4 sub-beats. Allowed types: `plot_beat`, `relationship_micro_beat`, `interior_beat`, `stakes_beat`, `callback_beat`. Only the sub-beats are shown to the model, so every fact a chapter needs (place, who is present, date, numbers, what the reader may and may not learn yet) must be written inside the sub-beat text.
4. `chapter_date` is written by the model as a `<!-- chapter_date: ... -->` first line and then compared by `proofreader.py` against the beat map by exact string match. So the date string must be generated by script from an ISO date, never typed by hand.
5. Word floor and ceiling default to 2300 and 2700 (EDITORIAL_CHARTER.md). No padding.
6. Output guard (`voxel_cli._META_PATTERNS`) rejects and regenerates any chapter containing: `[End of Chapter`, the word sub-beat, `Book 1` to `Book 9`, `book one` to `book five`, `the series`, `protagonist`, `no <word> arc needed`, `persuasion arc`, `Wound:`, `Competence:`. Canon text sent to the model must avoid those words, or the model may echo them.
7. The chapter system prompt in `content_provider.py` says "continuing an existing series". It is shared code, so it is NOT changed here. The canon compensates with explicit closed-world rules inside `plot_facts`.

## Why earlier books went wrong, and the matching rule for this canon

| Past failure (from repo notes) | Rule in this canon |
|---|---|
| Two characters with the same first name (Warren) | Name registry. Validator rejects duplicate first names and near-duplicates (edit distance 2 or less), and any name used in older books. |
| A scene repeated almost word for word across chapters | Every sub-beat is unique per chapter. Each fact has a single "established in chapter N" owner. Later chapters may only refer back. |
| Dates and ages drifting | One ISO date per chapter. Weekday, formatted string, ages and day counts are computed by script, not typed. |
| Model copied author notes and book numbers into prose | Canon text is written as in-world facts only, and is scanned for the banned words in point 6. |
| Model invented minor names, places, prices, numbers | Closed-world rule plus a pre-made pool of named extras, a locations list, a price list and a numbers ledger. |
| Facts revealed too early or contradicted later | Each chapter lists what the reader may learn and what must stay hidden until a named chapter. Validator checks no hidden fact appears early. |
| Cut-off or padded chapters | Unchanged guard in `voxel_cli.py`. Beat maps give 2 to 4 real sub-beats so length comes from content. |

## Closed-world rules to put in the model's plot_facts (draft wording)

- Everything that may be named is listed in this canon. The people, places, businesses, dates, prices and numbers given here are complete.
- If a scene needs a minor person who is not in the cast, use a role ("the woman at the counter") or take the next unused name from the extras pool. Never make up a new name.
- If a scene needs a place, price, distance, date or count that is not given, write the scene so it is not needed. Do not make one up.
- Do not add history, relatives, past events or town rules that are not listed.
- Do not do arithmetic in the prose. Use only the numbers given for that chapter.
- Magic follows the written rules exactly, with their limits. It never does anything not listed.
