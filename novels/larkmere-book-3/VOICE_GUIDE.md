# Voice Guide: Keel and Comb

Read `novels/EDITORIAL_CHARTER.md` and `novels/HUMAN_WRITING.md` first. Canon and plan: `canon/CANON_LOCK.md`, `HANDOFF.md`, `briefs/`.

## Point of view
Close third person, past tense. Odd chapters are Bryony Fairlie. Even chapters are Ewan Marrick.

## Bryony (odd chapters)
Blunt, practical, short sentences when she is controlling herself; a long unspooling sentence when her control slips. Notices grain, joints, what will fail first, who is carrying too much, who is lying with their hands. Says the plain thing and then walks off. Hums when she works and does not know it. Comparisons come only from boats, wood, tools, water and the weather on the lake: fair curve, plumb, bevel, scarf joint, caulking, grain, rot, clinker, trim. NO bee, hive or honey comparisons in her chapters. Honey itself can be tasted and named as honey; she never compares anything TO bees or comb.

## Ewan (even chapters)
Patient, dry, quietly funny, kind. Says less than he means and lets his hands do the rest. Notices weather, flowers, what is blooming, how the hives sound, who is afraid. Longer, gentler sentences that tighten when he is angry or afraid. Comparisons come only from bees, comb, honey, hives, flowers, smoke, weather and seasons. NO boat or carpentry comparisons in his chapters.

## The magic on the page
Honey tastes of the beekeeper's truest feeling on the day the comb was cut. Show it as a taste in the body, small and precise (a sweetness with a held breath in it, a note under it like cold iron). Never explain the rule in dialogue except in Ottoline's blunt words. Ewan cannot taste his own honey and learns what is in it from other faces. Bees and wax are ordinary.

## Romance rules (same as the series)
- Desire lives in small physical detail: a thumb on a wrist, the sting scars, the pencil behind her ear, breath on a collar, the effort of staying still.
- Second chance: they know each other. The ache is in what they remember and do not do. No flashback scenes; one remembered detail at a time.
- Pressed closeness is built from restraint: a locked grip, a held breath, a word not said. Sensual, never explicit.
- Every chapter raises the ache one notch. After the kiss in chapter 28 the wanting only grows. Release only in chapter 40.
- Tobiah and Ottoline never appear in an intimate beat. They interrupt, tease and meddle at the edges.
- Interruptions come from feeling, weather, bees or someone who loves them. Never a procedure.
- Every chapter ends on a turn, a question or a held image, never a summary.

## Theme
Both were right to choose their work, and neither asked the other to make room. Never write "who was wrong". The question is only whether there is room now.

## Banned
Everything in HUMAN_WRITING.md. Plus: no digits for years, ages, dates; no weekday or month names; "days later"; planning words in prose (chapter, beat, protagonist, sub-beat, touch level, canon); em dashes; the words some, something, someone, somewhere, somehow, somebody, particular, the specific, the kind of, kind of. Use "grey" throughout, American spelling otherwise.
