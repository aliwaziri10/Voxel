# Brief 5 | Bryony | thaw | touch level 1

PLOT: Cold rain. She tears out the first rotten planks and finds the rot runs deeper than she thought. She needs seasoned oak and tools, so she walks along the shore to the Fairlie yard, shuttered for seven springs. She forces the door for the first time: dust, her father's bench, his adze on its peg, the smell of old oak. The roof is dry. Ewan comes in out of the rain with a lantern, because he saw the door open. She asks who has been mending the roof. "Tobiah and I." She takes the adze off its peg. She does not say thank you.
PRESENT: Bryony (flat pencil behind her ear, sawdust in her hair, scar across the back of her left hand, canvas tool roll). Ewan (hive tool in his back pocket, wax under his thumbnails, smoke on his coat, old stings, folded bee veil). NOBODY ELSE.
SETTING: the Net Sheds in the rain, the shore path, the Fairlie yard inside.
MAGIC: none this chapter.
OBJECTS: her father's adze. The bench. The Fairlie yard roof. The Heron (exact description).
LOCKED: none.
WITHHOLD: no Leaving Jar, no keel note, no Abel.
BODY: his thumbprint in the dust of the bench where he must have wiped it; her breath; the adze's handle still warm from the lantern.
INTERIOR: seven winters of kept dryness means someone stayed and she cannot find a word for it.
STAKES: if the yard were not sound she would have no tools; she realizes she was held up by what she walked away from.
CALLBACK: ch3: her father's plane marks on the frames.
ENDS ON: the rain on the roof that held.
