# Brief 1 | Bryony | thaw | touch level 0

PLOT: Late afternoon on the thaw road where it comes down past Marrick Rise into Larkmere, mud and meltwater everywhere. She stops above the town to look at Larkmere Water (the ice gone grey and rotten at the edges) and the long roof of the Net Sheds. An early, small swarm drops out of the air and settles on her shoulder, her pack and the brim of her hat. She goes still. Ewan comes running down from the Rise with the skep and a bee brush. "Don't move. They're only deciding." It is the first time she has seen him in seven springs. He coaxes the bees into the skep with his bare hands and his breath, close to her face, and says nothing else. She tells him she was sent for by the keeper of the Net Sheds. His face changes: he did not know.
PRESENT: Bryony (flat pencil behind her ear, sawdust in her hair, scar across the back of her left hand, canvas tool roll). Ewan (hive tool in his back pocket, wax under his thumbnails, smoke on his coat, old stings on his forearms, folded bee veil in his belt). NOBODY ELSE. The lane is empty.
SETTING: the thaw road above Larkmere at the foot of Marrick Rise, mud, meltwater, early spring light, the town and the lake below.
MAGIC: none this chapter. The swarm is ordinary bees landing where they like. (Honey rule exists but is not shown.)
OBJECTS: the skep. Her canvas tool roll. Her hat.
LOCKED: "Don't move. They're only deciding." Ewan, here only.
WITHHOLD: no Leaving Jar, no Tobiah by sight (she may think of him as her brother, once), no kiss, no mention of the Heron's keel being soft beyond "the ferry". She does not say why she left.
BODY: his hand at her collar brushing bees; her pulse in her throat; she does not look at his mouth.
INTERIOR: she planned every sentence on the road and none of them survived the first bee.
STAKES: a swarm on a person is harmless if she stays still; the real risk is that she moves or speaks first and ruins the quiet.
CALLBACK: none yet; the first chapter seeds the road she left on.
ENDS ON: one bee walking up her sleeve toward her wrist while neither of them moves.
