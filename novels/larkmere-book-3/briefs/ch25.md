# Brief 25 | Bryony | swarm | touch level 5

PLOT: Abel makes the offer in the Fairlie yard: the coast yard will be hers when he lays down his adze. He is old, and wants to know where it goes before his hands go. She asks for time. He says the launch. That evening the town lights lanterns for the keel set: the new oak keel is down in the Heron after seven springs. Someone plays a fiddle on the quay. She dances badly with Ewan; his hand at her waist, her breath at his collar. Abel watches from the dark with his white eyebrow raised. She wants to tell Ewan and does not.
PRESENT: Bryony (flat pencil behind her ear, sawdust in her hair, scar across the back of her left hand, canvas tool roll). Abel (one white eyebrow, hands like spades, talks in boat measures). Ewan (hive tool in his back pocket, wax under his thumbnails, smoke on his coat, old stings, folded bee veil). The quay folk (unnamed, one short line at most). NOBODY ELSE.
SETTING: the Fairlie yard, the quay at night with lanterns, the Net Sheds door.
MAGIC: none this chapter.
OBJECTS: the new oak keel. The lanterns. The fiddle (unnamed player).
LOCKED: none.
WITHHOLD: she does not tell Ewan about the offer.
BODY: his hand at her waist; her breath at his collar; the heat of him through his shirt.
INTERIOR: neither of them asked the other to make room seven springs ago and she is about to do it again.
STAKES: the offer is a second life and she is dancing on the first.
CALLBACK: ch23: Abel arrives; ch16: the plank bent in steam.
ENDS ON: the fiddle stopping and his hand not leaving her waist.
