# Brief 8 | Ewan | thaw | touch level 2

PLOT: The first warm day: the ice on Larkmere Water goes out in one booming night and the whole town stands on the quay at dusk to watch the grey slabs drift. The quay folk see Bryony and mutter. One says she has a nerve coming back. Ewan steps in front of her without deciding to. She notices. They stand shoulder to shoulder watching the ice turn to water and for the first time since the lane they both laugh. The harbor lanterns come up one by one at dusk. The Net Sheds lantern is lit last. She asks who keeps it lit. He says Ottoline. It is a lie and honey cannot lie.
PRESENT: Ewan (hive tool in his back pocket, wax under his thumbnails, smoke on his coat, old stings, folded bee veil). Bryony (flat pencil behind her ear, sawdust in her hair, scar across the back of her left hand, canvas tool roll). The quay folk (unnamed, one short line at most). NOBODY ELSE.
SETTING: the quay and harbor at dusk, floating ice, the harbor lanterns, the Net Sheds wall.
MAGIC: honey rule 2: he knows a Marrick jar never lies and he has just lied out loud for the first time in a long time.
OBJECTS: the harbor lanterns. The Net Sheds lantern (exact description).
LOCKED: none.
WITHHOLD: do not reveal that Ewan lights the lantern; Bryony does not push.
BODY: shoulder to shoulder at the rail; the first laugh shaking through both of them.
INTERIOR: he has told himself the lantern is for the Heron.
STAKES: the town has not forgiven her and he has just chosen a side without deciding.
CALLBACK: ch3: the lantern on the seaward wall; ch4: her saying she will be done when the Heron is done.
ENDS ON: the green glass lit and the lie sitting between them.
