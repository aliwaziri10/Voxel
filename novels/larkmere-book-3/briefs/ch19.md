# Brief 19 | Bryony | swarm | touch level 3

PLOT: Swarm season in earnest. Bees leaving hives all over the Rise. Ewan asks her to help bring down a swarm from an apple tree: she holds the skep, he cuts the branch. The bees pour over their joined hands. He tells her to stand still, the way he did in the lane. She does. Her hand is under his, covered in bees, and she is not afraid. She sees how he works: patient, soft-voiced, quick. He watches her face.
PRESENT: Bryony (flat pencil behind her ear, sawdust in her hair, scar across the back of her left hand, canvas tool roll). Ewan (hive tool in his back pocket, wax under his thumbnails, smoke on his coat, old stings, folded bee veil). NOBODY ELSE.
SETTING: the orchard on Marrick Rise in swarm season, an apple tree, a ladder, the skep.
MAGIC: none this chapter beyond rule 6: swarms land where they like.
OBJECTS: the skep (exact). The ladder.
LOCKED: none (the "Don't move" line belongs to chapter 1 only).
WITHHOLD: no kiss; no jar; no Abel.
BODY: bees crawling over their joined hands; his thumb against her scar.
INTERIOR: she finds she trusts his hands with a thousand living things.
STAKES: swarms are weather: they go when they go.
CALLBACK: ch1: the swarm on her shoulder.
ENDS ON: the last bee walking off their joined hands into the skep.
