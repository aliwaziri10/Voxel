# Brief 21 | Bryony | swarm | touch level 4

PLOT: Ottoline leaves a jar of the new spring honey on the Net Sheds bench with one word, "Eat." Bryony takes one spoonful and tastes his present feeling, plain and unhidden: longing, and under it, gladness. She sits down on a plank. She walks up the Rise at dusk with the jar in her hand. She finds him in the Honey House labeling jars. She sets the jar on the bench harder than she means to. Neither speaks. Her hand goes over his on the lid.
PRESENT: Bryony (flat pencil behind her ear, sawdust in her hair, scar across the back of her left hand, canvas tool roll). Ewan (hive tool in his back pocket, wax under his thumbnails, smoke on his coat, old stings, folded bee veil). Ottoline (ring of keys at her belt, bent back, upright voice) only at the start, leaving the jar. NOBODY ELSE.
SETTING: the Net Sheds bench, the path up Marrick Rise at dusk, the Honey House.
MAGIC: honey rules 1 to 3: he cannot taste it; she can; she knows it holds the day of the cut.
OBJECTS: the spring jar (a plain round jar with a cloth top tied with string and a paper strip). The Leaving Jar on the top shelf, glimpsed.
LOCKED: none.
WITHHOLD: she does not tell him what it tasted of; she does not kiss him; no Abel.
BODY: her hand over his on the lid; her face an inch from his throat; his pulse under her thumb.
INTERIOR: the honey told her more than he has said in seven springs.
STAKES: she can never again pretend not to know.
CALLBACK: ch3: the first spoon; ch20: the cut.
ENDS ON: her hand still over his and the jar between them.
