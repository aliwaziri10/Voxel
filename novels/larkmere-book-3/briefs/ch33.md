# Brief 33 | Bryony | lime-bloom | touch level 10d

PLOT: At dusk Ewan carries the Leaving Jar down from the top shelf and sets it on the Net Sheds bench. He says he has never opened it and cannot taste it himself. "Open it or I'll pour it in the lake." She breaks the wax disc and cuts the blue linen. She takes one spoonful. It tastes of anger and love together, and under them forgiveness, already there, as though he forgave her before she was out of sight of the Rise. She weeps. She tells him what it tasted of. She kisses him hard, through tears.
PRESENT: Bryony (flat pencil behind her ear, sawdust in her hair, scar across the back of her left hand, canvas tool roll). Ewan (hive tool in his back pocket, wax under his thumbnails, smoke on his coat, old stings, folded bee veil). NOBODY ELSE.
SETTING: the Net Sheds bench at dusk, the lantern burning, the loft above.
MAGIC: honey rules 1 to 3: what she tastes is his truest feeling on the morning she left: anger and love together, forgiveness already there; he cannot taste it.
OBJECTS: the Leaving Jar (exact: a squat glass jar of dark honey, sealed with a disc of Marrick wax, a strip of blue linen tied round the neck). A spoon.
LOCKED: none.
WITHHOLD: he does not say "I would have said go" yet; that belongs to chapter 34.
BODY: her hands in his shirt; his hands at her wet face; the honey still on her mouth when she kisses him.
INTERIOR: she carried seven springs of guilt he never assigned.
STAKES: forgiveness is harder to take than anger.
CALLBACK: ch11: Ottoline's telling; ch7: "It's not for eating."
ENDS ON: the empty spoon and his hand at the back of her head.
