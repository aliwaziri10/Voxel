# Brief 9 | Bryony | blossom | touch level 2

PLOT: The Rise orchard in first blossom. The Marrick punt leaks and Ewan rows it to the Far Shore every lime-bloom, so she offers to caulk it, to prove her worth with a small job. They take it out on the lake together to test it: her first time on the water in seven springs by anything but the coast road. He points out the lime wood across the water and tells her what the flow looks like. Abel Trent's name comes up when he asks, carefully, who taught her; she says "Abel Trent, on the coast" and shuts the subject. Their knees touch in the narrow boat. A petal sits in his hair and she does not remove it.
PRESENT: Bryony (flat pencil behind her ear, sawdust in her hair, scar across the back of her left hand, canvas tool roll). Ewan (hive tool in his back pocket, wax under his thumbnails, smoke on his coat, old stings, folded bee veil). NOBODY ELSE.
SETTING: the Marrick punt on Larkmere Water in blossom weather, the orchard on Marrick Rise behind, the Far Shore ahead.
MAGIC: none this chapter.
OBJECTS: the punt (exact: the Marrick punt, a flat-bottomed boat with a patched seam). The Marrick wax. The lime wood seen from the water.
LOCKED: none.
WITHHOLD: do not describe Abel beyond his name; no offer; no Leaving Jar.
BODY: knees together in the punt; a petal in his hair she does not remove.
INTERIOR: the water is the first thing in Larkmere that feels easy.
STAKES: the Heron is the only boat big enough for the lime crates and she is the only one who can finish her.
CALLBACK: ch4: the deadline; ch5: the yard roof.
ENDS ON: the petal still in his hair when they reach the shore.
