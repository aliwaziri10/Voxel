# Brief 37 | Bryony | lime-bloom | touch level 10f

PLOT: Launch eve. Tobiah finds her in the Fairlie yard and says they are both idiots, that they want the same thing and are being polite. Abel asks if she will come to the coast. She says after the launch. He says, "Don't tell me. Tell him." Ottoline presses her ring of keys into Bryony's hands and tells her that keys are for people who stay or leave and she may choose which doors. She stands in the Net Sheds holding the keys and does not go to him. The lantern outside is unlit: Ewan has not come to light it.
PRESENT: Bryony (flat pencil behind her ear, sawdust in her hair, scar across the back of her left hand, canvas tool roll). Tobiah (bee veil too big for him, ears pink when angry, whistles through his teeth). Abel (one white eyebrow, hands like spades, talks in boat measures). Ottoline (ring of keys at her belt, bent back, upright voice). NOBODY ELSE.
SETTING: the Fairlie yard, the Net Sheds at dusk, the unlit lantern.
MAGIC: none this chapter.
OBJECTS: Ottoline's ring of keys. The Net Sheds lantern (unlit). The Heron (exact description) nearly complete.
LOCKED: none.
WITHHOLD: Ewan is not present; she does not decide.
BODY: the weight of the keys in her palm; her hands empty of him; the cold of the unlit lantern.
INTERIOR: she cannot ask him to make room and cannot leave without asking.
STAKES: if she goes she leaves the Heron unfinished and her heart behind.
CALLBACK: ch28: the lantern; ch3: Ottoline's keys.
ENDS ON: the unlit lantern and the keys heavy in her hand.
