# Brief 16 | Ewan | blossom | touch level 4

PLOT: Night work in the Net Sheds. They steam the first new plank in the long box over the stove and bend it onto the frames; it needs two people and fast hands. Her hands guide his onto the plank, they press together to hold the bend while it cools, her back to his chest, his breath in her hair. The plank takes the curve with a long sigh. Neither moves. The stove ticks. Wind in the rafters.
PRESENT: Ewan (hive tool in his back pocket, wax under his thumbnails, smoke on his coat, old stings, folded bee veil). Bryony (flat pencil behind her ear, sawdust in her hair, scar across the back of her left hand, canvas tool roll). NOBODY ELSE.
SETTING: the Net Sheds at night, the steam box, the Heron's ribs, the stove, the lantern's green light through the wall.
MAGIC: none this chapter.
OBJECTS: the first new plank. The steam box. The Heron's frames.
LOCKED: none.
WITHHOLD: no kiss; no talk of the past.
BODY: her hands on his, her back to his chest, his breath in her hair, the steam on both their faces.
INTERIOR: he has not been this close to her working since the year before she left.
STAKES: if the plank cools wrong it cracks and they lose a day of oak.
CALLBACK: ch4: the pry bar; ch12: the loft.
ENDS ON: the plank cooling and neither of them letting go.
