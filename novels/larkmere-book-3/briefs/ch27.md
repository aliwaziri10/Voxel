# Brief 27 | Bryony | swarm | touch level 8

PLOT: Tobiah walks into the Fairlie yard and gives her the boot-box. "I read the first one. I couldn't read the rest." She takes it. They sit on the bench. He calls her Bry for the first time. Abel leaves at dawn for the thaw road, saying he will be back for the launch and she will tell him then. That evening in the Honey House she brings Ewan the box to hold while she thinks. They stand foreheads together, breath shared. An inch apart. A swarm roars out of the Honey House eaves and the whole hive yard turns to noise; they come apart laughing and shaking.
PRESENT: Bryony (flat pencil behind her ear, sawdust in her hair, scar across the back of her left hand, canvas tool roll). Tobiah (bee veil too big for him, ears pink when angry, whistles through his teeth). Abel (one white eyebrow, hands like spades, talks in boat measures) at dawn only. Ewan (hive tool in his back pocket, wax under his thumbnails, smoke on his coat, old stings, folded bee veil). NOBODY ELSE.
SETTING: the Fairlie yard bench, the thaw road at dawn, the Honey House at evening, the eaves.
MAGIC: none this chapter beyond rule 6: swarms leave when they like.
OBJECTS: the boot-box. The skep.
LOCKED: none.
WITHHOLD: no kiss yet.
BODY: foreheads together, breath shared, an inch of air; his hand at her jaw; the roar of a swarm leaving.
INTERIOR: she is about to kiss him and the bees do it for her by interrupting.
