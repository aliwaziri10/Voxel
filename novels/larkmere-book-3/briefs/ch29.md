# Brief 29 | Bryony | lime-bloom | touch level 10a

PLOT: The limes have begun to bloom early on the Far Shore: Ewan says so at dawn, smelling the wind. In the Net Sheds that morning the Heron's new keel is in and the ribs stand open. She pulls him inside the hull cavity where no one can see. They kiss, her hands in his hair, his at her face and neck, the oak-sweet dark around them. She does not let the kiss become more. She is steady and he is not. Light comes through the open ribs in bars.
PRESENT: Bryony (flat pencil behind her ear, sawdust in her hair, scar across the back of her left hand, canvas tool roll). Ewan (hive tool in his back pocket, wax under his thumbnails, smoke on his coat, old stings, folded bee veil). NOBODY ELSE.
SETTING: the Net Sheds at morning, inside the Heron's ribs under the open frames.
MAGIC: none this chapter.
OBJECTS: the Heron (exact description) with her new keel. The open frames.
LOCKED: none.
WITHHOLD: no jar, no Abel offer told to Ewan, no more than kisses and hands at face and neck.
BODY: her hands in his hair; his mouth at her jaw; the grain of the new keel at her back.
INTERIOR: she knows what she wants and where it leads and she is not ready for the second thing.
STAKES: the limes are early; the Heron has to be ready sooner than she planned.

ENDS ON: bars of light on his shoulder and her not letting go.
