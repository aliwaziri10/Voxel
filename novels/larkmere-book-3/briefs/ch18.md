# Brief 18 | Ewan | blossom | touch level 5

PLOT: A storm comes down the lake after dark. He goes to the Net Sheds to check the roof and finds the loft leaking. A branch brings down the stairs; they are shut in the loft together. One stove, one blanket. They sit shoulder to shoulder, breathing in step, listening to the rain on the lake. He does not leave. She does not ask him to. They talk about nothing. At last she says her father built this loft for Ottoline's winters. He says he knows.
