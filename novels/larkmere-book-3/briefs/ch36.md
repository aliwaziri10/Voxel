# Brief 36 | Ewan | lime-bloom | touch level 10f

PLOT: On the Far Shore at dawn he and Tobiah cut the first lime comb and jar it, and he cannot stop what he feels going into every jar. Abel arrives at the Marrick house at evening. Ottoline serves the lime honey at supper. Every person at the table tastes his fear and love together. Abel puts down his spoon. Bryony says, "You're not asking." Ewan: "I'm not asking you to stay." The words cost him everything and fix nothing.
PRESENT: Ewan (hive tool in his back pocket, wax under his thumbnails, smoke on his coat, old stings, folded bee veil). Bryony (flat pencil behind her ear, sawdust in her hair, scar across the back of her left hand, canvas tool roll). Tobiah (bee veil too big for him, ears pink when angry, whistles through his teeth). Ottoline (ring of keys at her belt, bent back, upright voice). Abel (one white eyebrow, hands like spades, talks in boat measures). NOBODY ELSE.
SETTING: the Far Shore at dawn in the lime wood, then the Marrick kitchen table at night.
MAGIC: honey rules 1 to 5: the whole table tastes the day's cut; Ewan reads it in their faces.
OBJECTS: the lime jars (plain round glass jars with cloth tops tied with string and a paper strip). Spoons.
LOCKED: "I'm not asking you to stay." Ewan, here only.
WITHHOLD: no touch between them; no decision from Bryony.
BODY: he does not touch her across the table; her hand flat beside her bowl; his own hand shaking on his knee.
INTERIOR: he will not be the reason she stays small.
STAKES: everyone at the table now knows what he is afraid of.
CALLBACK: ch20: the spring cut; ch24: Abel's tasting.
ENDS ON: the silence after his line and the honey cooling in the spoons.
