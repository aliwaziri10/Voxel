# Brief 4 | Ewan | thaw | touch level 2

PLOT: He brings the first blocks of Marrick wax down to the Net Sheds with Tobiah carrying the second, because Ottoline has made it an order. Bryony tests the wax with her thumbnail and says it is clean. Tobiah is cold to her: he calls her "Bryony" flat and puts the block down as if it were hot. The old keel has to be eased out and the first rotten plank pried loose; she and Ewan take the long pry bar together, her back against his chest through two coats. He has never stopped knowing the exact height of her. Ewan names the deadline plainly: the Far Shore limes will flow and the Heron must carry the crates. She says she will be done when the Heron is done. He realizes he has agreed to see her every day.
PRESENT: Ewan (hive tool in his back pocket, wax under his thumbnails, smoke on his coat, old stings, folded bee veil). Bryony (flat pencil behind her ear, sawdust in her hair, scar across the back of her left hand, canvas tool roll). Tobiah (bee veil too big for him, ears pink when angry, whistles through his teeth). NOBODY ELSE.
SETTING: the Net Sheds, the Heron on her cradle, planks and shavings, cold light through the door.
MAGIC: none this chapter beyond the wax being plain: the wax is only wax (rule 6).
OBJECTS: the wax blocks. The pry bar. The Heron (exact description). The Net Sheds lantern.
LOCKED: none.
WITHHOLD: no Leaving Jar, no lantern truth, no talk of why she left.
BODY: her back against his chest through two coats at the pry bar; his breath held; the scent of oak on her hair.
INTERIOR: he decides to be polite and fails at once.
STAKES: the limes will not wait for the Heron; the flow is weather, not a schedule.
CALLBACK: ch1: the lane; ch3: she has already tasted a jar of his (he does not know).
ENDS ON: her saying "I'll be done when she's done" and his hearing himself say "Good."
