# Brief 12 | Ewan | blossom | touch level 4

PLOT: Night. He cannot sleep, sees the Net Sheds lantern burning, and walks down to find her in the loft window not working, only sitting, her father's keel note in her hand. He climbs the loft stairs. She shakes once, hard, like a plank letting go. He holds her; she lets him; neither speaks of the past. Her face goes into his throat. He feels her pulse under his jaw. He thinks of everything he did not ask seven springs ago and does not ask it now either. He leaves when her breath evens.
PRESENT: Ewan (hive tool in his back pocket, wax under his thumbnails, smoke on his coat, old stings, folded bee veil). Bryony (flat pencil behind her ear, sawdust in her hair, scar across the back of her left hand, canvas tool roll). NOBODY ELSE.
SETTING: the Net Sheds loft at night, the stove, the window on the lake, the lantern's green light on the floor.
MAGIC: none this chapter.
OBJECTS: the keel note. The Net Sheds lantern (exact description).
LOCKED: none.
WITHHOLD: no kiss, no leaving talk, no reveal about who lights the lantern.
BODY: his arms round her, her face at his throat, her pulse under his jaw, his hand flat at her back.
INTERIOR: he knows now that they both chose their work and neither asked the other to make room; he cannot say it.
STAKES: one wrong word and she is back on the thaw road by morning.
CALLBACK: ch8: the lantern; ch11: her face over the keel.
ENDS ON: the loft stairs creaking under him and her stove still glowing.
