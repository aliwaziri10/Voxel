# Brief 17 | Bryony | blossom | touch level 3

PLOT: The town comes down to the Net Sheds with offcuts, nails, a kettle and a quiet kind of thanks. Neighbors and quay folk who muttered at ice-out now bring what they can. One old neighbor says her father would be proud. She nearly breaks. A letter from Abel Trent arrives with the post: she reads it standing and folds it small; it says he is coming. Ewan puts his coat round her shoulders without a word and his hand rests at her nape for the length of a breath.
PRESENT: Bryony (flat pencil behind her ear, sawdust in her hair, scar across the back of her left hand, canvas tool roll). Ewan (hive tool in his back pocket, wax under his thumbnails, smoke on his coat, old stings, folded bee veil). The quay folk and neighbors (unnamed, one short line at most). NOBODY ELSE.
SETTING: the Net Sheds in blossom light, the quay, the town at the door.
MAGIC: none this chapter.
OBJECTS: Abel's letter. Ewan's coat. The kettle.
LOCKED: none.
WITHHOLD: do not state Abel's offer; the letter says only that he is coming to see the Heron.
BODY: his coat round her shoulders, his hand at her nape, her skin warm where his thumb rests.
INTERIOR: she was missed and did not know.
STAKES: Abel's visit puts a second road in front of her.
CALLBACK: ch5: the roof; ch8: the muttering.
ENDS ON: the letter in her pocket and his hand still at her nape.
