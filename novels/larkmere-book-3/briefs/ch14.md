# Brief 14 | Ewan | blossom | touch level 3

PLOT: In the hive yard he loses his temper at Tobiah for being cold to Bryony; Tobiah shouts that Ewan is lying about everything, including the lantern. Ewan knows about the boot-box under Tobiah's bed, the unopened letters, and says nothing. That afternoon he brings wax to the Sheds; she tests a fresh block with her thumb and he goes still when her thumbnail touches his. They stand with a block of wax between their hands for a moment too long. Tobiah is already in the Fairlie yard when Ewan leaves, a plane in his hand, asking Bryony to show him how.
PRESENT: Ewan (hive tool in his back pocket, wax under his thumbnails, smoke on his coat, old stings, folded bee veil). Tobiah (bee veil too big for him, ears pink when angry, whistles through his teeth). Bryony (flat pencil behind her ear, sawdust in her hair, scar across the back of her left hand, canvas tool roll). NOBODY ELSE.
SETTING: the hive yard, the Net Sheds, the Fairlie yard door.
MAGIC: none this chapter beyond the wax being plain.
OBJECTS: the wax block. Tobiah's boot-box (exact: a brown boot-box tied with twine, holding every letter Bryony wrote him, unopened), mentioned by Ewan's thought only.
LOCKED: none.
WITHHOLD: do not open the box; do not say why Tobiah did not read them.
BODY: her thumbnail against his thumbnail on the wax; he goes still; heat up his arm.
INTERIOR: he sees that Tobiah is drawn to her despite himself.
STAKES: if Tobiah and she break, Ewan loses the one he raised too.
CALLBACK: ch2: the Leaving Jar; ch8: the lantern lie.
ENDS ON: Tobiah's plane taking a first clean shaving off a Fairlie board.
