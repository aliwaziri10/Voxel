# Brief 6 | Ewan | thaw | touch level 3

PLOT: First fine day. He opens the hives for the first inspection of the year and Ottoline sends Bryony up the Rise with a message and a crate of old frames. A cranky bee stings her wrist as she sets the crate down. He takes her wrist, scrapes the sting out with his thumbnail and holds her hand until the swelling settles. He knows the scar on the back of her left hand and the way her pulse sits under the skin. A bead of blood, then a bead of honey from a cracked frame, which he touches to the sting without thinking, an old habit he realizes too late. Tobiah's whistle through his teeth from the doorway breaks it.
PRESENT: Ewan (hive tool in his back pocket, wax under his thumbnails, smoke on his coat, old stings, folded bee veil). Bryony (flat pencil behind her ear, sawdust in her hair, scar across the back of her left hand, canvas tool roll). Tobiah (bee veil too big for him, ears pink when angry, whistles through his teeth). NOBODY ELSE.
SETTING: the hive yard on Marrick Rise in thin sun, the Honey House door.
MAGIC: honey rule 5: a bead of honey on the skin holds the day it was cut; she tastes it on the sting and does not say what it tasted of.
OBJECTS: a cracked frame of old honey. The hive tool.
LOCKED: none.
WITHHOLD: no Leaving Jar, no Abel.
BODY: the sting, his thumbnail on her wrist, her pulse under his thumb.
INTERIOR: his hands remember her before he does.
STAKES: she could leave in a breath and he knows it.
CALLBACK: ch1: the bee on her sleeve; ch4: her back against his chest.
ENDS ON: Tobiah's whistle and her wrist sliding out of his hand.
