# Brief 26 | Ewan | swarm | touch level 2

PLOT: Tobiah drags the brown boot-box out from under his bed and sits with it on the Honey House step. Ewan finds him. Tobiah: "I never opened them." Ewan: "Why?" Tobiah: "If I opened them she'd be gone for real." Ewan has no answer but to sit beside him. Through the Fairlie yard door below he can see her planing, her forearms bare, and the wanting sits under his grief. Tobiah tells him to open his jar. Ewan says it is not that simple. Tobiah: "You coward."
PRESENT: Ewan (hive tool in his back pocket, wax under his thumbnails, smoke on his coat, old stings, folded bee veil). Tobiah (bee veil too big for him, ears pink when angry, whistles through his teeth). Bryony (flat pencil behind her ear, sawdust in her hair, scar across the back of her left hand, canvas tool roll), seen at a distance only. NOBODY ELSE.
SETTING: the Honey House step on Marrick Rise, the view down to the Fairlie yard.
MAGIC: honey rule 3: Ewan cannot taste what is in the Leaving Jar and is afraid of what the others would.
OBJECTS: Tobiah's boot-box (exact: a brown boot-box tied with twine, holding every letter Bryony wrote him, unopened). The Leaving Jar (exact description).
LOCKED: none.
WITHHOLD: the box stays tied; the jar stays closed.
BODY: her forearms through the yard door; his hand flat on the step.
INTERIOR: he is as unopened as the box.
STAKES: Tobiah is about to read the letters and Ewan does not know what is in them either.
CALLBACK: ch14: the boot-box under the bed; ch7: "It's not for eating."
ENDS ON: Tobiah's thumb on the twine and the knot not yet pulled.
