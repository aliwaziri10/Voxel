# Brief 20 | Ewan | swarm | touch level 2

PLOT: The spring cut. In the Honey House he cuts the first honey of the year and jars it, knowing every jar will hold whatever he feels. He tries to feel nothing and cannot. Ottoline, passing with her keys, tells him to think of anything but her. He fails. Tobiah comes in to help and says the jars smell like Bryony. Ewan labels each jar and sets the batch on the shelf below the Leaving Jar.
PRESENT: Ewan (hive tool in his back pocket, wax under his thumbnails, smoke on his coat, old stings, folded bee veil). Ottoline (ring of keys at her belt, bent back, upright voice). Tobiah (bee veil too big for him, ears pink when angry, whistles through his teeth). NOBODY ELSE.
SETTING: the Honey House in swarm season, frames, cutting knives, jars, the top shelf.
MAGIC: honey rules 1 to 4: this day's cut will taste of whatever he feels; he cannot taste it himself.
OBJECTS: the lime jars (plain round jars with cloth tops tied with string and a paper strip). The Leaving Jar (exact description).
LOCKED: none.
WITHHOLD: do not say what the new jars taste of; Bryony is not present.
BODY: honey on his thumb; he thinks of her mouth and tastes only sweetness.
INTERIOR: he fears what the town will taste.
STAKES: every neighbor who buys a jar learns his heart.
CALLBACK: ch3: the first spoon; ch7: the four jars.
ENDS ON: the new jars lined up under the old one.
