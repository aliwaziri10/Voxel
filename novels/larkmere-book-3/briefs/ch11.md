# Brief 11 | Bryony | blossom | touch level 2

PLOT: The old keel at last comes out of the Heron in one long groaning piece. Inside the scarf joint she finds her father's pencil writing: "Mind the heel. Marrick wax in every seam." She sits down on the shed floor. Ottoline finds her there and tells her the Leaving Jar is the comb Ewan took on the morning she left, kept seven springs unopened on the top shelf. Bryony: "Don't tell me." Ottoline: "I'm telling you." Ewan comes to the door with a message about the wax, sees her face and stops. She does not lean toward him. She wants to.
PRESENT: Bryony (flat pencil behind her ear, sawdust in her hair, scar across the back of her left hand, canvas tool roll). Ottoline (ring of keys at her belt, bent back, upright voice). Ewan (hive tool in his back pocket, wax under his thumbnails, smoke on his coat, old stings, folded bee veil). NOBODY ELSE.
SETTING: the Net Sheds, the big shed, the old keel lying in the shavings.
MAGIC: honey rules 1 and 3: the Leaving Jar holds his truest feeling on the day he cut the comb; Ottoline does not say what it holds because nobody knows.
OBJECTS: the old oak keel. The keel note (exact: "Mind the heel. Marrick wax in every seam."). The Leaving Jar (described by Ottoline only).
LOCKED: none.
WITHHOLD: do not say what is inside the jar; she has not tasted it; no Abel offer.
BODY: his eyes on her bent head; her own hands still on the keel; the distance of three steps.
INTERIOR: her father wrote to whoever came next and she is the next.
STAKES: she now knows the jar exists and cannot unknow it.
CALLBACK: ch3: the saying; ch7: "It's not for eating."
ENDS ON: her thumb on her father's pencil and Ewan unmoving in the door.
