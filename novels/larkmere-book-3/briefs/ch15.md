# Brief 15 | Bryony | blossom | touch level 3

PLOT: Tobiah in the Fairlie yard asks to learn boats; she gives him a plane and a board. Halfway through he stops and asks if she thinks he is stupid for not reading her letters. She says no. He tells her the truth he has held: he was ten when she left; Ewan raised him; Ewan is the reason he is not a stranger. She understands that Ewan raised her brother and never once told her. She goes to find Ewan; he is in the hive yard; she takes his hand to thank him and her thumb crosses his knuckles. He says Tobiah raised himself and he only fed him.
PRESENT: Bryony (flat pencil behind her ear, sawdust in her hair, scar across the back of her left hand, canvas tool roll). Tobiah (bee veil too big for him, ears pink when angry, whistles through his teeth). Ewan (hive tool in his back pocket, wax under his thumbnails, smoke on his coat, old stings, folded bee veil). NOBODY ELSE.
SETTING: the Fairlie yard, the hive yard on Marrick Rise.
MAGIC: none this chapter.
OBJECTS: a plane. A board. Her father's adze on its peg.
LOCKED: none.
WITHHOLD: no box opening; no kiss; Abel not mentioned.
BODY: her hand around his, thumb across his knuckles; his stillness.
INTERIOR: gratitude and old guilt in the same breath.
STAKES: if she does not say thank you now she never will.
CALLBACK: ch7: "You didn't write"; ch14: Tobiah with the plane.
ENDS ON: her hand still in his and a hive humming behind them.
