# Brief 35 | Bryony | lime-bloom | touch level 10f

PLOT: She works with her back to him the next morning, planing the last planks in a fury of clean shavings. Ewan brings tea and leaves it and does not touch her. A letter from Abel: he will come for the launch and wants his answer. She reads it standing. The launch has to be at dusk before the lime flow peaks. She does not know what she will say. She watches Ewan walk away down the shore and wants to call him back and does not.
PRESENT: Bryony (flat pencil behind her ear, sawdust in her hair, scar across the back of her left hand, canvas tool roll). Ewan (hive tool in his back pocket, wax under his thumbnails, smoke on his coat, old stings, folded bee veil) briefly. NOBODY ELSE.
SETTING: the Net Sheds at morning, the Heron's last planks, the shore.
MAGIC: none this chapter.
OBJECTS: the last planks. A mug of tea. Abel's letter.
LOCKED: none.
WITHHOLD: she does not tell Ewan about the offer; he does not ask.
BODY: touch withdrawn; she can feel where his hand is not; the heat in her palms.
INTERIOR: she is holding herself still the way a plank holds its bend.
STAKES: the launch is the deadline and she has no answer.
CALLBACK: ch25: the offer; ch27: Abel leaving.
ENDS ON: the tea going cold on the bench and Ewan a small figure on the shore.
