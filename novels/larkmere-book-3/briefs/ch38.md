# Brief 38 | Ewan | lime-bloom | touch level 10f

PLOT: He crosses to the Far Shore at first light to cut the last of the limes because the flow will not wait for any launch. A squall comes down out of the hills. The punt holds, because she caulked it. He is stranded in the lime wood hut with the storm roaring on the lake. By dusk the rain thins and he sits at the water's edge with a fire. Across the water the Net Sheds lantern lights: green, steady. He has never seen it lit by anyone else. He understands that she lit it. Then the harbor lanterns come up, and more, all of them, until the far shore is a necklace of lights.
PRESENT: Ewan (hive tool in his back pocket, wax under his thumbnails, smoke on his coat, old stings, folded bee veil). NOBODY ELSE.
SETTING: the Far Shore, the lime wood hut, the storm, the lake at dusk, the lights across the water.
MAGIC: none this chapter.
OBJECTS: the punt (exact description). The Net Sheds lantern lit across the water. A fire.
LOCKED: none.
WITHHOLD: no Bryony on the page; he does not know she is coming.
BODY: alone; his hands cold; the warmth of the fire on his face; his heart hammering at the green light.
INTERIOR: he decides, alone, to ask her plainly.
STAKES: a real squall could have drowned him; he nearly did not return.
