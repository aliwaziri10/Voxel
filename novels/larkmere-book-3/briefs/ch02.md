# Brief 2 | Ewan | thaw | touch level 0

PLOT: He carries the humming skep up the Rise, hands steady only because the bees need them to be. At the top Tobiah has seen her walk down the road and says flatly, "That was Bryony." Ottoline waits at the Honey House door with her keys, wearing an innocent face. In the kitchen Ewan asks who wrote to her; Ottoline says the Heron's keel is soft and the Heron is Fairlie-built, so who else. He cannot argue. He sets the swarm into an empty hive with his hands busy, and still shakes. Tobiah asks if she is staying. Ewan: "She's here for the Heron." In the Honey House he looks at the top shelf, where the Leaving Jar sits with its blank side out, and does not touch it.
PRESENT: Ewan (hive tool in his back pocket, wax under his thumbnails, smoke on his coat, old stings, folded bee veil). Tobiah (bee veil too big for him, ears pink when angry, whistles through his teeth). Ottoline (ring of keys at her belt, bent back, upright voice). NOBODY ELSE.
SETTING: the path up Marrick Rise, the Marrick kitchen, the hive yard, the Honey House.
MAGIC: honey rules 1 and 3: the jars on the shelves hold the days they were cut; Ewan tastes only plain honey from his own jars. He does not know what the Leaving Jar holds.
OBJECTS: the skep. The Leaving Jar (exact: a squat glass jar of dark honey, sealed with a disc of Marrick wax, a strip of blue linen tied round the neck, on the top shelf turned so the blank side faces out).
LOCKED: none.
WITHHOLD: do not explain why she left or what the jar means; Ewan only looks at it. No Bryony on the page except in his memory of the lane.
BODY: the smell of her coat and wood smoke still on his hands; his fingers shaking at the hive.
INTERIOR: seven springs of not hoping, undone in one swarm.
STAKES: Tobiah's loyalty and Ottoline's meddling both point at a collision he has no plan for.
CALLBACK: ch1: the bee on her sleeve; he counts how many he missed.
ENDS ON: Tobiah in the Honey House doorway watching Ewan not touch the jar.
