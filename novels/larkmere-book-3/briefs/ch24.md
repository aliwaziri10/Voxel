# Brief 24 | Ewan | swarm | touch level 3

PLOT: Abel comes up the Rise to buy honey and tastes one spoonful of the new cut. He says nothing for a moment, then says, "Boy." He tells Ewan, blunt as a plane, that she will not stay small and he knows it; he did not come to take her from anyone. Ewan says he never asked her to stay. Abel: "I know." From the hive yard Ewan watches Bryony laugh with Abel on the Fairlie yard step. He hates it and wants her.
PRESENT: Ewan (hive tool in his back pocket, wax under his thumbnails, smoke on his coat, old stings, folded bee veil). Abel (one white eyebrow, hands like spades, talks in boat measures). Bryony (flat pencil behind her ear, sawdust in her hair, scar across the back of her left hand, canvas tool roll), seen at a distance only. NOBODY ELSE.
SETTING: the Honey House and the hive yard on Marrick Rise, the Fairlie yard step far below.
MAGIC: honey rules 1 and 3: Abel tastes his longing and keeps his face still.
OBJECTS: the spring jar. A spoon.
LOCKED: none.
WITHHOLD: Abel does not state the offer; Ewan does not say he loves her.
BODY: his hands sticky and still; the sight of her laugh through the hive yard hum.
INTERIOR: he is afraid she is the kind of woman who should have the largest water.
STAKES: if he asks, he may cage her; if he does not, he loses her.
CALLBACK: ch2: the Leaving Jar; ch12: the loft.
ENDS ON: Abel's boots going down the Rise and Ewan still holding the spoon.
