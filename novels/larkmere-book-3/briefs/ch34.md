# Brief 34 | Ewan | lime-bloom | touch level 10e

PLOT: In the loft that night, after the jar. She is in his arms, kissing him, and then she pulls back with her mouth still wet from his and her hands still on him. She says she has not forgiven herself. He says, "I would have said go." And then he would have said come back, and he said neither. She says neither did she. They see it at once: neither asked the other to make room. They sit on the cot with their foreheads together and do not undo it. The stove ticks. The lantern burns green through the wall.
