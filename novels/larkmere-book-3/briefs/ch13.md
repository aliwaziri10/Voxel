# Brief 13 | Bryony | blossom | touch level 2

PLOT: Next morning she is brisk and busy, sets out the garboards, says nothing about the loft. Ottoline arrives and admits it: she wrote the letter to Bryony herself, did not tell Ewan because he would have forbidden it, and used the Heron as the honest reason. Bryony is furious: "You used my father's boat." "Yes." Ottoline says seven springs is long enough. Bryony mentions Abel Trent's name again in her own thoughts, and the coast yard she has not left behind. She watches Ewan carry a plank across the yard and looks away.
PRESENT: Bryony (flat pencil behind her ear, sawdust in her hair, scar across the back of her left hand, canvas tool roll). Ottoline (ring of keys at her belt, bent back, upright voice). Ewan (hive tool in his back pocket, wax under his thumbnails, smoke on his coat, old stings, folded bee veil), seen at a distance only. NOBODY ELSE.
SETTING: the Net Sheds in morning light, the yard door, the shore.
MAGIC: none this chapter.
OBJECTS: the garboards. The letter (Bryony still has it in her tool roll).
LOCKED: none.
WITHHOLD: no Abel offer; she thinks of his name only as her master.
BODY: she watches him carry a plank across the yard; the line of his shoulders; her own pencil gripped too hard.
INTERIOR: she is angry because Ottoline was right.
STAKES: if she is angry enough she will not finish the Heron.
CALLBACK: ch11: Ottoline telling about the jar; ch3: the letter that brought her.
ENDS ON: Ottoline's keys jingling away and the letter's paper soft in her hand.
