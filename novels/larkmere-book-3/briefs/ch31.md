# Brief 31 | Bryony | lime-bloom | touch level 10c

PLOT: Dusk in the hive yard, the hum of a thousand bees settling for the night. His hands slide under her coat to her bare back and stay there. She feels the hum through her ribs and his skin against hers. She kisses him and he kisses her back until her knees go. She stops him, not from fear of him but of how much. She says, "Not yet," and he says, "I know." She presses her forehead to his collar and breathes.
PRESENT: Bryony (flat pencil behind her ear, sawdust in her hair, scar across the back of her left hand, canvas tool roll). Ewan (hive tool in his back pocket, wax under his thumbnails, smoke on his coat, old stings, folded bee veil). NOBODY ELSE.
SETTING: the hive yard on Marrick Rise at dusk, the hives humming, the apple tree.
MAGIC: none this chapter beyond rule 6.
OBJECTS: her coat. The hives.
LOCKED: none.
WITHHOLD: no jar; the offer stays unspoken.
BODY: his hands under her coat at her bare back, skin, the hum through her ribs; her forehead on his collar.
INTERIOR: wanting this much frightens a woman who has always known exactly what she wanted.
STAKES: if she gives herself fully and then leaves, it will break them both.
CALLBACK: ch28: the lantern; ch29: the Heron's ribs.
ENDS ON: the hum around them and his hand still at her back.
