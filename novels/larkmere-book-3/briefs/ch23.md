# Brief 23 | Bryony | swarm | touch level 4

PLOT: Abel Trent walks down the thaw road with a pack and a stick, and she runs to meet him. He embraces her, sees the Heron's new planks and grunts approval. He sees Ewan on the quay and reads them both in one glance. Ewan puts a hand at the small of her back without thinking and she leans into it. Abel says nothing about it. The three of them walk to the Net Sheds. Abel runs his spade-hands along the new garboard and says it is a fair curve, and sound in the heel.
PRESENT: Bryony (flat pencil behind her ear, sawdust in her hair, scar across the back of her left hand, canvas tool roll). Abel (one white eyebrow, hands like spades, talks in boat measures). Ewan (hive tool in his back pocket, wax under his thumbnails, smoke on his coat, old stings, folded bee veil). NOBODY ELSE.
SETTING: the thaw road above Larkmere, the quay, the Net Sheds.
MAGIC: none this chapter.
