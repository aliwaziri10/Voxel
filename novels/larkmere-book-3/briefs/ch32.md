# Brief 32 | Ewan | lime-bloom | touch level 10d

PLOT: In the Honey House he cuts a fresh comb and puts honey on his thumb and holds it to her mouth. She tastes it and he watches her face. His shirt is open; her palms are flat on his chest and over the old stings on his forearms. They kiss between tastes. Ottoline's voice calls from the path that the Far Shore limes are in full bloom a week early and the town has to move the Heron's launch up. They stop with honey on their mouths and their shirts crooked.
PRESENT: Ewan (hive tool in his back pocket, wax under his thumbnails, smoke on his coat, old stings, folded bee veil). Bryony (flat pencil behind her ear, sawdust in her hair, scar across the back of her left hand, canvas tool roll). Ottoline (ring of keys at her belt, bent back, upright voice) outside, heard only. NOBODY ELSE.
SETTING: the Honey House, a fresh comb, the open door.
MAGIC: honey rules 1 to 4: the comb he has just cut tastes of this day and she tastes it on his thumb.
OBJECTS: a fresh comb. A knife. A thumb.
LOCKED: none.
WITHHOLD: she does not tell him what the honey said; the jar stays shut.
BODY: honey on his thumb to her mouth; his shirt open; her palms on his chest and the old stings.
INTERIOR: he sees her taste what he feels and he is not afraid.
STAKES: the limes will not wait; weather is moving the launch.
CALLBACK: ch21: her first taste of his heart; ch30: the wall.
ENDS ON: Ottoline's voice on the path and honey still on her lip.
