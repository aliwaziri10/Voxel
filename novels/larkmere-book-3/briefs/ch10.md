# Brief 10 | Ewan | blossom | touch level 3

PLOT: He takes the repaired punt out alone to the lime wood to see the far hives after winter and Bryony insists on coming to check her work. A blossom-season squall comes up out of the hills. The punt ships water. She bails with a tin scoop and he works the bilge pump; their hands meet on the pump handle and stay there through the worst of it. Rain, wind, laughter at the wrong moment. They reach the quay soaked. He comes close to saying what he has not said in seven springs and says only the weather.
PRESENT: Ewan (hive tool in his back pocket, wax under his thumbnails, smoke on his coat, old stings, folded bee veil). Bryony (flat pencil behind her ear, sawdust in her hair, scar across the back of her left hand, canvas tool roll). NOBODY ELSE.
SETTING: the punt on Larkmere Water in a squall, rain on the surface, the Far Shore a smudge, the quay.
MAGIC: none this chapter.
OBJECTS: the punt (exact description). The bilge pump. The tin scoop.
LOCKED: none.
WITHHOLD: no confession, no Leaving Jar.
BODY: wet hands over the pump handle, her palm under his; breath hard; rain in her lashes.
INTERIOR: he nearly says her name the way he used to and swallows it with lake water.
STAKES: the squall is real weather and could swamp the punt; a bad capsize would end the season.
CALLBACK: ch9: the punt, the petal.
ENDS ON: the quay boards under their boots and a sentence he does not say.
