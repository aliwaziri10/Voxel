# Brief 7 | Bryony | thaw | touch level 1

PLOT: Tobiah delivers the oak to the Net Sheds without a word. She tries one sentence: his name. He says "You didn't write." She says "I wrote every month." He says "I didn't read them" and leaves. That evening Ottoline makes her sit at the Marrick kitchen table for supper and sets out four jars of Ewan's honey to taste on bread: a day of plain content, a day of grief, a day of a saved swarm. Bryony tastes each and does not look at Ewan. Her eyes find the top shelf in the Honey House through the open door, a squat jar with its blank side out. She asks what it is. Ewan says, "It's not for eating."
PRESENT: Bryony (flat pencil behind her ear, sawdust in her hair, scar across the back of her left hand, canvas tool roll). Tobiah (bee veil too big for him, ears pink when angry, whistles through his teeth). Ewan (hive tool in his back pocket, wax under his thumbnails, smoke on his coat, old stings). Ottoline (ring of keys at her belt, bent back, upright voice). NOBODY ELSE.
SETTING: the Net Sheds at noon, the Marrick kitchen at night, a view of the Honey House door.
MAGIC: honey rules 1 to 3: each jar tastes of its own day; Ewan tastes plain honey and watches her face to learn what his own jars say.
OBJECTS: four jars. Bread. The Leaving Jar (exact description, seen only across the yard).
LOCKED: none.
WITHHOLD: no explanation of the jar; no reason for Tobiah's coldness beyond "you left".
BODY: his hand round a cup across the table; hers wanting something to hold.
INTERIOR: she realizes the jars are a year of his days and she is reading them.
STAKES: her letters were unanswered; every jar is a locked truth she is being fed.
CALLBACK: ch3: the first spoon; ch6: the bead of honey on the sting.
ENDS ON: "It's not for eating," and the room not breathing.
