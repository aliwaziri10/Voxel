## Plot as written
- Ottoline brings one jar to the Net Sheds at midday and says one word: "Eat." The strip is in Ewan's hand (clover and hawthorn, a dry wind from the south). It was cut with Bryony in the valley.
- Bryony tastes it. Taste note (NEW, log it): longing like a line paid out over a gunwale with a weight fast at the far end and no hand near the knife, and under it a plain gladness like a stove catching in a cold shed at first light. The first spoon after the thaw (ch.3) was all stillness; this one has been moving the whole time.
- She understands the town has tasted the same cut, and that the quiet on the shingle that morning was a different quiet from the old verdict.
- The Net Sheds lantern is lit by a hand she does not ask about.
- She carries the jar up the Rise to the Honey House. Ewan sees the jar and the horn spoon and the pen stops. The jar rocks on the bench, he steadies it, her hand comes down over his.
