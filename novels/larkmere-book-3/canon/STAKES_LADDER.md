# Stakes ladder: Keel and Comb

Written 2026-11-12 (Zia: "increase the stakes so it comes out among the best"). Read with `CANON_LOCK.md`. Where a brief's STAKES line differs from the line below, THIS FILE WINS. Names, places, objects and the honey rules still come only from `CANON_LOCK.md`.

Constraints that stay: no villain, no procedure, no money, no countdown with numbers. Obstacles are feeling, weather, magic or a person who loves them. Stakes rise by making each loss more concrete and more personal, not by adding plot.

## Core question
She built her life on leaving; he built his on staying. Each was right. The book asks whether there is room, and what each will risk to say so out loud.

## Seven devices (each is seeded, escalated, paid off)
1. THE CUT CLOCK (nature, not magic). When the flow is on and the supers are full, the comb must be cut that week or the hive swarms and the honey spoils. Ewan cannot wait until he feels calm. Whatever he feels on the day goes in the jar, and the town eats it. Seed 2 and 4, bite 19 to 21, 32, 36, 38.
2. THE HULL CREED. Bryony does not launch a hull she has not proved. Seed 3 and 5, tested 18, squeezed 29 and 32, cracked 35, broken on purpose 39. Her arc is trusting something she cannot test alone.
3. THE LAKE ESCALATES. Ten: a squall ships water, two people bail. Eighteen: a storm takes a moored boat and shifts frames. Thirty-eight: a squall nearly drowns him. Thirty-nine: she crosses in the dark. Each lake scene is worse than the last and pushes them closer.
4. THE JAR FEARS. From 11 she fears the Leaving Jar holds hatred. From 2 he fears it holds love, and that it will be tasted. Each dreads opening it for the opposite reason. Thirty-three: it holds both, and forgiveness already there.
5. THE SECOND ROAD. Abel's offer is real and good: the coast yard, her name on the transom. Leaving would be right on its own terms. Nobody is wrong. Never make Abel an obstacle.
6. THE WAX SEAM (payoff device). The keel note says "Marrick wax in every seam" (found 11). Every seam she closes, she closes with his wax: the punt (9 and 10), the new plank (16), the Heron. Thirty-eight: her caulking brings him home. Thirty-nine: his wax holds her hull in the dark. Her craft saves him; his craft saves her.
7. THE TOWN'S FORGIVENESS. Cold in 8. Slow thaw through the work. Thirty-nine: the whole town hauls the Heron down the slip by lantern light.

## Per-chapter STAKES (supersedes the briefs)
1. Stillness is the whole risk: one move or one word first and seven springs of planned speeches have nothing under them. He did not know she was sent for.
2. He learns she was sent for and cannot be angry at the one person who did it. Tobiah's flat "That was Bryony" says the house has chosen a side, and it is not hers.
3. The keel must come out, and the limes will not wait for her pride. Ottoline names the season as the clock. Bryony's creed enters: she does not launch what she has not proved. Fairlie keel, Marrick comb: her father's hands are already in this boat.
4. The flow is weather: the first honey of the year gets cut on whatever he feels that day, and he cannot prepare his heart. Seed the cut clock.
5. The yard roof stayed sound for seven springs because someone kept it so: she has been held up by what she walked away from. Her creed meets the fact that she has nobody to prove a hull to.
6. She could be on the thaw road by morning and nothing would hold her. The house knows it and he knows it.
7. Her letters went unanswered, and every jar on the shelf is a truth she cannot stop being fed. One jar is "not for eating".
8. The town's silence is a verdict. He lies about the lantern, and honey never lies: his first lie is the one thing he cannot jar.
9. One honest job is her only way to be useful and be forgiven. The Heron is the only boat big enough for the lime crates and she alone can finish her. The punt is caulked with Marrick wax.
10. The lake's first teeth: the punt ships water and two people bail with their hands on one handle. A capsize ends the season and maybe more. He almost says it and says the weather.
11. The keel note ties Fairlie wood to Marrick wax in her father's hand. She learns the jar exists and cannot unknow it. She fears it holds hatred.
12. One wrong word and she is on the thaw road by morning. The longer they work side by side, the more each word costs.
13. Ottoline wrote the letter. If Bryony stays angry enough she will quit the Heron out of pride, and the keel, the town and the last Fairlie work go with her.
14. Tobiah has kept her letters unopened for seven springs. If she and Tobiah break for good, Ewan loses a second person he raised, and Tobiah loses his sister twice.
15. If she cannot say thank you now to the man she left, she never will, and it is the one debt he has earned.
16. The plank must cool on the frame in one unbroken hold. If either lets go it cracks and a day's oak and a night's trust are gone. Their bodies are the clamp.
17. Abel's letter names a second road with her name on it. She carries it in her tool roll and tells nobody.
18. The lake takes a moored boat. The roof leaks, the frames shift, and the Heron is unproved against weather: she hears it in the timbers. The stairs fall and they are shut in together.
19. A swarm leaves when it chooses. The flow rises, hives fill faster than he can sort his heart, and nothing living waits.
20. The spring cut: whatever he feels goes into every jar forever and every neighbor who buys one tastes it. He cannot feel nothing. Tobiah smells her in the jars. The batch sits under the Leaving Jar.
21. She tastes a jar cut after her arrival and knows the town already knows. She can never again pretend not to know.
22. If either wakes first they will pull away.
23. Abel stands in the yard her father built: the coast yard is in front of her again, with an old man's hands and her own future.
24. If he asks her to stay he becomes the cage she left. If he does not ask, he loses her. His silence is a choice, and it goes into the next jar.
25. The offer is a master's yard with her name on it, and she is dancing on her first life with the second in her pocket. Abel's answer is due at the launch.
26. Tobiah is about to read the letters, and Ewan does not know what is in them. If Tobiah reads her anger he loses her again.
27. Trust is a brown box put into his hands. The swarm interrupts at the exact moment they would not have stopped.
28. The kiss. He has lit the lantern every dusk for seven springs and the locked line says so out loud. After this the wanting only grows.
29. The limes are early. The Heron's keel is in and the ribs stand open: her creed meets the weather, and she lets him into the hull anyway.
30. Someone who loves them has put a door and a supper in the way.
31. If she gives herself fully and then leaves, it breaks them both. She stops, and he knows why.
32. The limes are fully in bloom a week early and the launch moves up to the next dusk. The Heron is not proved: her creed against his flow.
33. Forgiveness already there, before she was out of sight of the Rise. Being forgiven is harder than being hated. She wanted the anger.
34. Neither ever asked. Saying it cuts both ways: it makes leaving fair and staying a choice.
35. The launch is the deadline and she has no answer for Abel, for him, or for herself. She wants to call him back and does not.
36. The lime cut puts his fear in every jar and the whole table tastes it. Abel sets down his spoon. Refusing to ask costs him his pride in public and fixes nothing.
37. Ottoline's keys: she may choose which doors. Tobiah calls them both idiots. The lantern goes unlit for the first dusk in seven springs.
38. The last cut will not wait for any launch. The squall could drown him. The punt holds because she caulked it. He sees the lantern lit by someone else.
39. She launches an unproved hull on a storm-stirred lake for one man, against her creed, on seams closed with his wax. The town hauls her down the slip.
40. No stake left but the question, and it is answered. The lake is open.

## Rules for the drafter
- Name the cost, not the feeling: say what is lost if it goes wrong.
- Raise the cost every chapter. Never resolve an earlier stake early.
- Keep Abel kind and right. Keep the town unforgiving until the work earns it.
- Never spell out the creed in a speech; show it in her hands (she tests a joint twice, will not hurry a bevel).
