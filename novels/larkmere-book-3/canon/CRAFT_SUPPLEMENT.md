# Craft supplement: Keel and Comb

Read after `VOICE_GUIDE.md`. Added 2026-11-12 after the lexicon pass. Fills gaps the voice guide and writer command left open.

## 1. Sub-beat plan before drafting
Plan 2 to 4: the plot beat; a relationship micro-beat (something shifts between them); an interior beat (she or he admits something to self); a world or stakes beat; a texture or callback beat. Lengthen only by adding a real sub-beat, never by stretching sentences. A chapter that supports one beat stays short and is flagged for Zia, not padded.

## 2. Image ledger
After every chapter, log in `canon/IMAGE_LEDGER.md`: signature images and similes, gestures, taste note, ending type, three most-used ordinary words. Before drafting, read it. No image, simile or gesture repeats within six chapters. No honey taste note repeats, ever. Do not end two chapters in a row on the same ending type.

## 3. Speech
- Bryony: short, plain, says it and leaves; breaks off to work; never apologizes first.
- Ewan: dry, answers with a smaller sentence than the question; humor lives in the pause.
- Tobiah: clipped, flat, questions that are accusations; whistles through his teeth when he lies.
- Ottoline: blunt, upright, commands in the tone of a weather remark; the only one who states the honey rule outright.
- Abel: boat measures stand in for feeling ("sound in the heel"), few words.
- Tags: "said" by default. Action instead of adverbs. People talk past each other; a line may go unanswered. Never let a character explain their own subtext.

## 4. Sensory rotation
Every scene carries at least three senses beyond sight. Bryony: touch, tool sounds, timber smell, lake cold. Ewan: hive sound, smoke, wax, taste, warmth on his forearms.

## 5. Season palette (ordinary scenery only; add no named thing)
- Thaw: meltwater, rotten grey ice at the lake edge, mud, bare hedges, catkins, rooks, sleet, thin low light, wet earth, first willow with bees on it.
- Blossom: apple and plum blossom, petals on the water, warm rain, hawthorn, long wet grass, cuckoo, first real warmth.
- Swarm: dry lanes, dandelion clocks, elder flower, clover, swifts, midges at dusk, long evenings, bees restless.
- Lime-bloom: heavy lime scent, a hum in the trees, hot still air, sticky honeydew on leaves, thunder over the lake, gold evenings.

## 6. Honey taste bank
Rule: every taste once in the book. Small, precise, felt in the body, never explained. In Bryony's chapters the taste is described in her own domain (wood, tools, water), never bees. Ewan never tastes his own honey; he reads it on other faces. Seed ideas, to be used once each and logged: a held breath inside the sweetness; cold iron under the sweet; a clamp easing a quarter turn; frost thinning on a window; warmth at the back of the throat like a door left ajar. Invent new ones; never reuse a logged one.

## 7. Warmth and humor
Cozy, not grim. Each chapter has one small warm or funny beat (Ewan's dry line, Tobiah's teasing, Ottoline's meddling, Bryony humming without knowing).

## 8. Words that collide with banned names
The checker rejects banned names. Avoid these words entirely, even lower case: wren, pike, quill, hale, mercer, bram, pell, mara, renn, voss, oakes, corrin, nell, jack. Avoid birds, fish or trades with those names. Also avoid any capitalized word not on the allowed list at the start of a sentence unless it is a common word.

## 9. Tells to watch in the drafter (Claude)
Beyond `HUMAN_WRITING.md`: "the air between them", "hung between them", "for the first time in years", "time seemed to slow", "the world narrowed", "tension crackled", "the weight of seven springs", "a breath she did not know she held", "heart skipped", "unspoken", a closing line that restates the feeling, three similes in a row, an adverb stuck on a tag, the same gesture ending two scenes.

## 10. Heat
Use the chapter's BODY line and `canon/HEAT_LADDER.md`. One remembered body detail per touch scene, never a flashback scene.
