- The ring that was kept clear around Bryony on the night the ice broke (ch.8) is gone. The man by the bollards who said "Has a nerve, coming back" says the only quay-folk line: "You made the table. Sit."

- Interior: for seven springs he has slept like a hive in a lean year with a guard at the door; last night he posted no guard.
- Dawn: a second blanket lies over them. It is the grey blanket with the scorched hem that hung on the stove rail in the loft in ch.18. The text does not say who put it there. Ottoline's keys are heard going away over the gravel. Her hand is still in his.
