# Novel Factory: DECISIONS (2026-10-22)

Zia delegated these decisions to Claude after reading the Kindling Line books. Zia may overrule any of them. Read `novels/FACTORY_HANDOFF.md` for the build plan and verified repo facts.

## Verified from the repo
- Kindling Line (Ivy Cassel), Book 1 STORY_SUMMARY.md and VOICE_GUIDE.md and chapter 5: adult romantasy in a cliff city (Thornmere Reach), dual point of view in close third past tense, 45 chapters, open-door heat with two to three on-page scenes that are not graphic ("Fourth Wing-adjacent"), short hook-driven scenes, magic with a cost. It is NOT written in a Gen Z voice (no first person, no slang). Its fast pace comes from set pieces and hooks.
- Warm Enough (Elif Kessler): cozy slow burn, closed door, 40 chapters, full canon and briefs already written.
- Published per Zia: Amity Falls 1 to 4, Larkmere 1 (Proof of Home), Kindling Line 1 to 3.

## Decisions
1. Two profiles. ELIF KESSLER = cozy slow burn, closed door, sensual not explicit, heat peak in the last fifth. IVY CASSEL = adult romantasy, fast set-piece pacing, open door in the style of Kindling Line (on-page but not graphic, never anatomical words), danger and stakes at high intensity in every chapter.
2. Intensity: emotional intensity and stakes stay high in every chapter for both. Physical heat climbs a ladder and peaks late. Constant maximum heat is rejected because it flattens the ending (found in Warm Enough chapters 34 to 40).
3. Targets: 20 books per pen name, not more yet. Ivy Cassel needs 17 new. Elif Kessler needs 14 new after Warm Enough (including Larkmere books 3 to 5). About 32 new books in total. The roadmap is built so it can extend to 25 each if the first books sell.
4. Order: finish Warm Enough first (it tests the whole pipeline), then Ivy Cassel, then Elif Kessler. Reason: romantasy is the larger market and rewards series read-through (Claude's judgment, not verified for 2026).
5. Series shape: Ivy Cassel = 5 new series of 3 to 4 books. Elif Kessler = Larkmere books 3 to 5 plus 3 new series of 3 to 4 books.
6. Variety rule: every book gets a registry entry (setting, magic rule, trade or craft, lead pairing and trope, antagonist type, season, ending device). The factory rejects any book that repeats another book's setting, magic rule or core trope combination, and audits every name and title against all Voxel books.
7. Chapter length: 2,300 to 2,700 words, hard ceiling 3,000. Both profiles. No padding.
8. Gate before scale: after the first book of each pen name is written and read by Zia (one sample chapter and the audit report), the rest are queued.
9. Free tier only. Publishing stays manual (KDP has no API). Covers and blurbs are still needed per book.

## Critical review: what could be wrong
- The market choice is judgment, not data. The gate in decision 8 limits the loss if it is wrong.
- No chapter has ever been generated by the Warm Enough writer. Prose quality on free models is unproven until the first real run.
- Repo visibility is unknown. Scheduled runs on a private repo use the 2,000 free minutes a month.
- Many books under one name can repeat plots. The registry check in decision 6 is the control.
- Amazon requires AI disclosure and watches high-volume releases. The existing ai_disclosure gate stays.
