## What this project is

Direct sequel to "Where the Frost Doesn't Reach" (Book 1, `novels/where-the-frost-doesnt-reach/`, published). Picks up from Book 1's final-page hook: at the wedding, Wren gives Mara a century-old letter from a woman named **Adelaide**, who made the *original* bargain — trading a town's safety for a person's memory — believing it would end with her own life. It didn't. **As of ch.38, the climax is fully resolved**: Mara, Caleb, Wren, Priya, Yusuf, and Dev successfully performed a "split the weight" redirection ritual with Denise (the present-day renewer, Adelaide's sister) at the old well on Voss land, freeing Denise from the bargain and allowing her to finally age naturally. Caleb's two stolen years have been returned to him. The book is now in its aftermath/resolution phase (ch.39-41 remaining).

## Established canon this book must not contradict

- Kell "renewed" a version of the bargain himself and was exposed/collapsed publicly at the harvest festival (Book 1, ch.43) — Book 2's antagonist (Denise) is a DIFFERENT, separate person, not the same person, not a resurrection of Kell.
- Wren is 20, formally enrolled in the valley's Finder apprenticeship. Her dangerous full reads (ch.12, ch.26) cost her real physical strain; ch.36 nearly cost her far more when the redirection ritual almost concentrated entirely onto her before the group forced an even redistribution.
- Mara and Caleb are newly married. Caleb's missing two years were Denise's 8-years-ago renewal (confirmed ch.29), and were **returned to him in ch.37**, arriving in fragments during the redirection ritual: a stranger's kindness with an umbrella outside the grocer's, a late kitchen-table night with Mara, a phone call from his mother, a quiet morning alone in the old chapel. A dedicated full-memory-processing chapter is still planned for ch.40.
- Thomas (Mara's father figure/informant in Book 1) died in November, before the wedding — not available as a source in Book 2.
- Priya's brother Dev was convicted of a coerced violation and is serving a supervised community-service term. Ch.24 onward: Dev formally joins the investigation. Ch.36-38: Dev and Priya are shown holding hands, something unspoken now sitting openly between them, worth carrying forward into the aftermath chapters if a Dev/Priya beat is wanted, though nothing further has been decided yet.
- Yusuf is now permanent council chair. Ch.25: authorized the formal inquiry via emergency chair authority. Present for the full climax (ch.35-38), took part in the redirection himself.
- The bargain's full mechanics (established across ch.3, ch.30-31): a continuous pull that collects early from its carrier if a renewal is delayed; taken memories are transferred into the carrier rather than destroyed, which is why "splitting the weight" (redistribution across willing people) was possible at all rather than needing to "undo" an erasure.
- **Ch.35-38 climax, now canon:** the redirection ritual required physical contact with the well, a spoken release from Denise, and — critically, discovered only during the ritual itself — active, willed effort from each participant to claim their own even share rather than passively allowing the well to distribute it, since the default failure mode is the load collapsing onto whoever is weakest (nearly Wren). Once stabilized, the ritual completed successfully. Denise is alive, lucid, at peace, and now aging naturally and rapidly toward her true age (confirmed via Odette in ch.21 as born 1938) for the first time in fifty years — gently, not violently like Kell. Each of the six now carries a small permanent piece of the grief/memory Denise used to carry alone; the long-term meaning of that is an open question raised at the end of ch.38, not yet answered.
- Mara's grandmother's maiden line is Voss (Book 1). Denise's real birth year is 1938 (Odette, ch.21).
- Adelaide's letter named **Ambrose Whitlock** as the man who taught her the bargain (ch.20), and Odette's family stories (ch.21) suggest Whitlock predates the town itself and taught one person per generation — this thread is DELIBERATELY UNRESOLVED, reserved for the Book 3 hook in ch.41. Do not resolve Whitlock's identity or origin within this book.

## Standing rules for this book (same as Book 1 unless noted)

- Target 2,000–2,500 words per chapter, 2,100 acceptable floor (don't pad to hit the number if it dilutes pacing). ~41 chapters total. Priority is pace and page-turn tension over hitting a word count exactly.
- **No em dashes anywhere in chapter prose.** CORRECTION 2026-09-14: this was violated in the first draft of ch.36 (2 instances, both inside tense dialogue/action beats where the rhythm of the sentence made an em dash feel natural to draft first) and caught and fixed in the same session before Zia saw it. Treat the em-dash check as its own explicit pass, separate from the "particular" word-search pass, since it's easy to draft one into a high-tension action scene without noticing.
- Match established voice exactly (Mara: dry, controlled, engineering-metaphor; Caleb: steady, plainspoken, dry humor sideways; Wren: blunt, a little raw, faster-talking than the adults around her).
- **Avoid the word "particular" entirely if possible.** The single most recurrent AI-tell word in this book. Treat the find-and-replace pass as mandatory on every chapter without exception before pushing. Same applies to "the specific ___" and "the kind of ___" constructions.
- No other AI-authorship tells (uniform 40-85 word clause-stacked chapter openings, rule-of-three lists, stock phrases).
- New chapters must not contradict Book 1 or any earlier Book 2 chapter.
- All filenames lowercase (`chapter_NN.md`).
- Zia is a non-technical, voice-dictation user (expect transcription garbles — "Waza boys"=Wazzaboyzz, "Vaza"/"Voxel"/"Watson"=Voxel). Give exact file paths and full GitHub URLs in copy blocks for anything Zia needs to see/paste himself.
- Push each finished chapter directly via the API, verify by reading it back, before moving to the next.
- CORRECTION 2026-09-14: repo owner is `aliwaziri10` (the connected account's own repo), NOT `Wazzaboyzz`.
- Per Zia 2026-09-14: once a session is actively drafting, keep writing multiple chapters back to back without pausing to check in after each single chapter. Each chapter still gets individually pushed the moment it's finished ("one chapter, one push"), but batches of updating beat_map.md/HANDOFF.md can happen every few chapters.
- **MULTIPLE SESSIONS MAY BE WRITING CONCURRENTLY, AND MAY BE PROVIDED AS PASTED CONTENT RATHER THAN WRITTEN LIVE.** Before starting or pushing ANY chapter, always re-fetch the actual `chapters/` directory listing and re-read this HANDOFF.md fresh — do not trust a chapter count from earlier in your own conversation or from this file's own tracker below, since either can be stale relative to what's actually live on GitHub. When Zia pastes a chapter, verify it against the live prior chapter's actual content before pushing it, don't assume the tracker below is accurate.
