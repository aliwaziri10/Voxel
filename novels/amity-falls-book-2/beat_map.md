# BEAT MAP — What the Valley Still Owes (Amity Falls Book 2)

Approximately 41 chapters. Authoritative chapter-by-chapter plan. Check this before writing or editing any chapter, AND re-check the live `chapters/` directory first, since this file can itself go stale between sessions (confirmed multiple times now).

**Revision history (abridged, see git history for full detail):** 30 -> 28 -> 45 -> 43 -> 42 -> 41 chapters across earlier sessions as beats got absorbed into single scenes or split back out to hit the 100k+ word target. Chapters 1-25 are final and unchanged at this point.

**Who did it, decided already, unchanged:** the present-day renewer was **Denise** — Adelaide's younger sister, renewing the bargain in six-to-eight-year cycles since the 1970s. Confirmed as of ch.29: she used her 8-years-ago renewal specifically on Caleb, using his existing grief at the time as cover.

**CLIMAX RESOLVED as of ch.35-38 (2026-09-14):** the "split the weight" redirection was attempted at the well with all six (Mara, Caleb, Wren, Priya, Yusuf, Dev) plus Denise. Ch.36: it nearly failed, the load concentrating dangerously onto Wren (the weakest link) instead of dividing evenly, until Mara realized the group had to actively fight for their share rather than passively consent, forcing an even distribution. Ch.37: once stabilized, Caleb's two years returned to him specifically, in fragments, as part of the redistribution. Ch.38: the ritual completed successfully. Denise is freed from the bargain entirely and has begun aging naturally and rapidly toward her true age (mid-to-late 80s) for the first time in fifty years, gently rather than violently the way Kell's collapse was — she's confirmed alive, lucid, and at peace at the end of ch.38, not killed by the redirection. Each of the six now carries a small permanent piece of foreign memory/grief that was part of what Denise carried; what that means long-term is an open question raised but not yet answered in-story (Dev asks directly at the end of ch.38).

**Remaining scope:** ch.39-41 are all post-climax: aftermath processing, Caleb's full-memory chapter, and the Book 3 hook. No more surveillance/investigation/confrontation content remains — the story is now in resolution.
