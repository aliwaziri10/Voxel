# BEAT MAP — What the Valley Still Owes (Amity Falls Book 2)

Approximately 41 chapters now (down from 42 — see the ch.29 note below). Authoritative chapter-by-chapter plan. Check this before writing or editing any chapter, AND re-check the live `chapters/` directory first, since this file can itself go stale between sessions (confirmed multiple times now).

**Revision history (abridged, see git history for full detail):** 30 -> 28 -> 45 -> 43 -> 42 chapters across earlier sessions as beats got absorbed into single scenes or split back out to hit the 100k+ word target. Chapters 1-25 are final and unchanged at this point.

**Note on ch.29 (2026-09-14):** as actually drafted, ch.29 (the confrontation) absorbed a large chunk of what ch.30 was meant to cover separately (Denise's fuller confession, the scope and loneliness of fifty years) AND resolved the "Caleb's missing years" question directly and definitively: Denise confirms outright, unprompted, that she personally took Caleb's two years during her 8-years-ago renewal, using his existing grief as cover — this was NOT Kell's doing, and it is NOT ambiguous going forward. This changes ch.34's original beat (which assumed the source of Caleb's missing years was still an open complication to be discovered) — that beat has been replaced below with a needed emotional-processing chapter for Caleb instead, since the original complication is now moot. **Everything from ch.30 onward is renumbered one chapter earlier than the previous table said.**

**Who did it, decided already, unchanged:** the present-day renewer is **Denise** — Adelaide's younger sister, renewing the bargain in six-to-eight-year cycles since the 1970s, one small specific memory per cycle rather than a whole life torn apart the way Kell did it. Confirmed as of ch.29: she used her 8-years-ago renewal specifically on Caleb, using his existing grief at the time as cover.

**Mechanics confirmed as of ch.30-31 (2026-09-14):** the bargain is a continuous pull, not a static debt — it collects early, from Denise herself, aging her the longer a renewal is delayed, which is why the six-to-eight-year interval isn't a choice but a deadline. As of ch.30, Denise is critically overdue (down to roughly two days of reserve as of ch.32) specifically because she's been holding off out of fear of the group's scrutiny, meaning an unmanaged collapse is imminent and would be worse than Kell's, either ending Denise outright or dumping fifty years of postponed collection onto whoever's nearest. Ch.31's key reveal: memories Denise takes aren't destroyed, they're transferred into her and she still carries all of them, including Caleb's two years — reframing "breaking the bargain" as redistributing what Denise already carries rather than undoing something erased. This is the mechanical basis for the "split the weight" plan. Ch.32: first rule of the plan is informed, freely-given consent from anyone who carries a piece, no one talked into it; mechanics are still mostly guesswork (well, dusk, a physical token, the loss spoken aloud, per Denise's own established ritual practice) with real risk the well could still concentrate the load onto one person rather than dividing it evenly, which Mara flags as needing an active design solution, not yet solved by end of ch.32. Ch.33: Caleb processes the confirmation that Denise personally carries his two years and chooses to take part in the redirection anyway, not from forgiveness but from wanting to see it through with Mara at his side.
