# BEAT MAP — What the Valley Still Owes (Amity Falls Book 2)

28 chapters (revised from 30 — chapters 1-2 as actually drafted covered what was originally planned as four separate beats; see note below). Authoritative chapter-by-chapter plan, written before any prose, so the mystery doesn't drift chapter to chapter. Check this before writing or editing any chapter. Update the "Written" column as chapters are completed.

**Revision note (after ch.1-2 drafted):** the original beats 3 (Mara pulls county records) and 4 (tells Caleb everything) were absorbed into chapters 1 and 2 as actually written — ch.1 already has Mara at the records office with the Voss connection in hand, and ch.2 already ends with Mara telling Caleb the full truth and all three committing to investigate together. Original beats 5-30 have been renumbered down to chapters 3-28 below, unchanged in content otherwise.

**Who did it, decided now so nothing contradicts later:** the present-day renewer is **Denise** — the woman who pressed pie into Mara's hands at the wedding, a fixture of the valley's social life for decades, trusted precisely because she's ordinary and everywhere. She was Adelaide's younger sister, and has been renewing the bargain in six-to-eight-year cycles since the 1970s, each renewal costing her almost nothing visibly because she trades pieces of *other people's* memories of small, specific losses (grief, mostly) rather than her own life the way Kell did, which is why she hasn't aged or collapsed the way he did and why no one has ever suspected her. The 1980s drought file name Wren found is Denise's own name, under a maiden surname (Voss — same family line as Mara's grandmother, making Denise a distant relative Mara never knew about).
