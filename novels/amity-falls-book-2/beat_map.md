Approximately 42 chapters now (down from 45/43 — see revision history). Authoritative chapter-by-chapter plan. Check this before writing or editing any chapter, AND re-check the live `chapters/` directory first, since this file can itself go stale between sessions (confirmed twice now, 2026-09-14).

**Revision history (abridged, see git history for full detail):** 30 -> 28 -> 45 -> 43 chapters across earlier sessions as beats got absorbed into single scenes or split back out to hit the 100k+ word target. Chapters 1-23 are final and unchanged at this point.

**Note on ch.24/25 (2026-09-14):** as actually drafted, ch.24 absorbed BOTH the "Priya's memory taken as retaliation" beat AND most of the "Dev's aftermath/joining the investigation" beat in one scene (Dev notices the loss, confronts Priya, formally asks to join, is accepted — all in one chapter). Ch.25 (arrived as content from Zia, verified against live ch.24 before pushing) then covered what was going to be a later beat: Yusuf, moved by Priya's loss on top of everything else, authorizes a full formal inquiry using emergency chair authority. This means three original beats compressed into two chapters. **Everything from here is renumbered again, one chapter earlier than the previous table said.**

**Who did it, decided already, unchanged:** the present-day renewer is **Denise** — Adelaide's younger sister, renewing the bargain in six-to-eight-year cycles since the 1970s, one small specific memory per cycle rather than a whole life torn apart the way Kell did it.
