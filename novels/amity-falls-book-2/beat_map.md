# BEAT MAP — What the Valley Still Owes (Amity Falls Book 2)

**BOOK COMPLETE as of 2026-09-14. All 41 chapters written, pushed, and verified live on GitHub.**

Authoritative chapter-by-chapter plan, kept for reference and for any future revision pass.

**Revision history (abridged, see git history for full detail):** 30 -> 28 -> 45 -> 43 -> 42 -> 41 chapters across earlier sessions as beats got absorbed into single scenes or split back out to hit the 100k+ word target.

**Full story summary:** Adelaide's original bargain (Book 1's final hook) was being renewed every six to eight years by her younger sister Denise, who traded pieces of the valley's grief for its safety while quietly aging almost none at all. Mara, Caleb, and Wren uncovered the pattern, brought in Priya, Dev, and Yusuf, and confirmed Denise as the renewer. Denise confessed fully (ch.29) once confronted, including that she'd personally taken Caleb's own two missing years eight years prior. Rather than simply exposing or punishing her, the group (with Denise's full cooperation) attempted an untested "split the weight" redirection ritual at the old well (ch.35-38), successfully redistributing fifty years of carried grief across six willing people and freeing Denise to finally age naturally. Caleb's two years were returned to him. The valley processed the truth publicly (ch.39), Caleb fully reckoned with his restored memory including his father's death (ch.40), and the book closes on ordinary healing alongside a deliberately unresolved thread: Adelaide's letter named Ambrose Whitlock as the one who taught her the bargain, meaning Denise's fifty-year cycle was only one branch of something older and still unaccounted for (ch.41, Book 3 hook).
