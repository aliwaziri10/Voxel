**Note on ch.29 (2026-09-14):** as actually drafted, ch.29 (the confrontation) absorbed a large chunk of what ch.30 was meant to cover separately (Denise's fuller confession, the scope and loneliness of fifty years) AND resolved the "Caleb's missing years" question directly and definitively: Denise confirms outright, unprompted, that she personally took Caleb's two years during her 8-years-ago renewal, using his existing grief as cover — this was NOT Kell's doing, and it is NOT ambiguous going forward. Confirmed as of ch.29: she used her 8-years-ago renewal specifically on Caleb, using his existing grief at the time as cover.
